# Iron Heart Collection 

## Introduction

### [[Come to the Heart]] 

This collection of Alchymeic Energies is an invitation to real heart feeling, allowing our feeling Heart to be nourished by Love. 

It is necessary for us to awaken the 'feeling centre' in order to allow ourselves to be love. When this feeling centre is open, it is felt to be a vulnerable position and we may quickly close it again to protect ourselves. It is difficult to remain loving in a world that feels threatening on many occasions. It follows that we cannot remain in a self-contracted, self-protective position and love at the same time. 

When our heart is open, we may come to know such things as compassion, empathy, intimacy and love. Such attributes lie beyond the surface of the skin, beyond ourselves as a 'someone', these attributes are part of the moral universe. We can merely “think' we are compassionate and loving, coming to this assumption by putting these pieces together in our mind, yet our feeling can fail to be genuine. Real feeling is signalled by an obvious connection to the moral universe that we know as love, which is Only God. 

The Heart is a 'volume' – the 'volumetric' feeling centre that touches all matters infinitely. It has no boundaries. 

Love is the food of the Heart. We all need such nourishment, we cannot live without this.
