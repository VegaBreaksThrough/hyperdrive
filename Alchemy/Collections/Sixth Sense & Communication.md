**Our Senses and Psychic Awareness**

Most of us are aware of our five basic senses: seeing, feeling, smelling, hearing, and tasting. What many are less aware of is our “sixth sense,” the preternatural sense of other-worldliness—a connection to something greater than our physical senses can perceive. This is a sense beyond ordinary comprehension, often called psychic.

This collection invites everyone to experience themselves as energy. Psychic ability is a natural capacity, though often suppressed by a paradigm suggesting we are merely “dead matter,” rather than vibrant energy. Scientists investigate only the physical world, which can undermine our humanity. Being psychic is our inherent potential, and these energies are meant to awaken that capacity.

**The Discs**

The Discs are provided in a specific order, which is recommended when first engaging with them.

[[A0454|A0454 - Blue Series]]:

1. **[[The Harry Lime Theme]]**

The lower mental realm corresponds to an “it” intelligence, a reflexive consciousness similar to how a flower turns toward the sun. This realm doesn’t reach rational thinking and is disconnected from the real world, producing reflexive, non-rational solutions. This limited consciousness compresses and congests our subtle structures, creating a block to the life-force current. This Energy is a reminder to allow conductivity throughout, decompressing and opening the passageway to higher development.

2. **[[The Yawning Gap]]**

Anthropology suggests that Cro-Magnon man suddenly emerged from Neanderthal man, an evolutionary shift that science struggles to explain. Modern science lacks understanding of how far-reaching this development was. This adaptation’s failure is linked to increased self-consciousness, which fosters a fear-of-death drama that contracts the brain-mind. The Yawning Gap Disc aligns with True Blue but relates to the evolutionary event 150,000 years ago. True evolution moves beyond self-consciousness into a fearless, self-sacrificing consciousness—the ultimate aim of evolution.

3. **[[True Blue]]**

“True Blue” implies reliability and trustworthiness. This Disc involves transforming the lower mental function, currently far from “True Blue.” The lower mental realm is the intellectual domain, mostly driven by “male mind” structures. Its highest, albeit dangerous, achievement is symbolized by the nuclear bomb—a psychic manifestation of the doubt of love. This Disc lifts lower mental functioning to serve the heart, awakening mystical states where the mind bows in humility.

4. **[[Bluer than True]]**

Bluer Than True reaches beyond True Blue into higher mental, intuitive, mystical awareness, awakening frontal brain functions for Divine states. This functioning arises from intuitive recognition of Truth, penetrating illusion and dissolving limitations, notably the fear of death. This Energy leads to reclaiming the higher brain functions (clairvoyance, clairaudience, clairsentience), which ultimately dissolve into omnipresence and omniscience, grounded in the Divine power of Love.

5. **[[Way Beyond the Blue]]**

Way Beyond The Blue features Alchymeically transformed elements, from hydrogen to a hypothetical element 200, symbolizing transcendent realms. These elements lift consciousness above earthly chaos (around elements 119-120) into mystical states, where powers associated with saints become accessible. However, this ascent is not Truth itself but rather an awakened form of realized consciousness, blissfully immersed in the Divine.
