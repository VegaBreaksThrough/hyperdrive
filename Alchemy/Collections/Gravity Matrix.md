‘Gravity’ is a key aspect in the matter of incarnation. The word ‘Grave’ is held within it. With  each spin around the sun, we are becoming a day older, heading toward the ‘grave’ (thus death  and mortality are the end point of this continual spin of existence). ‘Gravity’ is locked within  every aspect of our involvement in human existence. It weighs upon us, eventually crushes us,  bends us over and we die. 

Thus ‘Gravity Healing’ is lifting us above all that is crushing and beyond the density weaved by  the cycle. Without help individuals find it profoundly difficult to know their relationship to the  Divine. Hence at this time God is coming to mankind, as mankind can no longer come to God. As at this time humanity has fallen toward the hell worlds. 

Ultimately, the gravitational pull of existence is so intense around the terminal point of the  Universe, that Light is completely excluded and we are drawn into all of the ‘Gravity’ as an  entrapment. Hence, we witness the ‘Gravity’ of our heartlessness and loveless ness, which  perfectly coincides with the ‘gravity’ of this earthly vision. We sink down into more dense  states. The more gravity, the denser, the more the gravitational pull in excluding the Light. 

This is the only place within the Universe where there is Lawfulness. Without lawfulness, there  would be no consequence, as in the subtle realms. With consequence there is feedback. Through Divine Grace there will always be an Enlightened Being appear at any one time, to  come to show the way. Without the appearance of Enlightened Beings, this realm would  continually wind toward hellish realms. 

They come to reveal the Truth to mankind. Their appearance prevents the imploding of this  realm into darkness. Each teacher comes unfolding a little of the whole process, so that this  place does not fall into the hellish realms. But at this time, the degree of turning away from God  has brought a critical point that has now forced God to come to mankind.
