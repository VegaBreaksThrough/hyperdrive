There is a moral quesztion here that must inform our actions.  Obviously the actions that have lead to the present condition of the world have arisen from ‘self focused’ purposes, and thus we have come to a critical time in history, for human kind and the planet itself!  For thousands of years most of humanity has been motivated by these ‘self focused purposes’.  As a result the warring, the polluting, the ignorance, the exploitation, the devastation, the greed, the utter unconsciousness has had a profoundly negative energetic impact on the earth as a living organism. The results of which are before us now, every field is polluted and disturbed; Chemical, Electrical and Magnetic.

  

Since the consequences of our actions have brought us to this critical moment, there appears to be only one way beyond the outcome of ‘self focused’ actions, and that is transcendence of self. So that our actions arise out of the mystery and felt response to everything and every being rather than from the self focused position. Ultimately this is the ‘Esoteric’ point of view.

Discs

  

Melting Ice

  

We are now facing the consequences of exploitation, egoic pursuits and greed as we watch the melting ice caps and face the prospect of global warming. The impenetrability of the male mind has greatly contributed to this present day condition; the thickening of the earth’s atmosphere and subsequent trapping of the sun’s rays. Melting Ice is intended to bring consciousness, feeling and awakening to the urgency humanity faces – to move beyond ‘self’ and awaken as a current of Unity.

  

Verdant Functions

  

Holds the Essences of Nourishment. Verdant is green, like Chlorophyll in plants,  which allows the process of photo-synthesis turning sunlight into food. Hence, Verdant becomes the function and helps us absorb the sunlight.

  
  
  

[[A0243|Sun Works]]

  

Energetically this Vial is to do with all phenomena that can be observed about the sun; sun spots, sun gases, sun forces, solar rotation etc. The Alchymie of using these, an energetic resource, is held within the intention of this Disc. Energetically lifting the effects of the Sun, it’s gravity, the rotation, and how it is the major influence to life on earth are all aspects of this energy.

  

Wands

  

Drought Daemon Wizard

  

The dryness, the harshness and unrelenting red heat carry an almost ‘daemonic’ presence of aggravation and suffering. Drought is like a daemon that cripples the land and people. Work this wand to energetically release these effects and purify the karma that creates such a condition. 

  

Rainmaker Wizard

  

The rain brings a blessing of coolness, relief and nourishment as it cleanses the land and replenishes life. Energetically, this Wand is intended to ‘break the spell’ and evoke the creation of rain. Return the balance – try working Drought Daemon and Rainmaker together. 

  

Earth Works

  

To do with all of the phenomena that can be observed within the earth plane. Earthquakes, tidal waves, electric storms, geothermal, geo triplet. The energetic resource of this Vial is felt in relation to our own form.
