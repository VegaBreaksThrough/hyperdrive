# Protagonists

**Primary Protagonists**

1. **Hermes Trismegistus** – The central figure, a sage who receives divine revelations and imparts wisdom.

2. **[[Poimandres]]** – The divine intellect (Nous) that reveals cosmological and spiritual knowledge to Hermes.

3. **Tat** – The son and disciple of Hermes, often asking questions and receiving teachings.

4. **Asclepius** – A student of Hermes, engaged in discussions on divine and natural philosophy.

5. **Ammon** – A lesser-known interlocutor in the dialogues, sometimes identified as a student of Hermes.

  

**Divine and Cosmic Figures**

6. **The Mind (Nous)** – The divine intellect, often associated with Poimandres, guiding Hermes.

7. **God (The One, The Good, The Father)** – The ultimate source of creation and the highest principle in Hermetic philosophy.

8. **The Demiurge** – The creator of the material world, sometimes depicted as subordinate to God.

9. **Nature (Physis)** – A divine force responsible for creation and the natural order.

10. **Man (Anthropos)** – The cosmic human, made in God’s image, capable of ascending to the divine.

11. **Higher Beings (Celestial Intelligences, Daimons, Gods)** – Various spiritual entities that interact with humanity.

  

**Other Interlocutors and Seekers**

12. **The Disciple** – An unnamed student receiving teachings from Hermes or his followers.

13. **Unnamed Seekers** – Various figures who ask questions and engage in spiritual inquiry throughout the dialogues.
