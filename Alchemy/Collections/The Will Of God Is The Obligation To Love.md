#### For the reader, from the editors:

The contents of this book have been gathered from formal and informal writings; articles, statements, dictations, conversations with, and poetry by the author known as Jessa O’My Heart, during the years 1996 - 2008. The author refers to them as ‘Scraps’ or ‘Scribbles’. Those of us who love and recognise the author’s ecstatic understanding of the nature of existence have gathered and presented his writings here, in the form of this publication, for the enjoyment of those who read with an open mind and heart, in the hope that they are found to be inspiring and illuminating.

We suggest contemplating each article or statement, in the same way as one might savour a love letter; taking time to feel its essence, rather than trying to garner something we might call ‘knowledge’. A love letter is read for its emotional impact upon the heart. There is no particular order to the writings, and what seems most relevant may change from one day to the next. We suggest returning to the articles and reading them again and again over time. The writings can be challenging and confronting - they are [[Ecstatic Communication]] and present the form of reality. They may often focus on a topic or area usually avoided, brushed aside or ‘covered’ over with a veil of illusion to make us feel more comfortable about ourselves. Certain philosophical stances may prevent us from seeing, such as, ‘my rational mind says this is impossible’ and so we may dismiss the form of reality as it is presented here if we don’t allow ourselves to be a ‘broken-hearted’ reader. Also see Metareflection: [[The Medium is the Message or is it the Massage?]]

### **Glossary**

**[[Actuality]]:**
Everyday ‘reality’, or the dream state, is termed [[Actuality]] for the purpose of this book. [[Actuality]] is the everyday ‘reality’ that most people take for granted, but in Truth is not real. [[Actuality]] is like a dream state, which we only call ‘real’, rather than recognizing it as a temporary psycho-physical effusion or dream. (See Glossary: [[Reality]])

**[[Alchymie]] / [[Alchymie|Hermetic]] / [[Alchymie|Alchymeic]]:**

*(See the Scribble: [[Alchymie|What is Alchymie?]])*

Alchemy is commonly known as the transmutation of base metal into gold. Considering this as an early form of chemistry is fallacious. In our understanding, this statement is a metaphor, being that it is the person that is the base metal. So it is about enlightenment - the gold of enlightenment. The word is spelled as it was in the old language – [[Alchymie]] – to differentiate it from chemistry. This spelling reflects our understanding of [[Alchymie]] as real spiritual transformation.

The origins of [[Alchymie]] are ancient and mysterious. The legendary Egyptian philosopher and Alchymist, Hermes Trismegistus (also known as Thoth), developed the [[Alchymie|alchymeic]] process known as Hermetic or [[Alchymie|Hermetic]] Science and wrote the Emerald Tablet. Upon this Emerald Tablet, Hermes inscribed his famous observation: “As above, so below,” as well as a description and language of the process of creation. Most, if not all, historical evidence of Hermes Trismegistus, including the Emerald Tablet, has been destroyed, hidden, or lost over time. There are now only channeled or intuited versions available.

The likelihood that any serious statement of Hermetic Science exists with any authority is most unlikely. Commonplace descriptions may exist in the world of the “puffers” and those willing to make exorbitant statements. (Puffers are dilettantes with respect to Alchymie, huffing and puffing and getting no- where, because they take it all so literally!) 

We have to separate myth from actuality - Gold as a metal is normally made in stars (through nuclear fusion). Imagine trying to transmute it from base metals in a little pot on earth! The process of turning lead into gold has to be metaphorical or allegorical. Turning us into enlightened beings itself takes a huge amount of energy and is remarkably difficult - practically impossible for most people. Spiritual transformation has all the elements of Alchymie - it requires a huge amount of heat applied steadily over years to make a difference to the usual consciousness, which is dense, karmic and lead-like. Gold is the symbol of enlightenment. 

There is scant information on Pharaonic Alchymie, but it can be surmised that Hermetic Alchymie is a development upon Pharaonic Alchymie. The scarcity of historic information leaves us little else to say.



**Angelic Forms / Angels:**
Within the cosmic mandala, there are angelic realms. Angels are fixed forms in this world - whereas the human being can range from everything from the lowest to the highest states. Angels purport to be, in some cases, great help to human beings, but they need to be directed. It is also purported that they live a long, long time, but they are still forms within the cosmic domain and eventually everything put together falls apart. One can imagine they must enjoy blissful states all the while, although they can obviously suffer pain for all the loveless condition that is the earth realm. Eventually they’ll be translated to the Divine domain. What gathers that is a mystery.

**Ascension:** 

It is possible to release attention from its earth bound concentration, to let it drift up the frontal line, beyond the crown, encountering many subtle objects as our attention drifts ever upward. As we allow this process of rising, or apparent rising, we are actually reclaiming parts of ourselves that were occluded by having our concentration bound to the lower energies below the brow. 

**Attention:** 
Normally attention is undisciplined and wandering in the world. Wherever it rests in the world, we duplicate in body and mind. For instance, looking at barbells - we end up with barbells in body and mind. Attention is ‘enforced’ according to our karma. We are not in control of attention, only the discipline of attention. To discipline attention forever in the heart, we must keep it at the root of the nose, randomly remembering to do so, until at last it becomes stable and drops into the heart on the right side. There is no self-will involved. When the attention is disciplined we are simply throwing everything away all the time via conductivity (breathing to and from infinity) and keeping attention at the root of the nose.

**Awakened / Awaken:** 
This cosmic condition is a dream state (not just like a dream state). The only difference is that there are consequences to actions in this state - it is a consequential dream state. To be awakened is to realise our cosmic condition as a dream state. Awakened we are in some sense then gifted with the form of reality and to know when we have lost that form. 

**Awakening:** 
This awakening is no different from waking up in the morning. 

**Below the Brow:** 
Below the ‘brow’ of the gross-vital form. Our practice is to keep attention at the root of the nose, which is the seat of consciousness relative to the body. That is: everything high and low passes through such a point (only relative to the body). Below the brow we are equivalent to a dog. If our attention is enforced concentration below the brow, as long as we are drawn to such an event, we live what we might call the ‘low life’ – drawn to fixations in the genitals, navel and over exhaustion of the eyes. 

**Blissful:** 
This is merely a by-product engendered by the condition that is happiness. Happiness itself is blissful. It is not acquired, it is simply arising as a condition of happiness itself. It is the bodily experience that is happiness - simple recognition of reality itself.

**Body:**
A *multidimensional* structure that coincides with the cosmos. There are millions of different ways of viewing the body: Human body, energy body, emotional body, *gross* body, etheric body (appear like a ghost to those living as the *gross* vital body) etc. 

Holistic practitioners already account for the *subtle dimension* of the body, but there is much, much more than even that. There is also the *causal body*, and etheric body as comprehended by yogis and *realisers*.

The *whole body* is the whole cosmos, all of the extended form of the universe. It is an unfathomable *multidimensional* structure. 

The common use of the word body is associated with the food body – the *gross-vital* form - the vegetative function, that abomination, that unnecessary addition to *consciousness*, it’s a *disease*. At so-called birth, we contract a disease in the form of the food body, which continues for as long as the *gross-vital* is present, but we still have an impulse towards this *disease* even when it drops away.

We appear here, in this earth realm, as the *gross-vital* form. Without it we don’t appear here. This *body*, that appears here as all those people we bump into in the street, also appears to be solid, but even that is also only a matter of *mind*. (The female disposition identifies with the *food body*, the *unconscious* vegetative function that ultimately dies.) The *food body* is not ‘alive’ and further it is not responsible for the uptightness and it has no way to respond to this type of prompting. The *food body* is a vegetative function. We bring change to it, not by talking to it from the point of view of the *mind*, but by transcending the *mind*. The *mind* is in time; the *body* is in space. The *body* is the past and it reflects the activity of the *mind* - in transcending the *mind* in *ecstatic communion* with the Divine, the body at last follows into that *communion*.

The body is referred to in this book as the gross, or physical, form of existence. It is one aspect of the whole being, which also includes subtle and causal dimensions. The body is the vessel through which we experience the physical world and is subject to the laws of nature, including birth, growth, decay, and death.

**Body-mind / brain-mind:**
This term refers to the integrated functioning of the body and mind, particularly how the brain processes and influences both physical and mental experiences. The body-mind is a holistic concept where the physical and mental aspects are interconnected, affecting each other continuously.

The brain-mind is a ‘limiter’ that produces this vision of human life. There may be aspects of mind that are not dependent on the brain, but for life in this world, the mind is dependent on the brain.

**Body-mind-self:**
This term describes the combined identity of the body, mind, and self as experienced by individuals. It represents the ordinary, ego-based sense of self that identifies with the physical body and personal mind, often leading to a limited and separate sense of identity.

The ‘soul’ or all that which must be yielded up at last in order to realise God.

**Causal Body / Causal Space:**
This is prior to waking and dreaming or the gross and subtle. It is the deep sleep state, in which we are rejuvenated, the source state. It is untouchable from the dream state or waking consciousness. The hermetic touch is prior to the causal, sourced from the realm of Divine states - it is untouchable and incorruptible - beyond anyone’s influence.

The causal body is the deepest layer of the human experience, beyond the gross (physical) and subtle (mental) bodies. It is associated with the realm of pure potential and the source of all forms and experiences. Causal space is the dimension in which this body exists, often related to deep sleep or transcendental states.

Sitting formally as happiness, breathing to and from infinity. At last, this becomes an ongoing moment-to-moment resting in Only God. (See the Scribble: [[Are Fridge Magnet Admonitions Messages from Houdini?]] )

Communion refers to the deep spiritual connection or union with the Divine. Divine Communion is the state of being in constant awareness and relationship with God, where the individual self is transcended, and only the Divine remains as the true reality.

**Confession / Happy Confession:**
Confession in this context is the acknowledgment of one’s limitations, errors, and sins before God, accompanied by a sincere desire for forgiveness and transformation. Happy Confession goes further to recognize and confess the joy of surrendering to Divine Will, embracing the truth of existence beyond personal suffering.

**Conscious / Consciousness:**
Consciousness is the state of being aware of and able to perceive, think, and experience. It is the fundamental quality of being that allows us to engage with reality, both internal and external. In spiritual terms, consciousness is often seen as the essence of the Divine, the underlying reality of all existence.

**[[Conductivity]] / Conducting:**
Conductivity refers to the ability to channel or transmit spiritual energy, often related to practices that open the body and mind to Divine influence. Conducting is the process of allowing this energy to flow through oneself, facilitating healing, transformation, and spiritual awakening.

**[[Cosmic Display]]:**
This term describes the entire manifest universe as a play of Divine energy. The [[Cosmic Display]] is the outward expression of the Divine, seen as an interconnected and dynamic field of existence, where every part reflects the whole.

**[[The Nine Angelic Realms|Creative Centres]]:**
[[The Nine Angelic Realms|Creative centres]] are the focal points within the body-mind where energy and consciousness converge to manifest creativity and expression. These centres are often associated with [[The Nine Angelic Realms|chakras]] or energy points in the body, which influence different aspects of life and consciousness.

**[[Creative Control]]:**
Creative control refers to the ability to direct and shape one’s experiences and reality through intentional focus and alignment with Divine Will. It is the practice of using one’s creative power in harmony with the greater purpose of life.

**[[Crucible]] / Alchymeic Crucible:**
A crucible is a container where substances are melted or transformed by heat. In Alchymic terms, the crucible represents the process of spiritual transformation, where the soul undergoes intense purification and refinement to achieve enlightenment.

**Death Process:**
The death process is the transition from physical life to the afterlife. It is viewed not as an end but as a transformation, where the soul leaves the physical body and enters a different state of existence. This process involves the shedding of the gross body and the continuation of life in subtle or causal dimensions.

**Disease:**
Disease is seen as a manifestation of imbalance or disharmony within the body-mind-self. It is often the result of blocked energy, unresolved emotions, or spiritual disconnection. Healing requires addressing the root causes of disease on all levels – physical, mental, and spiritual.

**[[Divine Madness|Divine Madness]]:**
Divine madness refers to the state of being beyond conventional reason and logic, where one is completely immersed in the ecstatic experience of the Divine. It is a form of spiritual intoxication, where the individual transcends ordinary understanding and operates from a place of pure love and inspiration.

**Divine Destiny, Divine Inspiration, Divine Purification, Divine Healing, Divine Transformation, Divine Possession:**
These terms describe the various ways the Divine influences and guides the life of an individual. Divine destiny is the ultimate path or purpose that God has for each soul. Divine inspiration is the inner guidance received from God. Divine purification is the cleansing process that removes obstacles to spiritual growth. Divine healing is the restoration of health and harmony through Divine intervention. Divine transformation is the profound change that occurs when one aligns with Divine Will. Divine possession refers to being fully surrendered to and possessed by the Divine Presence, where the ego is completely dissolved.

**Dream of Death:**
The dream of death refers to the illusory nature of physical death. It is the belief that death is the end of existence, rather than a transition to another state of being. In truth, death is merely a shift from one form of existence to another, part of the ongoing cycle of life.

**Dream-state / Subtle Dimension:**
The dream-state is the level of consciousness where we experience dreams, often associated with the subtle dimension of existence. This dimension is more refined than the physical but still involves form and experience. It is the realm of thoughts, emotions, and subtle energies.

**Ecstatic / Ecstasy:**
Ecstasy is the state of being carried away by overwhelming joy, love, or spiritual experience. It is a condition where the ego is transcended, and one becomes fully immersed in the presence of the Divine. Ecstatic refers to the nature of such experiences, which are beyond ordinary comprehension and profoundly transformative.

**[[[[Ecstatic Communication]] / Real Communication:**
[[Ecstatic Communication]] is the transmission of Divine truth and love through words, actions, or presence. It is not merely the exchange of information but a deep, soul-to-soul connection that transcends ordinary communication. Real communication involves being fully present and open to the Divine in every interaction.

**Embodiment:**
Embodiment is the process of bringing spiritual truths and principles into physical form. It involves living in alignment with Divine Will and manifesting the qualities of the Divine in every aspect of life. Embodiment also refers to the way in which the soul inhabits the body and expresses itself in the world.

**Energy and Feeling:**
Energy and feeling are closely related, as feelings are often the expression of underlying energies within the body-mind-self. Understanding and managing these energies is essential for spiritual growth, as they influence our thoughts, emotions, and physical well-being.

**Eternal Life:**
Eternal life is the state of being that transcends physical death. It is the [[Realisation|realization]] of one’s true nature as an immortal soul, always connected to the Divine. Eternal life is not merely endless existence but a continuous state of blissful union with God.

**Exist:**
To exist is to have being, to be alive, to be present. In spiritual terms, existence is not limited to physical life but includes all dimensions of being. True existence is rooted in the Divine, the source of all that is.

**[[Feeling Dimension]]:**
The [[Feeling Dimension]] refers to the realm of emotions and sensations, where we experience life on a more subtle level. It is the bridge between the physical and the spiritual, allowing us to connect deeply with ourselves, others, and the Divine.

Touch is whole body involvement: It involves everything. [[Alchymie|Hermetic]] is holistic, referring to the [[Alchymie|alchymeic]] transformation through imbuement of the Touch - whole body engagement in and as reality. It is not a ‘something’. It is the flash of gold in the pan. It is Eureka! It is the excitement of life rushing through our veins after we have been numbed. It is God power that awakens the sleeping beast. It is our response to, as well as the Presence Itself, of the Divine. Our true response demonstrates our love of God more than self. Hence, the [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] is a prayer as well as the Eureka! 

The [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] penetrates the illusion of separation of the mind. It is not possible to make this ‘clear’ to the mind. It is our intuited and felt response. We can’t wait upon understanding, clarity, or wisdom: We must simply be that response. 

The [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] is the entrance of the Divine into the ordinary situation.

**Hermes Prophecy:**
A prophecy of the philosopher and Alchymist Hermes Trismegistus:

“Every sacred voice will be silenced.  
Darkness will be preferred to light.  
No eyes will raise to Heaven.  
The pure will be thought insane and the impure will be honored as wise.  
The madmen will be believed brave and the wicked esteemed as good.  
Knowledge of the immortal soul will be laughed at and denied.  
No reverent words worthy of Heaven will be heard or believed.  
So I, thrice great Hermes, the first of all men to attain all knowledge,  
have inscribed the secrets of the Gods, in sacred symbols and hieroglyphs, on these sacred stone tablets, which I have concealed for a future world, that may seek our sacred wisdom.”

**His Victory:**
Refers to the triumph of the Divine over all illusions, obstacles, and ignorance. It signifies the ultimate success of God's will in the world and within each individual, where all resistance to Divine truth is overcome.

**Illusion / Dream:**
The perception of reality as separate from the Divine, which is considered an illusion or dream. In spiritual teachings, the world as we ordinarily perceive it is seen as a temporary and deceptive appearance, masking the true, eternal reality of God.

**Immortal / Immortality:**
Immortality refers to the eternal existence of the soul, beyond physical death. It is the [[Realisation|realization]] of one's true nature as eternal and indestructible, living in unity with the Divine.

**Karma / Karmic Destiny:**
Karma is the law of cause and effect, where every action has a corresponding reaction, shaping one's future experiences. Karmic destiny refers to the accumulation of actions and their consequences that determine the course of an individual's life.

**Life-force:**
The vital energy that animates all living beings. It is the sustaining power of life, flowing through the body, mind, and spirit, often associated with breath, prana, or chi.

**Mystical / Mysticism:**
Mysticism is the pursuit of direct, personal experience of the Divine, beyond intellectual understanding or religious rituals. Mystical experiences are profound encounters with the sacred, often characterized by a sense of unity, love, and transcendence.

**No seeking / No dilemma:**
A state of spiritual [[Realisation|realization]] where there is no longer any search for truth or conflict within the mind. It is the condition of being fully at peace, knowing that all is already complete and perfect in the Divine.

**[[Reality]] / [[Realisation|Realization]]:**
Reality, in the spiritual sense, refers to the true, unchanging nature of existence, which is God. [[Realisation|Realization]] is the awakening to this truth, where one sees beyond the illusions of the world and recognizes the Divine as the only reality.

**Remembrance / Divine Remembrance:**
The practice of constantly recalling and being aware of the Divine presence. It is the cultivation of a deep, ongoing connection with God, where the mind and heart are always oriented toward the Divine.

**Sub-human:**
The human is that creature awakened to the truth of us, which is Only God. All else is sub-human, which is most of the world – everything is designed upon the desires and impulses of the sub-human in this world, which is not a great moment – very humble stuff.

**[[Subtle communication]] / [[Subtle communication|subtle body communication]]:**
The subtle forms of the gross body can often get distorted through tensions in the gross vital form, and these distortions get broadcast as [[Subtle communication|subtle body communication]]. Most people are quite nonchalantly unaware they are broadcasting their own contracted states, which is most often felt as a pain in the head or heart, but can be any pains of greater or lesser intensity in the gross-vital form.

**Unconscious / Unconsciousness:**
The mind is our capacity to reflect things to ourselves. To have no reflection is to be unconscious. We need to be able to engage the reflecting process and to be cognizant about ourselves in this world, which includes appropriate levels of behavior. We have to be able to engage the moral disposition. (N.B. We need to be able to participate in the mind of reflection to be cognizant, but not be trapped by it, or its constructs as an addictive process. We need to be possessed by heart feeling as love.)

**Usual / Ordinary / Conventional / Conventional life:**
This often refers to the common personality and what is ordinarily or usually done. From our degeneration into rationalism, we see this world as being peopled by billions of apparent others. The truth is there is no such thing as billions of apparent others; they are figments in a dream. In our rationalism, these figments seem to take on an actual existence, whereas really they are just figments of ourselves we embrace as apparently real. It is all just an ongoing dream. Just so, all kinds of rational notions are imposed on these figments. These are conventional because we live by cultural conventions, including all levels of desiring and restrictions. Of course, there are extremes that stand outside the bounds of what we like to call humans, for instance, psychopaths—where everything is simply decided by their own core personality, as judge, jury, and executioner.
