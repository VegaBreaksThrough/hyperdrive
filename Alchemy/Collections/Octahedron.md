# Introduction

The Octahedron is an Alchemical Energy Tool based on Hermetic understanding. They are a group of Octahedrons that mark an exceptional development. They have arrived at a time when humanity greatly needs the restoration of the intuition of the awakening of the Heart. Therefore, these tools are directly enabling the opportunity to initiate a completely new vibrational level, an evolutionary leap that ignites a great acceleration of energy within our auric fields and the energy circuit of the body to invite the true blossoming of human potential. The Octahedron is designed to be used on oneself as well as for the purpose of performing energy work on others. It is always better to familiarize oneself with the product before using it on others, by connecting with its current and allowing that initiation within oneself.

# The Alchemy of Creation

With the Divine vision observed while creating the Octahedron, there were two Fractalizers or pyramids. The upper pyramid or spiral represents the masculine disposition (which is oriented upwards and outwards). This pyramid appeared pointing downward, towards the earthly plane. The other pyramid, representing the feminine form (which is the world in its extended form), is shown pointing upward above the plane of the earth. In the alchemical process, these two pyramids or viewpoints intersect at the Third Eye or seat of Consciousness. That polarized current, that condition of awakening as changing polarities, allows for the real birth of what is shown as the Octahedron. It is the seed of life, beginnings, the spark, and the fire of True Life, where each polarity awakens to include the other. The true awakened form is where the Masculine as Consciousness descends into the dimension of feeling reflected in the body, and the Feminine rises above the body to include Consciousness. The true Hermetic form updates, "as above so below."

# The Creation of the Octahedrons

In Hermetic terms, the Octahedrons are the seed of mineral life, upon which everything depends. Hence, they were delivered from the Light to become tools for use in the body-mind-self. The golden tips of the cornerstones are the points of direction. The sphere contained in the center is the source of the directional intensity of the Octahedrons. While creating these tools, extreme levels of heat and reconstructive energy were felt. Each one is a dynamo to provoke a greater flow of energy in our auras and, likewise, in our lives.
