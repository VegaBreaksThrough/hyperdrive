Darkness is much more than the absence of light or knowledge. This is how we have discovered it, little by little, in the journey of transforming ourselves.

When we use the term "darkness" in that path of [[Realisation|Realization]], we expand that ordinary definition to refer to any lack of Consciousness in the understanding of the Mechanics of [[Reality]], that is, in the functioning of everything we know.

If [[Realisation|Realization]], in a very simplified way, alludes to a state of continuous and perpetual Happiness, a state obviously much higher than that of Peace or Balance, we could infer that darkness is everything that prevents us from remaining in Happiness.

We then move from a concept that exposes absence to one of an abundant nature in "test" situations. This expansion in the concept gives us, to those whose occupation is to transform darkness, a greater focus and compression and, therefore, a better result in our activity.

In addition, it leads us to the conclusion that the only possible place where that darkness could exist is within us, so we are solely responsible for its existence in our [[Reality]].

Undoubtedly, this concept of "darkness" presents us with a greater challenge, but it allows us to identify more clearly everything we must transform in ourselves to move forward in the only path that is our true responsibility and only purpose: [[Realisation|Realization]].

Now, are you able to identify the darkness in any of the "trial" situations in your life?
