*Intimacy* with the Divine is the only true *intimacy* that is possible. We do not enjoy *intimacy* with any apparent others. When two or more people are absorbed in God, this enjoyment of *intimacy* is shared. This is not ‘closeness’, which is really not possible since all of us are like waves on the ocean \- we come to be, we endure for a time and then we are gone from this realm.
