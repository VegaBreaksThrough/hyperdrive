# Jessa

**No seeking / No dilemma:**
_"A state of spiritual realization where there is no longer any search for truth or conflict within the mind. It is the condition of being fully at peace, knowing that all is already complete and perfect in the Divine."_

**Reality /Relisation:**
_"Reality, in the spiritual sense, refers to the true, unchanging nature of existence, which is God. Realization is the awakening to this truth, where one sees beyond the illusions of the world and recognizes the Divine as the only reality."_

# Carl Jung
Stage 1: outer world, defining ourselves in the world. Natural life is the nourishing soil of the soul. Contact with nature, the body, the feeling, the instinct,  not to be lost in abstract thinking.
Stage 2: inner world, quest for [[Realisation|Individuation Process]]. Integrating the conscious and unconscious into a whole.
Conformity is the death of selfhood.

The task of Alchemy is psychological. It is the art of expanding consciousness to self-realization. We can only surrender to the higher wisdom of the self.
