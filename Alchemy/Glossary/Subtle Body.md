Together, Ida, Pingala, and Sushumna form the core energetic system in the **subtle body**, guiding the balance between opposites (moon/sun, intuition/action) and fostering spiritual development.

1. **Ida**:

• Represents the **lunar energy**.

• Associated with the **left nostril**, feminine qualities, intuition, and the **parasympathetic nervous system** (calming energy).

• Symbolizes **cooling**, reflective, and nurturing aspects of the self.

2. **Pingala**:

• Represents the **solar energy**.

• Associated with the **right nostril**, masculine qualities, action, and the **sympathetic nervous system** (stimulating energy).

• Symbolizes **warming**, active, and energizing aspects of the self.

3. **Sushumna**:

• The **central energy channel** that runs along the spine.

• It becomes active when **Ida** and **Pingala** energies are balanced, allowing energy (prana) to flow freely.

• This is considered the path to **spiritual awakening**, as it aligns the chakras and enables the rise of **kundalini energy**.
