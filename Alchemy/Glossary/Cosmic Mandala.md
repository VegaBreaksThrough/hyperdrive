The **Cosmic Mandala** of **Adi Da Samraj** is a framework within his teachings that represents the structure of existence and consciousness. It illustrates the relationship between the individual and the greater [[Reality]], encompassing physical, subtle, and transcendental dimensions. The mandala serves as a map for spiritual understanding and awakening.

  

Below is an overview of the Cosmic Mandala and its levels, as described in Adi Da’s teachings:

  

**1. Overview of the Cosmic Mandala**

• The mandala represents **[[Reality]] as a whole**, including:

• The physical realm.

• Subtle realms of energy and mind.

• Causal and transcendental realms.

• It is both a **symbolic representation** and a **tool for realization**, guiding practitioners through stages of spiritual development.

• Adi Da emphasizes the necessity of awakening beyond identification with any level of the mandala to realize the “Bright,” or the ultimate nature of Reality as Consciousness Itself.

  

**2. Levels of the Cosmic Mandala**

  

The mandala is divided into **seven levels** or stages, corresponding to aspects of existence and spiritual progression:

  

**1st Level: Gross Physical Realm**

• **Description**: The realm of physical matter and sensory experience.

• **Focus**: Survival, bodily existence, and engagement with the material world.

• **Significance**: Represents the starting point of human life and awareness, where identity is rooted in the body and physical reality.

  

**2nd Level: Subtle Etheric Realm**

• **Description**: The energy body, including prana (life force) and the flow of energies within and around the physical body.

• **Focus**: Life force regulation, emotional patterns, and vitality.

• **Significance**: Awareness begins to extend beyond mere physicality into the subtle energy dynamics of life.

  

**3rd Level: Mind and Thought Realm**

• **Description**: The domain of mental activity, thought processes, and conceptual understanding.

• **Focus**: Rational thinking, problem-solving, and egoic self-awareness.

• **Significance**: The ego solidifies its sense of individuality through thoughts and intellectual engagement.

  

**4th Level: Higher Subtle Realm**

• **Description**: The intuitive and psychic dimensions of the mind, including imagination and visionary experiences.

• **Focus**: Inner exploration, meditation, and encounters with archetypes and symbolic imagery.

• **Significance**: Points to the beginning of transcendence, where one starts to go beyond egoic constructs.

  

**5th Level: Causal Realm**

• **Description**: The source or seed of individuality, often experienced as profound stillness or emptiness.

• **Focus**: Detachment from the gross and subtle dimensions, identification with pure awareness.

• **Significance**: Leads to a sense of formlessness and non-duality but can still involve subtle self-identification.

  

**6th Level: Transitional Realization**

• **Description**: The level of ego-death and transcendence into non-dual awareness.

• **Focus**: The dissolution of identification with the body, mind, and subtle egoic tendencies.

• **Significance**: Awareness shifts from the personal self to the universal or cosmic aspect of existence.

  

**7th Level: Transcendental Bright**

• **Description**: The ultimate realization of the “Bright,” or the transcendental Consciousness that is the Source and Reality of all.

• **Focus**: Absolute unity with Divine Consciousness, beyond any differentiation or duality.

• **Significance**: Represents the culmination of spiritual awakening, where the individual is no longer separate from Reality.

  

**3. Key Principles of the Cosmic Mandala**

• **Interconnectedness**:

• The levels are not separate but interrelated, forming a continuum of existence.

• **Transcendence**:

• Each level represents a stage that must ultimately be transcended to realize the “Bright.”

• **Practical Application**:

• Adi Da’s spiritual practices aim to help practitioners identify their current stage and progress through the levels toward realization.

  

**4. The Bright and the Cosmic Mandala**

• The **Bright** is Adi Da’s term for the ultimate, formless, and indivisible Consciousness that pervades all levels of existence.

• The mandala is a symbolic depiction of the process of returning to this fundamental Reality, transcending the illusion of separateness.

  

**5. Spiritual Practice in Relation to the Mandala**

• **Self-Observation**:

• Awareness of one’s current stage or fixation within the mandala.

• **Meditative Practices**:

• Techniques to transcend identification with specific levels of existence.

• **Guidance by the Guru**:

• Adi Da emphasizes the necessity of spiritual transmission and guidance to navigate the mandala effectively.

  

**Conclusion**

  

Adi Da’s **Cosmic Mandala** offers a profound map of existence, from the gross physical to the ultimate transcendental reality. It highlights the need to transcend all levels of identification to realize the **Bright** as the true nature of Reality. This mandala is not just a theoretical model but a practical guide for spiritual seekers on their journey to ultimate liberation.
