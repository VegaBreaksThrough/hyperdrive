That which is not our *real possibility* is drawing us to zero. All pursuits at a worldly level are drawing us to zero. Our *real possibility* is to be absorbed utterly in and as *Only God*.

###### **[[Receptivity]]:**

The breath lets us *know* that we can and must open ourselves to the reception of air, food and water: the things which sustain us. We can intensify this receptive process via the breath whereby we can open ourselves to receive more Divine *light* than the breathless shallow breathers. We can refuse food and water for some time (but not long) but we can not refuse the breath for any length of time at all. We must breathe and the breath must be full \- breathing to and from infinity, unmodified by desire (perfect *receptivity* \- at rest and not agitated).

###### **[[Sacrifice]] / Self-sacrifice / Submission and Surrender:**

Surrender suggests a defensive position against the presumed *other*, so *surrender* is to mean yielding up the defensive position. Whereas *[[sacrifice]]* is yielding up the pattern of desires and all seeking, until there is nothing we are clinging to, high or low. In *surrender* we are yielding up the presumed other and therefore *surrendering* the defensive position with the *other*: No *other* is freedom. *Submission* is our yielding to *grace*. *(See Glossary: [[Symbol of our Sacrifice]])*

###### **[[Understand|See]] / [[Understand|Know]] / [[Understand]] / [[Understand|Feel]]:**

Psychically *see* or *know* or *feel* or *understand* \- [[A0214|clairsentience]] \- everything is revealed. Revelation is not like digging up treasure, but occurs simply by abiding in and as that revelation. We *see* that it is simply given. So we don’t rake through the head, looking for some thing through the head. We rest in contemplation, until all is revealed, simply and *gracefully.* It may take days, some time, or be immediate. In any case, we have to wait upon the revelation. Once we set the contemplation in action, it will go on until all is revealed that needs to be in order to satisfy the contemplation.

For example: Things pertaining to [[Memory]] may appear in the environment of our life-sphere. Something may appear on a road sign, or someone may come up and tell us, or it may appear on television. It can appear in the environment. (So [[Memory]] extends into the environment \- Any way at all it can appear. [[Memory]] is everywhere dense.) So [[Memory]] does indeed hang about the *body* somewhere – even thousands of miles away from where our *body* seat is.
###### **Self and Other:**

*[[The Self|Self]]* \- this is regarded as the ‘[[Monad]]’ of Godfrey Libnitz, which is a self-enclosed sense of identity of all things we place under the category of ‘me’. Then we have a whole *other* or ‘not me’ – things we don’t want to identify with. Of course, such a monad does not exist. There is nothing that is self-supporting.

*Others* – there can’t really be others – in truth there is only the One, unity.

###### **Serve / Service / Serving:**

We appear here as *energy and feeling* and with apparent time on our hands. So to *serve* is to yield up our energy and time and grant these to *other* apparent beings in *service* to them. ‘Ego’ would love to claim all our time and energy for ourselves and not yield it up to anyone or anything else (even floors, walls etc).

*Service* is fundamental, it is not a struggle. All *service* is simply moving in our loving. *Service* is devotion in action, a form of *Divine communion.*

###### **Separative / Separativeness:**

Separated and separating. Since there is only the *pattern of relations*, when we fall out of this pattern, this becomes an illusion of separation, for truly there is nothing to be separated. There is no separate *self* \- that is an illusion. There is only the *pattern of relations*. We can enact an apparent separation, as an apparent actor, but all is an illusion. We can only appear to establish such a position, where we act as this absurd character \- the ‘lone ranger’.
