# Jessa
That which is *real* is eternally unmodified. *Reality* is the immutable *God light* that is revealed only after we have sacrificed everything into this *God light* \- space, time, heart beat and so on. The word *reality* is used to describe that which is immutable and eternal, that which underlies the dream state. Recognising this is what we call *realisation*.

Reality, in the spiritual sense, refers to the true, unchanging nature of existence, which is God. [[Realisation|Realization]] is the awakening to this truth, where one sees beyond the illusions of the world and recognizes the Divine as the only reality.
