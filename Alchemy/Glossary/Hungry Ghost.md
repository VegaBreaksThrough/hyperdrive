We appear here as temporary effusions, much like a ghost would, along with the rest of this appearance here. We are filled with the desire to taste of many things we come across, only to discover everything we seek to eat lacks any fulfilment. Still our hunger mounts and like a ghostly vampire, we have this ever- mounting desire to consume. Hence, we are the *hungry ghosts* we fear.
