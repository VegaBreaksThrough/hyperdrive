# Plato 
The [essence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essence "Essence") of a person, being that which decides how people behave.
Source of life and the mind
Three parts: 
	1. The _logos, logistikon_ (head, reason)
	2. The _thymos, thymoeides_ (Thymus, chest region, emotions, spirit, which houses anger, as well as other spirited emotions)
	3. The _eros, epithymetikon_ (Stomach, appetite or desire, which houses the desire for physical pleasures)
	In This context,
		1. Justice is the state of the whole in which each part fulfills its function
		2. Temperance is the state of the whole where each part does not attempt to interfere in the functions of the others 
		3. Injustice is the contrary state of the whole, often taking the specific form in which the spirited are obedient to the appetitive while they together either ignore the logical entirely or employ it in their pursuits of pleasure.
