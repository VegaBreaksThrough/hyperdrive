Adi Da tells us we cannot know what anything is; we look at common objects around us, and we think we know what they are, for instance a chair, but what actually is it? At last we realise we can’t know what anything is. At last we *see* only the *mystery of existence*, and such a position is Divine ignorance. As we evolve, our relationship to the world becomes Divine disinterest. We are no longer drawn excitedly to the world.
