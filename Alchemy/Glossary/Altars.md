*Altars related to altars*

As Pallisade could not finish clearing some altars, he realized that they were being held by other altars.

In light of this, he searched for and cleared the related altars and magicians.  Finally, he resumed the clearing of the initial altars.

Although it is not a very common case, if the altar clearing process becomes difficult, the recommendation is to ask: "Are there altars related to these altars?", and follow the usual procedure to clear them.
