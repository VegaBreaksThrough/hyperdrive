Creative control refers to the ability to direct and shape one’s experiences and [[Reality]] through intentional focus and alignment with Divine Will. It is the practice of using one’s creative power in harmony with the greater purpose of life.

"Normally we think of control as manipulative, where the dice roll in our favour. Creative control however is a way of relating to the frustrations of life, with creative response, not manipulative, not trying to control outcomes therefore, but living in grace – where we see everything is simply given, and we are resting in that given quality, expecting no particular outcome, but hoping for the best."  - Jessa, The Will Of God


_Real living_ is more like _[[satori]]_  than mulling around in the [[muttering dark]]. It behoves us to cease our fascination with all this falsity and stand free within the heart’s recognition of the Truth of us, which is revealed when we embrace the heart of Truth, rather than an obsequious relinquishment of _creative control_ to the thought daemons of our recalcitrant head, the [[The Dalek Invasion|Daleks]] of the soul, the ‘[[Wrong Thinking Harry]].’

**So, via the Self-Point we discover real creative control.**  **“Know ye not that ye are gods?”**
