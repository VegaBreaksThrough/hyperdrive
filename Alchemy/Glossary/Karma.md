We don’t appear here as bright shining innocents \- we carry with us consequences, from other life-times and from our actions in that lifetime. These consequences are the seeds of our present life circumstance. We call these seeds ‘karma’. Evolution is the way to dissolve *karma* \- evolving from *unconscious* animals to the exquisite states of Divine *consciousness*. *Karma* is dissolved by making it unnecessary.

*Karma’s* are tendencies and tendencies come from the mind \- the mind, driven by fear, seeks pleasure or avoids pain. Even what seems to be pleasurable is ultimately destructive and painful \- “these things that are pleasing you hurt you somehow.”[^1] Earth is a feedback circumstance, however life can become a mysterious  *karma* because you don’t enjoy immediate feedback \- it can occur in another life-time so you can’t connect the dots. What we need is the *grace* of immediate feedback or ‘instant karma’[^2].
