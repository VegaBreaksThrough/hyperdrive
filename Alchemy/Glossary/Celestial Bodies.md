Any star or natural object that is part of the celestial sphere and is outside the Earth's atmosphere. It includes stars, [[The Hermetic Gods|Planets]], satellites, comets, asteroids, nebulae, and galaxies.

It includes [[Celestial Mechanics]], that is, the synchronous order of the movement of the stars that gives human beings the possibility of overcoming challenges and learning from them.
