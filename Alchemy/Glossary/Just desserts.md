Viewing various behaviours from the point of view of the human being, and then presuming God might look at them the same way. If someone is getting some of the things we feel they should, we call this *just desserts*. For instance, Saddam Hussein being hung for his misdeeds may be considered *just desserts*. However we are really just informing ourselves of what God may feel about this. We can’t really be sure they are *just desserts*, we just think we are taking God’s eye-view, but we are actually making assumptions about how God should view things.
