*[[The Voyage to Arcturus]]* is a book written by David Lindsey in the early 1900s. This book is about a journey where the main character encounters beings on a planet who have an extra feeling organ coming out of the centre of their chest. We humans do not have such an overt organ, but a covert one also in the centre of the chest. The condition of this covert organ in most of us is contracted to a level of unfeeling. It is necessary for us to awaken the *feeling centre* or *feeling dimension* in order to allow ourselves to be love.

When this *feeling centre* is open, it is felt to be a vulnerable position and we may quickly close it again to protect ourselves. It is difficult to remain loving in a world that feels particularly threatening on many occasions. It follows that we cannot remain in a self-contracted self-protective position and love at the same time.

The feeling dimension refers to the realm of emotions and sensations, where we experience life on a more subtle level. It is the bridge between the physical and the spiritual, allowing us to connect deeply with ourselves, others, and the Divine.
