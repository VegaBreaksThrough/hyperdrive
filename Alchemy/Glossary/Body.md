A *multidimensional* structure that coincides with the cosmos. There are millions of different ways of viewing the body:

Human body, energy body, emotional body, *gross* body, etheric body (appear like a ghost to those living as the *gross* vital body) etc.

Holistic practitioners already account for the *subtle dimension* of the body, but there is much, much more than even that. There is also the *causal body*, and etheric body as comprehended by yogis and *realisers*.

The *whole body* is the whole cosmos, all of the extended form of the universe. It is an unfathomable *multidimensional* structure.

The common use of the word body is associated with the *food body* – the *gross-vital* form \- the vegetative function, that abomination, that unnecessary addition to *consciousness*, it’s a *disease*. At so-called birth, we contract a *disease* in the form of the *food body*, which continues for as long as the *gross-vital* is present, but we still have an impulse towards this *disease* even when it drops away.

We appear here, in this earth realm, as the *gross-vital* form. Without it we don’t appear here. This *body,* that appears here as all those people we bump into in the street, also appears to be solid, but even that is also only a matter of *mind*. (The female disposition identifies with the *food body*, the *unconscious* vegetative function that ultimately dies.) The *food body* is not ‘alive’ and further it is not responsible for the uptightness and it has no way to respond to this type of prompting. The *food body* is a vegetative function. We bring change to it, not by talking to it from the point of view of the *mind*, but by transcending the *mind*. The *mind* is in time; the *body* is in space. The *body* is the past and it reflects the activity of the *mind* \- in transcending the *mind* in *ecstatic communion* with the Divine, the *body* at last follows into that *communion*.
