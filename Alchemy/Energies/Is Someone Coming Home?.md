_Here in the frore days falling to the hand To ease the pain of final seasons
With fierce believing and dramas bequeathed By fear and the endless multiplication of error You screech in a gale
Riding the night of naming hours
You bellow and howl with each pulse of your doing Your torment is complete.

_Alone in the terrible waxing storm
You gather the shards of whimpering days And you name them by numbers alone
You reap the comfort of a small appellation Your sorrow is complete.

_O! Your promise has passed alone, wasted Drunk by the hand wet with slender courage And all of the mansions of the breathless sea
Hold in their intoning dark-sea murmur The drowning echo singing pain only
Your weeping is done.
I see you in a streak in the storm
Clasping in despair the sticky clay of creation And the frailty for such error
Brings the dark that dims your being The telling is done.
O see! Your numbers are names
You throw them in the counting hour.
There may yet be time
To stay the feast of your frailty upon last flesh There may yet be time
To grasp the hand that holds forth before the day.
The pleading is done
Is someone coming home?
