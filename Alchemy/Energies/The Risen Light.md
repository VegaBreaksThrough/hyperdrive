_And now, once again I appear on the shore The edge of the dream twixt teeth and tail
Where the glazed saying sand echoes farewell As the teething tide mounts to carry you away.
And these feet that stroll beside you in this lastness Rise beyond this shore
And fall breathlessly in the depths of Grace.


_The dark shaping having risen Departs from whence it came
And the glazed shore shaping eyes Descending the harrowing night
Casts not a flicker to the simple dawn that rises here.


_For now, the moment comes illumined With the joyful presence of the Beloved.
And all that arises here
Departs in the easy joy of its coming
And I am not that which knows to where it goes Or its rising place
For I am that which is blessed
In the Grace of simple unknowing But you in the shaping time
Gather your morn to your day And stand there aghast
As suffering descends to darken the rim of your eye And from there you look
Out along the bleakness of the shaping shore Locked into long walks with despair
Arising from out the sounding ground.


_Yet, all the forms that spring from your darkened eyes Quit with strength
Lead you to the mysterious plains of your dying That rise above my shore
And all the tumultuous storms in a tea cup Only madden the longing for your dying.
Harken! The Beloved is nigh
And he comes to gather in your terrible night To His glorious morn always rising
And He is already there
Always already that tranquil morn
Always rising beyond your shaping shore.


_Hush! The Beloved is always already here
When the sounding ground echoes you no more.
When the grounding sound
And the sounding ground departs the rising eye And Glory is the Illumined Light of the Beloved That fills the moving Being.

_And see then, and understand
Understand those beings abound in the untold joy Of the singing Light
Who in their Grace, lend their sight, to your night And gather your dawn with the Risen Light._
