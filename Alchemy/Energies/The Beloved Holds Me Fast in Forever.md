O! I sail my great ship of waves rushing ever sunward 
Horizons churning gold splashes solidly over my bow 
Setting these hour glass decks Awash with ticks and tocks.

Time is running away astern bubbling and boiling 
In a frenzied frothing of much hail and farewell. 
The vast reaches stretch languidly before me now

Beckoning and belying my great thundering Heart’s 
Mounting fierceness of wondrous and glowing intent.

Stars crash and spin through my eyes filling 
My bellowing sails with a furious pulse.

My destination is out there already feeling my touch. 
It is out there awaiting my arrival with trepidation 
As I spill ever onward in a maddening gale plying 
The mighty pressing waves with an ease of my reach.

I am heading, pulsing atomically, For the longing shore.

O! My shore waits for me out there, trembling

With growing wondrous Heart-stopping expectation.

This arrival will shake the sleeping being 
To the core of its

Dreaming, awakening bolt upright, Dazed and unknowing.

When I come riding in a crash up the waiting shore 
The Beloved will rush and hold me fast, forever 
Beyond any reach,

In the Eternity of His fierce embrace.
