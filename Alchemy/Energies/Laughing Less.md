Hanging here somewhere, somewhen Laughing less

No tears belong to me 
No heart-ache is 
greater
Than a world clinging to the heartless 
An angular hollow of absolute zero 
Laughing less, no tears are mine
All this cramp passes by and through 
Its terrible swell ruins my heart
And still I must stay for this witness 
Laughing less, no tears are mine

**
