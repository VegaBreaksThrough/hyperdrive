The sulk of the ongoing _oedipal conflict_ is hardly ever resolved throughout our adult lives, such as the times of feeling petulantly hurt when not rightfully acknowledged; a resort to earlier adaptations found to return again and again beyond our apparent control.

The principal form is therefore the withdrawal of _love_ and the reaction of retaliation. This _energy_ shows us the form of the conflict and moves us to Heal beyond such a reaction to the life process.

The _oedipal_ reaction forms in the very early life of the individual in response to the perceived unavailability of love. This reverberation goes on to often severely cripple us as functional adult human beings. _Real_ growth depends on the resolution and/or transcendence of the withdrawal/retaliation conflict.
