There are all manner of signs about most of us that fear is the principal motivation of our life; our anxieties, our emotional reactions, our [[Subtle Body]] communications, our lack of radiance and so on; the list is endless. What is the source of fear? What are we afraid of? Somehow we have become imbued with a cosmology based upon the mere appearance of ‘things’. The mere appearance of ‘things’ is also the basis of the heuristics of science. Since the view of science is ubiquitous as the underpinning of the social structure, it is easy for our own position to be reinforced as a casual acceptance. So, what is this cosmology? The primary acceptance is that there are all these ‘things’, and that there are ‘living things’ and ‘nonliving things’ and some aspects that appear to be associated with both categories. All these ‘things’ appear to be in a great container called the ‘universe’, which according to some is finite but unbounded. The nature of a thing is that it can be discerned to be separate from all other apparent ‘things’. In the view of ‘things’ we see that we ourselves are a separate ‘something’, namely this ‘fleshy structure’ and the ‘destiny’ of all ‘fleshy things’, though we desperately try to avoid thinking about it, is not at all enthralling, since non-survival is its prime possibility and we are thoroughly implicated by this ‘possibility’ since we are very likely ‘inside’ this structure, or worse, that we are simply this flesh form and when it is gone, we are gone, the dear departed. Poof! No more! 
Is our actual position something more than what appears to be? The fact is that none of us are absolutely convinced by the ‘when you’re dead, you’re dead’ theory and we are informed of this by our intuition. Through our intuition we see that we are actually always happy, always free of the destiny of the body. If there was no intuition beyond the mere appearance of things we would be left with an intolerable [[Actuality]] of an entrapment that would drive us instantly insane. Most of us are not raving mad, indicating that we do intuit something greater than mere fleshy deadends. However, we do not allow the magnification of this intuition to the point that we are able to bring the enjoyment of the intuitive position to the moment to moment matters of our living and so we act as though there was nothing greater than the appearance of things. We act as though we are unhappy, as though ‘when you’re dead, you’re dead’ is true, we presume we are all this limitation and through this identification with the body, it is clearly seen that bodies die and so we presume an identification with that which dies. From this point we begin to try to believe something that can create a hedge around this fear. Yet no ‘belief’ about Jesus or anything else can actually survive the death process that we are afraid of, and what we tried to hide behind the hedge pops up to terrorise us in life and through death. Mere belief cannot carry us through the radical process of death.
Our weak intuition is apparently no match for all the arguments of the scientists and their clones (which most of us are), and these arguments, when we embody them in our fear, makes naysayers of us all; we are effectively against life and this is shown in the obviousness of our totally diseased condition of nervous, twitchy paranoia, which is a perfect reflection of our negation. 
Is there a way beyond all of this? Should we even consider such a way might exist? Herein lies a difficulty for most of us, which is that we demand proof in the form of the scientific model, so even if a way beyond the presumed limitation exists we can’t see it until we can see it, taste it, touch it and so on. What we are asking for is that which is intuited to be beyond and greater than the world as it appears to be, apprehended by and through bodily experience. Yet bodily experience itself is a ‘closed system’ created by the brain as a limiter, which ordinarily reveals nothing beyond itself, even if it is expanded into subtle experiences. The brain is a limiter, it is the rules of limitation that make this ‘earthly vision’ possible. Scientists believe that a bigger brain will reveal more, but most likely it would only hide more; create more rules of limitation.
What an apparent dilemma! There would seem to be no way out, we would like to be happy, we would like to be not just plugging along with the body train, but this mere liking for all of this to be different does not carry with it any force for real change. All is not hopeless; there is still this matter of intuition, which if we could totally trust, would carry us beyond all this dilemma, because intuition is a revelation of that which is beyond the mere appearance of things. There is also help in this place which is in the form of the Divine presence, which our intuition can enable us to see in our discernment to be all that exists, or, if in the form of a realiser, we must be sure that we are not merely hopeful in our fear, or else we may be deluded and grasping at that which is less than love and therefore not such a presence. This may seem to be a difficulty in itself for those lost in the ‘doubting world’. However, true apprehension of the Divine presence is truly self-authenticating and cannot, at last, be mistaken for the counterfeit ‘realisers’ of the ‘spiritual supermarket’, we simply know and we know that we know. The Divine presence is signed by its power to work real change in our bodily and worldly circumstance. We may continue to doubt even when that presence is right in front of us, because our doubt prevents us from seeing the miraculous event of our usual existence. It is up to us to resolve this in our own heart in order that we can stand in and as the radiance of the living Divine, where the doubt of love is cast aside forever, as Only God stands out as the source of our existence and real life. This resolution comes upon us suddenly when we see that the force of reality is truly undeniable and the flimsy arguments of our defence of our suffering are pierced like a bubble, where a moment before stood a great wall of dark impenetrable shadow. The light of consciousness casts no shadow and shadow has no place within this domain.
