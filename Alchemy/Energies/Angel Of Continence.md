In a second your attention is possessed by the activity of your [[Nous|mind]] that seeks and does not find where peace rests, stubbornly seeks answers to what it does not understand and in the pain of its restlessness, the appetite of its desire is insatiable, incontinence drags the unconscious individual to spill its energy unnecessarily. 

The Angel Of Continence awakens the intelligence to operate and navigate the phenomena that arise in our relationship with life, it provides the temperance that is necessary to agency and embody the creative flow of the changing cycles. It is not about holding back energy, as constipation collapses the natural cycle of flow, it is about maintaining a relaxed flow that frees and drives ideas to fruition. Continence comes with the sweet and true mastery of reason. 

**Continence** may refer to:

- Fecal continence, the ability to control defecation;
- Urinary continence, the ability to control urination, the involuntary excretion of urine;
- Sexual continence, a synonym of [Coitus reservatus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coitus_reservatus "Coitus reservatus")
- [Sexual abstinence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_abstinence "Sexual abstinence")

- [Incontinence (philosophy)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incontinence_(philosophy) "Incontinence (philosophy)"), a lack of self-control (Greek: ἀκρασία - Akrasia)
	- "lacking command" or "weakness", occasionally transliterated as **acrasia** or Anglicised as **acrasy** or **acracy**) is a lack of mental strength or willpower, or the tendency to act against one's better judgment.
	- It is sometimes translated into English as **incontinence** ("a want of continence or self-restraint"). Beginning with [Plato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plato "Plato"), a variety of philosophers have attempted to determine whether or not akrasia exists and how best to define it.
