_The_ _Bottomless_ _Pit_ is mentioned in the book of _Revelations_ as a horror beyond all horrors and perhaps this _Bottomless_ _Pit_ is actually the same one referred to in _Revelations,_ however the inspiration for the _water,_ _The_ _Bottomless_ _Pit_ are those individuals who after considerable help by *Infinity*, still show an intractability with respect to their fundamental clinging to their structures of fear. Therefore it is seen that we are throwing all of this help into a _Bottomless_ _Pit_ of unyielding despair. This _drink_ therefore is *Infinity* 's last ditch stand for the failed case held over in perpetuity.
