Regions, people and cultures are the quaint hangovers from a madness that obviously began a long time ago. Now all these remnants of ‘ancient madness’ can only count on their tourist value for their raison d’être. Why are these cultural differences just a madness? Because they are simply the [[A0017|animation]] of a particular set of characteristics that only have sympathetic value and no intrinsic value. Our sympathetic tendencies are reinforced by our insertion into our culture of destiny. As such there is, in terms of the characteristics, no point to the preservation of either the ancient madness or anything of the present madness, since there is no Truth to be found anywhere. In the recent past when the different cultures enjoyed almost total isolation, what occurred could at best be called ‘superstitious’ or ‘neurotic’ and at worst psychotic in the forms of cannibalism or worse, the ‘enemy’ was paramount in the [[A0017|animation]] of the psychoses. The different regions in their isolation could be regarded as relatively discrete realms, where a particular array of influences held sway. What determined our arrival in any given realm as a matter of birth? Coming into association with any realm purely depends upon what type of madness our psyche is fitted to and our psyche is generated out of what has come to fascinate us as a matter of desiring and what fascinates is a matter of an act of attention, where attention is fixated, relatively unshakably, upon some point within the ‘bodily structure’. The ‘bodily structure’ is the entire pattern of relations and where they have their being. Through this fixated attention we are helplessly driven into the [[A0017|animation]] of fixed characteristics. This then is the foundation of cultures. In causal space it would seem that we are staring at some point within a cube and as we stare, it slices into different planes, one of which is this ‘earth plane’ or realm. 
So cultural differences are not cute and touristy, but merely the signs of a present and ancient madness. A true culture could still exhibit great variations, perhaps more than what can be seen at present, but a true culture would be free of madness. The present cultures are a matter of stark raving lunacy, because they have no basis in Truth, which is to say, they are adrift within a false view of themselves and this place and further none of these cultures have any understanding of just how mad everything really is, everyone is insensitive to the ‘unbounded pain’ of this place. 
There is at present no true culture and the odds against tomorrow giving rise to such an event are long indeed. However, these improbabilities aside, a true culture begins with us, allowing ourselves to feel the ‘pain’ of all our present activities. This ‘allowing of pain’ can only come about by awakening to a level of trust of that which lies beyond this world and therefore beyond our madness. We must lose our taste for this shocking place at every level of its fascination for us, right down to perception and the heartbeat. The essence of awakening from madness into the pure and sweet light of understanding is to love God more than we are fascinated with this realm, this ‘body structure’, this self. Without such a love we will not be able to tolerate the demand of being burnt up in the fire of transformative consciousness, which is the basis of real culture and therefore real life. So, we must yield up all the mad characteristics we tend to animate (such animation is what is called our ‘personality’) and be free of all such personality now and forever more. The true form of self and culture is to be found ‘floating freely’ in the enlightened condition of Only God, granted at last every Blessing and Wonder by our Great and Exceedingly Glorious God.
