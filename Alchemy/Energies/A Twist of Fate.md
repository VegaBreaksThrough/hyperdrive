Like Oliver, many aspects of our existence would like to be able to say, "Can I have some more?" Many causes exist for poor distribution of all of life's needs. Apart from the essential needs there also exist the unwanted aspects that suffer from a less than pleasing supply.
'Twists of fate' can lead to our failure to conduct the life-force. Probably the principal cause of the distribution failure is that unconscious functions arise as a 'field of tensions' in the usual person rather than simultaneously.
Simultaneity occurs only by abiding as the epitome of the body The Heart of Truth, or Real Consciousness intuited on the right side of the Heart. Neither the head nor the emotional heart is the epitome of the body.
Quite so.
