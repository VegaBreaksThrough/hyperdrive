### [[The Will Of God Is The Obligation To Love]] - Jessa O' My Heart

“Me and my shadow all alone and feeling blue.” A shadow is formed when something obscures the light; there is the umbra or deeply dark area and penumbra or grey area. In this world evil, or that which is against life and love, is a very dark shadow, perhaps it could be said that it is a dark wall encompassing everything here. The shadow world, the dark wall, grows in reaction to our living as un-love and therefore, since it encircles us, we are all but totally defined as a creature bound helplessly to the dramatisation of un-love. In this situation where darkness falls, everything endlessly degrades unless a transforming presence appears in the midst of this hellishness. The soul in the grip of the shadow does not even see the possibility or need for release; there is a numb acceptance of the deteriorating conditions as the cesspool reaches the height of the nose. Thus, it is clearly necessary in these times that God must come to man and begin to dispel the dark that has over many years encroached on this place. The [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] is one aspect of bringing the Divine in to restore the conditions of real life, wherein we can begin to feel again and to be inspired to be ‘saved’ and then ‘released’ beyond this condition, which itself is also to be restored to a much lighter circumstance.

### Carl Jung
 - **Definition**: The Shadow represents the parts of the self that are repressed[^1], denied, or disowned. It embodies the darker, unconscious aspects of the personality.
- **Integration**: Jung believed that acknowledging and integrating the Shadow is essential for personal growth and self-awareness.
- "Unfortunately there can be no doubt that man is, on the whole, less good than he imagines himself or wants to be. Everyone carries a shadow, and the less it is embodied in the individual's conscious life, the blacker and denser it is. If an inferiority is conscious, one always has a chance to correct it. Furthermore, it is constantly in contact with other interests, so that it is continually subjected to modifications. But if it is repressed and isolated from consciousness, it never gets corrected.
- It forms through societal norms, personal experiences and repressions. Some things are thought, to be unacceptable and are this repressed.
- The personal shadow: it has an emotional content to it.
- when we do not want to assimilate what we despise, we project it unto others. And when we refuse to recognise our shadow, our ego is unaware of it. Making the shadow autonomous, without any possibility of commanding it.
- the fantasies that we see within are facts with a tangible effect/outcome. Fantasy is not nothing.
- "The shadow is not necessarily always an opponent. In fact, he is exactly like any human being with whom one has to get along, sometimes by giving in, sometimes by resisting, sometimes by giving love whatever the situation requires. The shadow becomes hostile only when he is ignored or misunderstood."
- it is just a part of me that needs understanding and compassion.
- The tendency to see one's shadow out there in a person or a group is the more dangerous aspect of the modern psyche.

### Shadow Works
1. Write down the strong emotional reactions and notice the patterns. Identify the triggers.
2. Our shadow is in our dreams too.
3. We need to intimately know our shadow.
4. Analysis of dreams reveals the unconscious.
5. Realization of the Shadow: observe our shadow outwardly by watching our emotional reactions and being honest about our relationships with others. Observe it inwardly by exploring dreams.
6. We reduce the adverse effect of the Shadow not by waging war, but by bringing darkness to the light and the light to darkness. The chemistry of enlightenment.
7. Truth brings relief. That is the process of authenticity.
8. Practice being radically honest with yourself. Look for synchronicities that provide insights.

### The Collective Shadow
- Inflicts Massive harm
- Denies responsibility.
- emerges through genocides, death, etc...
- it hides behind "noble" motivations
- outwardly shows in poverty, I'll health and crime in society.
- inwardly, it fosters self-hatred depression and a desire for revenge, causing others similar suffering.
- collective shadow is often assimilated to "evil"
- The courage with which we bear our darkness frees others from having to carry it for us.
- Denial is usually the first reflex when facing our responsibilities in the past.
- the only way to deal with the collective shadow is to face our personal shadow facing challenging self-examination. It uncovers our positive traits and keeps us aware of our unconscious tendencies, emotions, dreams...
- there are things in the shadow that I do not produce, but that produce themselves  and have their own life.
