I am coming to Know what it is I Love.

I am coming to See

The great tide of the Beloved always rising.

I am coming to Know

The rounding out of my tangled whimper.

I am coming to See

The Light of all in the eyes of my small friend.

I am coming to Know

The Beloved standing free before the life of all.

I am coming to See

His wondrous moving Light unfolding in my Heart.

I am coming to Know

The impeccable Glory of His raining Grace.

I am coming to See

And take my leave of all domains less than brightness.

I am coming to Know

His great flood of joy in my artless surrender.

I am coming to See

The resonant secret fire of the Beloved in the being.

I am coming to Know

And say farewell to all that says farewell to the Heart.

I am coming to See

The Home of home in the light of the Lord of Grace.

I am coming to Know

And gather the all in the secret Heart of Love.

I am coming to See

The always already presence of the tearful Beloved I am coming to Know what it is I Love.
