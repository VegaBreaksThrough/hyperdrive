The [[Alchymie|hermetic]] touch is a reminder, lifting us beyond some level of complication. In fact wherever this touch appears, in whatever form, it is about remembrance, not seeking in some other place (clearly remembrance does not move us to seek, since seeking is the dramatisation of some felt negativity, a consequence of felt separation), but to at last awaken us in place (whatever hole we may find ourselves in) in this world. Remembrance is the discovery that there is no separation anywhere and this allows us to rest in the undifferentiated wholeness that is reality that is consciousness. Truly, whatever the [[Alchymie|hermetic]] touch is, it is always already the unmodified wholeness that is the mystery of existence.

---
*Touch* is *whole body* involvement: It involves everything. Hermetic is holistic, referring to the *alchymeic* transformation through imbuement of the *Touch* \- *whole body* engagement in and as *reality*. It is not a ‘something’. It is the flash of gold in the pan. It is Eureka\! It is the excitement of life rushing through our veins after we have been numbed. It is God *power* that awakens the sleeping beast. It is our response to, as well as the Presence Itself, of the Divine. Our true response demonstrates our love of God more than *self*. Hence the *hermetic touch* is a prayer as well as the Eureka\!

The *hermetic touch* penetrates the illusion of separation of the *mind*. It is not possible to make this ‘clear’ to the *mind*. It is our intuited and felt response. We can’t wait upon understanding, clarity or wisdom: We must simply be that response.

The *hermetic touch* is the entrance of the Divine into the ordinary situation.
