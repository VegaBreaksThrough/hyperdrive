The “Holomovement” is a term coined by **David Bohm**, a physicist and philosopher, to describe his interpretation of quantum mechanics and the nature of reality. It refers to the idea that the universe is a dynamic, interconnected whole, where everything is in a state of constant flow and transformation.

1. **Key Features**:

• The **implicate order**: A hidden, underlying reality where everything is enfolded.

• The **explicate order**: The unfolded, observable universe we experience.

• The **holomovement** is the ongoing dynamic interplay between these two orders, creating the universe as we know it.

2. **Holographic Metaphor**:

• Bohm likened this process to a hologram, where every part of the hologram contains the whole. Similarly, every part of the universe reflects the whole.

• It emphasizes interconnectedness, suggesting that separateness is an illusion.

3. **Implications**:

• Influenced thinking in physics, philosophy, and even consciousness studies.

• Highlights the idea that all phenomena, including mind and matter, emerge from the same underlying reality.
