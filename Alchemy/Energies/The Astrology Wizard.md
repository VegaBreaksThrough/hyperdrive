**The Astrology Teacher** refers to the influence of the planets and constellations upon an individual. The **brain-mind** is the unextended form; the world at large—the **cosmos**—is the extended form. The extended form reflects the unextended. Astrology reveals a **map of the cosmic influence** on an individual, based on the unique circumstances of their birth.

  

For those who recognize the **veracity of astrology**, they are witnessing that the **inner becomes outer**, and the **outer influences the inner**. As Hermes Trismegistus states: _“As above, so below.”_

  

The **purest form of astrological practice**, symbolized by the Astrology Wizard, seeks to **dissolve all destinies written in the stars**, which are reflections of the brain-mind self—the **psyche**. Thus, the psyche is the **machine of destiny**, and the stars form the **four-dimensional pattern** of that psyche.

### **The Main Elements of a Birth Chart**

Taking my chart as an example
  
![[Screenshot 2025-04-18 at 8.48.33 PM.png]]

**The 12 Zodiac Signs**

- They are identified on the outer part of the circle, one after the other, in a counterclockwise order (the first is Aries).
    
- Each sign covers 30°.
    



**The Planets**

- Inside the circle, each planet is marked with coordinates from 1 to 30 (a low number = early in the sign, a high number = later in the sign).
    
- Each planet is located in a sign and also in a house.
    
- Exact planet positions are shown in a box at the top left.
    
    - This allows you to see the exact positions of planets within signs, as well as their symbols.
        
    - Example: Sun: 4 Leo 46’ 44” (4 = degree, Leo = sign, 46’ 44” = minutes and seconds for precision).
        
    - If there is an “r” at the end, the planet is retrograde (which slightly changes its meaning).
        
    - The bottom of the box shows the exact positions of the Ascendant, Midheaven, and some houses.
        
    

  

**The Houses**

- Numbered at the center of the circle.
    
- The starting point is the horizontal axis of the chart, which represents the Ascendant (AC)/Descendant (DC) axis.
    
    - The Ascendant or “rising” sign is the sign on the horizon at the moment of birth (where the sun was rising).
        
    - The degree of the Ascendant is the starting point for house placement — starting from the 1st house in a counterclockwise direction, totaling 12 houses.
        
    
- House placement is independent of the signs, so a house usually spans 2 signs.
    
- The Midheaven (MC) is halfway between the Ascendant and Descendant.
    
- Each house represents a different life domain or may refer to specific people.
    

  

**The Aspects**

- Represent angles between planets (bottom left section).
    
- The angles planets form with one another have specific impacts and meanings.
    

  

**Major aspects:**

- Tension: square (90°), opposition (180°)
    
- Harmony and support: sextile (60°), trine (120°), conjunction (0°)
    

  

**Minor aspects:**

- Less influential (e.g., semi-sextile)
    

  

**Elements and Modalities**

- Represent further qualities of the signs (small box to the left-center).
    
- Each sign has an element:
    
    - Air (orange), Water (blue), Fire (red), Earth (green)
        
    
- Each sign has a modality (Cardinal, Fixed, Mutable)
    
- A planet takes on the element and modality of the sign it is in.
    
- The chart lets you quickly see if a pattern or imbalance exists.

### **Summary**

- **Planets** = Basic drives, needs, and energies.
    
- **Signs** = How these forces express.
    
- **Houses** = Where they operate in life (career, money, health, etc.).

### **The Signs**

The signs are divisions of the celestial ecliptic, the ribbon of stars where the Sun, Moon, and planets are located. The signs indicate how planets express themselves, while the houses show the areas of experience where these energies are likely to operate.

  

The signs can be grouped according to different characteristics, alternating from one sign to the next in each category.

- **Signs = how** planets express themselves.
    
- **Houses = where** these energies operate.
    

  

**Signs can be grouped by:**

  

**Polarity**

- **Positive (Masculine, Yang):** extroverted, energetic, objective, assertive
    
- **Negative (Feminine, Yin):** introverted, subjective, receptive, nurturing
    

  

**Modality**

- Linked to how one interacts with the world (operating mode)
    
- **Cardinal:** natural leaders, initiators, make things happen
    
- **Fixed:** focused, persistent, firm
    
- **Mutable:** adaptable, flexible, resilient
    

  

**Elements**

- **[[Fire]]:** creative force, vitality, courage, restlessness
    
- **[[Earth]]:** practical, grounded, productive, attuned to reality
    
- **[[Air]]:** intellectual, curious, sociable, mentally active
    
- **[[Water]]:** emotional, intuitive, empathetic, vulnerable
