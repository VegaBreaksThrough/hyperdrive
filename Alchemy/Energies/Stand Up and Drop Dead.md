Normally we are focused on the limitations of the [[Frontal Line]] personality. In fact, without consciousness, we are puppets dangling on the strings of our own structure, with an enforced concentration on those energies that are below the brow. Without consciousness we are simply prone to be, all day, whatever we find ourselves to be upon waking. The undisciplined attention looks down into the complications of the [[Frontal Line]] and is locked into this design and therefore feeds this design, this limitation, with energy. The admonition to ‘stand up and drop dead’ is the demand for consciousness and therefore release from meditation upon the limitation of the [[Frontal Line]] personality. If we live in the disposition of one who is already dead, then we do not suffer any limitation or implication of the one who lives as the one who must die. Therefore most certainly, most happily, most freely and mostly more and not mostly less, ‘stand up and drop dead’.
