Ayahuasca comes from South American culture, where the plant itself is used to elevate consciousness to an expanded state. This allows for a greater capacity to see beyond the closed structure of the mind, which is a particular part of the dark spaces existing in this world. Elevating consciousness or expanding it is the purpose of the plant. Thus, this energy specifically refers to the ability to allow expanded consciousness, to be a penetrating force, enabling greater intelligence, rather than the “nut” of limited conventional form.
