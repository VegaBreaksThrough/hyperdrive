No matter what our present level of understanding is, it is simply and obviously true that there is Only God. 
This is a radical statement since it follows that there is nothing else. What appears to be is merely the projection of our human consciousness and this includes the good and the bad in terms of our welfare. It follows that we cannot blame God for anything that appears to happen to us, since nothing is (rightly speaking) happening to us, but is rather, a gracious gift from the Beloved, even if that happens to involve death. This note comes out of infinity and, as such, is the blessing regard of the Divine. Understand or not would appear to be a choice since this too is from the Beloved.
