The constellation of Orion includes the greater nebula and the lesser nebula, and this energy is felt to be about evolving the heart of consciousness, and its extension above, the brain-mind. When we feel into the energy of dolphins and other mammals of the sea, we are immediately touched by their joyous freedom and seeming humour and their often-friendly overtures in relation to us. The dolphin seems to reflect much of the energy of the Orion constellation and clearly this energy is about evolving us beyond preoccupation with the waking state and the subtle state into the very heart of consciousness. The dolphins themselves are f ixed forms and do not suffer or enjoy all the possible states of humanity. The human being can know the states of the ‘hell’ worlds right through into the ultimate Divine states of cosmic possibility. What the dolphin’s energy reminds us to be is responsible for energy and feeling. In being restored to humour we are free of all the dreadful implication of this worldly embodiment as conscious understanding and not mere gleefulness, which is an unconscious activity of trying to exclude the actual nature of this world by producing a sanitised version.
