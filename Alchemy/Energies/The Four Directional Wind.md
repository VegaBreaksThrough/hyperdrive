Some of this congestion is the result of the contraction of brain-mind as unabated thinking. In the death process our attention is released through the top of the head and this is difficult for the congested individual and hence a [[The Madness Of Dissolution|Madness Of Dissolution]] occurs at this point.

The [[South Wind]] is feeling enclosed in a body, enclosed in a world, enclosed in unconsciousness. The South Wind vial moves to liberate us from this enclosure, bringing consciousness to bodily life, turning us from having our heads buried in the south to standing upright as a conscious presentation.

The [[East Wind]] is a renewing, enlivening and rejuvenating energy. It blows from the east, thus going against the rotation of the earth and this rotation cycles us daily toward death. Therefore in the east is death, decay and orgasm, since orgasm exhausts toward death. The east wind blows counter to all death and decay.

The [[West Wind]] is madness, confusion, chaos and daemons and the usual west wind carries all this disturbance with it. The west wind vial has been reversed into a *Healing energy* that dispels all madness and fear and so on.
