Feeling as though we have the weight of the world on our shoulders, being a mere beast of burden, is a reflection of the egoic disposition wherein we feel “we are the doer”, “we are the actor’”, full of anxieties and despair, surrounded by endless emergencies. This obviously is an atheistic position, lost latently in betrayal of all relations. This position of angst is simply not the Truth for it has failed to awaken to grace, wherein all matters are always already achieved in the Divine. ‘The three minute googy’ reflects our anxiety as we are impossibly squeezed through a three-minute hourglass egg timer, which is more or less the position of the usual person. To awaken from the feeling of being forced through an egg timer is to awaken to the understanding that we are simply alive as that which is merely observing all of these ‘goings on’ as they arise to understanding.
