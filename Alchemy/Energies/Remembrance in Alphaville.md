Conventional Life is the Dramatisation of Forgetfulness

Our association with this vision, this so-called earthly life, is about forgetfulness and so this life is the dramatisation of having forgotten. What we have forgotten here is the Truth of us: Our real life. In lieu of remembrance we seek knowledge, as though knowledge could actually be the equivalent of Truth, but knowledge does not enter us upon existence or real life. We cannot remember this matter of our real life without a connection to Truth, and this is that to which we must continually resort, at last, moment to moment, now and forever more. This connection to Truth is discovered as a matter of Divine intuition. We must realise there is no level of resort to self-effort that can amount to remembrance. It needs to be revealed to us that there is the necessity for us to yield to that which is greater and beyond us. Awakening to the grace of the Divine is our yielding at last and our abiding in that remembrance that is our real life. This is the transcendence beyond the dramatisation of forgetfulness that is the realisation of the dead ends of the world only - the conventional destiny. In our availability to the grace of the Divine we are truly resurrected beyond the death that is ours by our so-called ‘birth’ into this world; into our only true birth; as the present and always immortal form of Only God.
