Some people are very, very, very, slippery, and this is the form of their non-receptivity. They are somehow able to dance around some Vial or energy they are ostensibly willing to receive, but effectively are refusing. The Chess Players Mirrors are an enigmatic prize, captured in an inter-galactic war. Originally employed to obfuscate, they are used effectively to hold someone in place, in order to render them more receptive. "[[Alice]] is very slippery, wham her with the Chess Players Mirrors."
