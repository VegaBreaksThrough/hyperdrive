1. ### (SAGITTARIUS, THE ARCHER) FIRE FROM THE HEART OF KAUS AUSTRALIS (Epsilon)
    

This fire from the heart symbolizes the bow with the arrow, pointing towards the cusp of our existence, the path to fulfillment, the destiny of prosperity, the path to the goal, this fire shows us the right path, perfect, without failures, the whole path, the Divine Plan is written on this star.  
  

KEYWORD: The Way of Integrity.
