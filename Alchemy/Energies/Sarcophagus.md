Awakening from the dead. Horror movies are typically peopled by fearful zombie-like creatures that inexorably crawl their way into our nightmares. We may like to think that such creatures are only the stuff of movies and dreams. However the obscene denizens of the horror movie reflect the ‘deadness’ of the usual man or woman in our unenlightened condition. We are the sleepwalking zombie we fear. In visions arising during many healing encounters, often a corpse was discovered at the bodily base of the individual. This corpse is the dream of death that is the root of the personality in the conventional disposition. This ‘dream’ is the mind in the temporal structure of beginnings, middles and ends, with a special emphasis on ends: The enigmatic black hole of non-existence from the mind’s point of view. This ‘dream’ is reflected in the gross physical form, at least as a general malaise or as severe and crippling dysfunction. This dream of death also finds its way pervasively into the environment, where it sucks the life out of us until we die of exhaustion and toxicity. 
Our vast investment in the dream of death must be transformed, so that at last we can be prepared for an unimpeded ‘birth’ through the top of the head. A ‘birth’ through the top of the head may be surpassed for a few individuals who may enjoy total dissolution into the light, when the breath and heart beat cease. May God grant such a boon to all those who allow themselves to be ‘touched’ profoundly. God bless all.
