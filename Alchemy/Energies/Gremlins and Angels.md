The Spielberg ‘Gremlins’ are an adequate model of the common man, and both are ‘saintly challenged’. The common man is only clever at best and has no intelligence. Being merely clever is a hangover from our life in the tree tops, where being clever was probably a strong survival trait in the lawlessness of the natural order of things. The present day jungle has added levels to the natural jungle that do not ordinarily appear in the tree tops. Our weird present day jungle, also relying on cleverness, has added ascendancy and power, along with all the Machiavellian bag of underhand tricks. The clever do not have any restraint upon their behaviour, since winning the game is really all that matters and part of the game may also include keeping up good appearances, the public and private ‘face’. The present day world operates only on cleverness and what is clear for all to see is that the cleverness defiles all that it touches and trivialises human existence to the lowest levels of ‘dog eat dog’, “nothing personal, just business.” What is missing from the picture of the ‘clever gremlins’? Cleverness or the ability to learn and perform all manner of tricks has no feeling for the sacred, it is merely jumping through hoops.
The sacred, or in part that which is inviolate, is an evolutionary development beyond the cleverness of all the jungles. The sacred at the present time does not truly exist, because the world is merely clever and not yet up to sacred. What is called sacred, and perhaps inviolate in a certain sense, are simply small areas set aside where some ritual, said to be sacred, is performed. Mostly however, these rituals fail to have any Divine power that needs to be associated with the sacred. If we were truly capable of making anything sacred we would be inviting a power into this world that would tear the very fabric of this place apart. The world at large has no capability for such a thing, since ‘clever gremlins’ are simply fascinated with merely knowing about, not actually ‘being’; the ‘gremlins’ know stuff, a posteriori. If we awaken from this fascination with knowing into the intelligence that is God and God alone, i.e. to be intelligent is to be ‘alive’ as the God presence; no other thing is that intelligence. 
Now we can see why intelligence is scarce and only cleverness prevails. The appearance of intelligence is a vast development upon the self-conscious cleverness of competition and survival of the animal kingdom. Such a change is so great that only a handful of individuals throughout history have been able to demonstrate such very conscious intelligence. So let us be humbled by this. Give up these games of: “Look at me, I’m the king of the castle.” Be humbled and give up all our false claims to the sacred, where actually we are only involved in the profane. To be truly intelligent is a very great matter: it is to be the presence of Only God, alive as the present immortal. When this is the case (a billion or so years from now) across the world, we could say that this place is ‘peopled’ by angels who have the capacity to make everything sacred, thereby lifting all of this natural order, based in the motivations of fear, into the delight of the Sacred Kingdom. Such a transformation of ‘gremlin’ to ‘angel’ is for now perhaps a ‘long way off’ for the angelically challenged gremlin humanity. The present day ‘gremlin mind’ is totally incapable of grasping such a change, which is far beyond childhood’s end, far beyond the end of the ‘rainbow’ in a world where ‘we’ as angels walk this ‘earthly plane’ as the sacred footsteps of the living Divine. In a billion years or so.
