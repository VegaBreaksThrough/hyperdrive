Many of us spend our hard earned obtaining predictions of our future circumstance from astrologers, tarot readers and all manner of soothsayers. In fact, there are many who will not make a move in life without regular consultations with their favourite psychic predictor and indeed many prominent movers and shakers are the clientele of such ball-gazers.

We engage the services of those who _know_ what is the ‘shape’ of the way ahead so that we can supposedly avoid many pitfalls and invite only the fair sailing in light seas. Yet if these ‘readings’ are truly the sign of the ‘way ahead’ then this reading is essentially unavoidable. Whatever we end up doing, the reading itself is part of the ‘way ahead’ and in this way we may appear to be avoiding the dark destinies, but what we are thereby unerringly sailing into is, after all, only our destiny. With the psychic consultations, we are, with the consultant, merely being drawn into that destiny. All of this therefore is merely our destiny and if we can _know_ certain outlines of it then there can be no ‘escape’ through such knowledge. We are caught in the Cassandra dilemma because such knowledge does not enjoy the freedom of _[[Real Intelligence]]_.

All that is the future landscape of our movement does not essentially change its shape unless it is undermined through being made unnecessary. The future remains necessary as long as the principle of _self_ is continually the source of our indulgent motivations. The ‘shape’ of the future only dissolves or is transformed or is made unnecessary if the ‘shaper’ - that is the egoic _self_ - is no longer the centre of activity but is instead given over to _mindless embodiment_, wherein we transcend desire and, therefore, all manner of fixed destinies. _Mindless embodiment_ is the disposition of love, and so it follows that all ordinary destinies that could perhaps be a matter of prediction are overwhelmed by love, as indeed are all destinies that may be reflected in the stars, the tarot cards or the entrails of life. All destinies are made unnecessary as we are dissolved in the _reality_ that is love.

Those of us who awaken as the reality that is love are absolutely unconcerned about our future since such a future is always outlined in love, and therefore, that destiny is love; and such a destiny does not at last travel in darkness or with dark companions in the woe of crisis after crisis and the ultimate demise in the degradation of human existence. Such a one does not make the present moment merely a springboard into some future moment, but is altogether lost in the present moment of only love, _Only God_ and therefore lives only as present forms of enjoyment.
