_I saw in a late, leafy autumn afternoon As I was riding high in a flurry
And by at speed
A ragged woman in a splash of whitewash Changing the shape of a cottage of tumbles.
The gesture fell out of the blue to me As some other vision of splendidness.
For all around
And interminably it seemed A painter of soot and dark Found more favour.
My Heart leapt at a rush that way Amidst the glow of white
And ancient timbers To touch and bless
This unquenchable brightness.
_
