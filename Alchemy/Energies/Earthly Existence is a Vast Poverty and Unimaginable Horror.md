Those of us who are sprinkled around the world living some classy little life are perhaps wont to think as we gaze around upon all that we have, that life is wonderful and that no matter how great the present moment is, the promise of  heaven, as many of us are told, will be greater. In fact we have just recently been informed officially that there is no hell. Isn’t that a bonus? 
What is wrong with this picture? It’s a sitcom world wherein we are locked into a totally manipulated vision of ‘who we should be’ and ‘what is actually going on’ and what is going on reaches far beyond the mere social politics of, particularly, Westerners. Most Westerners choose the blinkers of a very parochial existence. It scarcely ever occurs to us that we are part of the greater mass of humanity. If it did so occur to us, immediately we would see that in no way can life be epitomised as wonderful. Yet beyond what we might see in the social chaos there is a much more sinister level to our unconscious existence. We are exactly in the position of a cow chewing grass somewhere who will shortly be reduced to so many hamburgers. The human race is, at our present level of functioning,  just so many farm animals. Is this news? The structure of the ‘human’ world is designed to prevent our development beyond mere animals for slaughter. Our political lives openly discourage involvement with that which may turn us beyond our vital existence within the consumer society, we simply must pursue the ‘latest and the greatest’ or die. It is even suggested that the pursuit of that which is truly human as is spoken of by any number of genuine spiritual teachers, is very likely dangerous or even evil. This is done obliquely, sometimes by trying to taint the genuine spiritual life through the suggestion that the ‘mad cults’ that pop up from time to time are simply part of the total stream of spiritual seekers and therefore ipso facto all spiritual seekers are associated with ‘mad cults’. Hence, parents around the world are suitably horrified when their children are ‘inveigled’ into some forbidden spiritual pursuit, yet no-one very much minds if their child is an ordinary sexmad, drugged-up, spaced-out, directionless dodo, since this fits the normal picture, so we can rest easy.
To this point all appears to be within the realm of the ordinary world of the news. Yet behind this political brocade, a subtle, more sinister, more powerful and largely unseen world of manipulation of our lives is revealed, at least to some of us. This class of control is manipulation of our ‘human’ structure to prevent development toward the Divine states of our real existence. The medical and pharmaceutical world are unwitting participants in our disfigurement. This is achieved through any number of ‘treatments’ which destroy the central nervous system at its most delicate level and the immunisation of children that prevents their possible higher development through certain alterations of the structure of the blood. It is through the agency of the central nervous system, particularly the forebrain, that the doorway to the higher states of existence, though not ultimate states, are awakened through real practice. It has been seen through accident victims and frontal lobotomies that the ordinary individual can be separated from their frontal brain without incurring any obvious loss of function, in fact we may just seem to be a much ‘nicer guy’. The conventional allopathic level of ‘health care’ is an open doorway for all the subtle horrors of the bottomless pit because of its one-dimensional reductionist view of human life. The allopathic medicine in its error works to reduce us to our most trivial level i.e. the allopathic meditation upon the error of reductionism produces ‘techniques’ which will prove their point when we are all effectively lobotomised at last. Beyond the allopathic world, most, if not all, structures of human existence, including popular culture, are open doorways to ‘hell’ and we are as balloonists whose balloon is failing to sustain us, as we sink ever deeper. Yet in our unconscious life as farm animals we are all the willing watchdogs of ‘hell’ as our usual lives perform the murder of everything with every fitful breath we draw and we ourselves are eaten as the spurious energy we constantly cast off in our endless suffering and like good little farm animals we may not even consider that we are suffering at all. Every aspect of the usual world gathers every vestige of energy that might have otherwise allowed the development of [[Real Intelligence]] and we are left as virtually brain-dead grinning fools ripe for the plucking.
Just for good measure, the New Age movement is largely the willing, arrogant and naive participant in grabbing the last of us who show some level of intuition toward Divine matters, to make certain that none of us can ever truly reach beyond our circumstance of ‘hell’. This is achieved by inviting demonic possessions that ostensibly offer ‘healing’ for the human race. This ‘healing’ is couched in a little glow of promise that hides the means of our disfigurement. Of course, we deserve all we get because we gladly seek the promise of fantastic powers and riches and immortality in the body and so on, so that in our egoic madness we can enjoy all the benefits of a superhero comic character. In our madness we all desire to be glamorized, forever worshipped as the one and only and the greatest and therefore we are easily beguiled into the promises of the tricksters of the bottomless pit where we crash like Icarus. We are easily lured because we are the hungry ghosts who are always focused in our greed of wanting, yet nothing that the tricksters offer is ever satisfying and we find ourselves bereft and wanting more. What appears to be a thirst quencher turns to hot sand in our mouths. We want in our presumed unhappiness and so we are led into the casual conventional life of self-indulgence, along with the popular culture of despair and degraded dead-ends.
The Truth gives us nothing in the usual sense, it is not about powers or any level of bodily experience. It is in no way circumscribed by bodily life. The Truth demands everything of us and this everything is what we feel is our life. But our life is a self-nature that does not exist, an illusion of horrors, a bardo journey of encountering the demons of our own creation, if not now then through death. The Truth does not appeal to those on the so-called path, since the Truth wants the path. When at last we can hack it, the Truth is simply the Truth and we must be absolutely divested of everything in order to awake from the dream of ‘hell’ into the glory of only love, Only God, wherein not a jot of self-nature exists and in fact it never did. The Truth is not a matter of becoming; it is our very being and can only be awakened to at last through our surrender to grace, now and forever more.
