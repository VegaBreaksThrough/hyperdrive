Most of us do not realise that the so-called life that we ‘have’ is a shaky edifice, built upon our fear of death, which we do not necessarily cognise as such. In our ‘untransformed’ state, all, but all, that we ‘have’ around us, including people, animals, things, places and the things that we do, is merely and only our fear of death projected into all these aspects of our living; our ‘life.’ In this ‘projection of death’ we are attempting to eliminate change, in the sense that change is the principle sign of the inevitability of ‘death’. The rotational energy of the earth is about this inevitability of ‘death’, each day gone is one day ‘nearer’. Of course, we don’t like to think about it or we have some cute little philosophy or some ‘hedge’ we keep in some back pocket. However, no matter what we think, there is nothing that we can do to stave off the inevitability of ‘death’ or the freak out of the death process. So we can think of every aspect of our so-called ‘lives’ as being a ‘monument’ to our ‘death’ and this ‘monument’ also includes our own ‘face’, which is how we see ‘ourselves’ and what we have managed to convince others that we ‘really’ are. Thus our ordinary lives, the ‘something’ that we have ‘dreamed up’ is simply a way of feeling that we have ‘eliminated death’ or at least feel a little more comfortable with it. In this way, what we are always meditating upon is ‘death’, no matter how ‘lively’ we may appear to be. It is simply true that if we have a life that life is about being a ‘hedge’ around our ‘fear of death’ and we have set ourselves up to be ‘ripped off’, since those “that have must lose”. So we ‘have’, in the sense that our ‘personality’ is grounded in all that only comes to an ‘end’, in what can radically alter or ‘die’, therefore we must ‘lose’. What we have failed to see is that ‘nothing’ can belong to some supposed ‘me’, not even the feeling that we have a ‘body’. Everything is simply the ‘body of God’ and we should always have been living in the recognition of this and this is not the equivalent of some ‘new agey cuteness’ that “God is in everything, isn’t that wonderful?” This recognition lies beyond what may be thought at the level of mind, into the life lived in grace, as the sacrifice of everything to the infinite presence of Only God, returning to God what always belonged to Him.
Now, when the force of the Divine enters the ‘circuitry’ of the life that we ‘have’, that life begins to fall apart, quite literally. The life is about ‘fear’ and so when the Divine enters, which is not about ‘fear’, the life begins to be brought to an end. If we only resist such a process, by trying to hang on to ‘something’ as it sails out the door, we will only feel disturbed, perhaps even very disturbed and what such clinging shows us is that such a ‘one’ is not truly committed to real life. What we see is that the more that we ‘cling’, the more we will be ‘ripped off’. The Divine does not apologise for this ‘rip off’, our life of ‘fear’ cannot in any way be supported by God, we cannot obtain that kind of ‘help’; “Please God I love you, but please may I keep all my stuff?” All the ‘stuff’ is about fear and if we resist giving up all the stuff, then we are resisting giving up our fear, which is refusing to love God more than self. If we continue to resist, we will receive the ‘smack of God’, in the form of the loss of everything, including some level of our ‘functional existence’, which may include what we call our ‘sanity.’ What we will not get away with is attempting to ‘scapegoat’ God for what is taking place, which is, after all, only the ‘creation’ of our resistance to awaken as love and therefore refusing to give up everything for that. What we ‘hold back’ will only become a source of pain for us. So turn to that which has no need of ‘having’ and let everything go, including wives, husbands and children; these are just the ‘chips’ God has lent us temporarily so we may ‘play’ the ‘game’ and so as the ‘wheel of life’ spins, don’t bet all your chips on ‘zero’.
