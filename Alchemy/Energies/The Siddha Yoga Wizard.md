"Siddha Yoga" ("perfect" or "perfected" yoga) is a Sanskrit term adopted by Muktananda to describe the path of [self-realization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-realization "Self-realization") that he embarked on under the guidance of his spiritual teacher, the Indian saint [Bhagawan Nityananda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhagawan_Nityananda "Bhagawan Nityananda"). Muktananda regarded the path he learned from his teacher as a perfect path because it embraced all of the traditional yogas ([jnana yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jnana_yoga "Jnana yoga"), [[Karma Yoga Wizard]], [raja yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raja_yoga "Raja yoga"), and [bhakti yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhakti_yoga "Bhakti yoga")), spontaneously bringing the disciple to perfection in each.

A definition of "Siddha Yoga" is also offered by Swami Shankar Purushottam Tirtha, a yogi from the dual Tirtha/Siddhayoga lineage, who wrote two books on "[Siddhayoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siddhayoga "Siddhayoga")" in the early 1900s:

The easy way of attaining salvation is said to be Siddhayoga... Siddhayoga or Siddhimarga is that means by which yoga can be attained without difficulty... Siddhayoga is attained by the infusion of spiritual force through the good grace of a saintly preceptor... Siddhayoga or Siddhimarga is nothing but the knowledge of the unity of Self and Brahma...[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siddha_Yoga#cite_note-FOOTNOTETirtha19902%E2%80%934-10)

A further definition of "Siddha Yoga" was offered in 1948 by Swami Purushottam Tirtha's disciple, Swami Vishnu Tirtha:

*"Therefore the [yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoga "Yoga") of [[Serpent Power]] is known as [Mahayoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahayoga "Mahayoga"). It is also sometimes called Siddhayoga because it can be acquired only through the favor of a perfect master (Siddha Guru), without any effort on the part of the initiated.
It has been said that through shaktipata (divine [[Grace]]), [[Serpent Power]] is soon awakened, and that Mahayoga or Siddhayoga is the direct outcome."
