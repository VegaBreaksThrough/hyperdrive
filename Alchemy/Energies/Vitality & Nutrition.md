(13) The Biodynamic Wizard [Wand]  
Biodynamics is a regenerative agriculture system with a holistic focus and practice, based on the work of Dr. Rudolf Steiner (1861-1925). This is a cultivation system based on preparing organic fertilizers that restore life force to the soil. Healthy, well-structured soil, rich in humus and high biological activity, is a prerequisite for any sustainable farming system. Unlike conventional agriculture, which adds water-soluble fertilizers to the soil, biodynamics takes an organic approach to bringing soil to life, allowing what is grown in the soil to be a living organism, full of life force. Thus, the inspiration of this wizard energy is to awaken our conscious participation in this process (beyond the degrading use of pesticides, insecticides, and herbicides), in which we assist in restoring the Earth plane and, in turn, receive an optimal level for nutrient production. Let this energy be an inspiration for positive ways of life in gardening.
