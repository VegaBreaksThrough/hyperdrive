Jegudiel is the guardian archangel of all who work in some field of endeavor and rewards for successful spiritual labors. He is the advisor and defender of all who work in positions of responsibility to the glory of God, and as such was traditionally resorted to by kings, judges and others in positions of leadership. Jegudiel is also known as the bearer of God’s merciful love.
