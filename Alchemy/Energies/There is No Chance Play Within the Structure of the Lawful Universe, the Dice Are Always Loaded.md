God does not play dice with the world. God is not in touch with this world except through the agency of those totally surrendered into the mystery of existence, or through those, therefore, in whom God has had His victory. What operates here is simply the law and the reactions of this lawful universe to living in false relationship to the law. Living, therefore, is not a game of chance, but of receiving [[Just desserts]] in every moment. Such [[Just desserts]] are the destiny of ordinary existence. There is no way to change any of this at the level of mere desire. What is a matter of ordinary destiny remains necessary until made unnecessary through a shift in the principle of existence in this place. The shift in principle is from identification with limitation and therefore survival as that limitation to the principle of identifying with the mystery of existence and abiding as that. Living as this latter principle, we are no longer generating the reactions of the lawful universe against some presumed centre that is ‘us’ by this presumption. From the point of view of the Mystery, there is no centre and no boundary and so we exist in a disposition of [[Real Observation]] or [[Real Intelligence]] and such a disposition undoes our bondage to limitation at last.
