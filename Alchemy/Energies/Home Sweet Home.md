This energy deals with the binding quality of simply being a householder, especially a householder with children. There is a phenomenology about the home that goes unnoticed, like the structure of a classroom exudes an ideology of education. Likewise the structure of the nuclear home is an articulated space that in its very nature is a bondage defining the 'forms' of relationship that can occur within that articulated space. The _Home_ _Sweet_ _Home_ *energy* aids us in transcending these structures of our relational existence which includes the energy and feeling status of those with whom we are associated. In this way it is possible with _Home_ _Sweet_ _Home_ to be in the world, but not of it, therefore we are able to move freely.
