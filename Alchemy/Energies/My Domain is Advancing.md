My Domain is advancing.

Barely noticed,

Certainly not seen in the click and clamour That crowds in upon the heart mercilessly Pounding in its pointedness

A grim face etched in hardening clay.

  
  

My Domain is a crowder

Moving inexorably and ever more quickly The inky clutter is helpless in my advance.

At the end of the tether, I am achingly Weary of the remorseless presence

Of the minions of some god of madness Wretching out from below the navel. My advance will crush this foulness

As easy as the demise of offending bugs.

  
  

This other side of my Domain of simple sweetness Moves powerfully into the compressing waves

Of the rippling plane, sucking up this disgusting Rock-face clawing heat, of the most unlovely.

My Domain holds them hard in their

Consequence of atrocious rock grinding thoughts.

My Domain is advancing, easily carving up This stench, this utter filth of soul

That sucks and blubbers into every corner Of this terrible crowded place the

Aching, twisting, wrecking hammer of the tragic.

The unlovely will not see me coming

They will not hear my tread or feel my sword cut deep.

They will simply and by their own gravity, be Sucked up into the tightest blip of hard reflection

That was ever conceived of In any realm of lawfulness.

  

As this zone of goodbye fastens hard and bites deep There will be much rejoicing as great choirs of angels Swell their song across the wide-eyed brightness

As my Domain of Sweetness and Light takes hold.
