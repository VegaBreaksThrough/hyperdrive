The Cthulhu mythology was the creation or perhaps the [[Memory]] of one H.P. Lovecraft. In his visions he saw an ancient and very dark unimaginable horror that merely to glimpse would send the viewer instantly and irretrievably insane. It is the unconscious ‘stuff’ of such visions that binds humanity inexorably, preventing any ultimate release beyond the dark density. These are essentially levels of our fears of our own mortality, the illumination of which can be the release from the bondage to such fears.
