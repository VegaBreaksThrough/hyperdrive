(Latin – the literal translation is ‘in flight from God and bondage to ordinary life’.) This energy refers directly to the fallen state of the born condition where the vision of God has been forgotten. In the witnessing of this energy the vision that was revealed was the human form tied and bound, feet and arms and the head bent down, thus the energy depleted and full of sorrow, a great despair upon the heart as intuition of the loss of God was obvious. Energetically this *Vial* is directly related to the release of the bondage to servitude.
