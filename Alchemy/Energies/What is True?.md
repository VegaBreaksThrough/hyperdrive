The ‘fact’ is that throughout our human history we have found it very hard to say something is true, without having to modify it to ‘false’ at some time later. How is this possible? If something is true, it’s true for always isn’t it? “It’s true for the moment, but all the facts haven’t come in yet.” If being true depends ultimately upon all the facts, then there can be nothing that we can assert to be true, without fear of contradiction at some time. In the mean time, what are we doing? It would seem we are “as if-ing” again. But what about all these scientists, they seem to be able to make all manner of ‘assertions’ about the trueness of this and that? Further, there are ‘technologies’ based on these ‘assertions’ that ‘work’, such as computers and so on. There are a few sticky ones here, like jumbo jets and bumble bees shouldn’t be able to fly! Without knowing what keeps the bumble bees up, it would seem it is just our faith or belief that keeps jumbo jets aloft or maybe the Divine wanted jumbo jets to fly and simply made it so. To get back to scientific ‘assertions’ and technology, the suggestion here is, as we have noted before is that, “what works is what is true” or what is commonly called the ‘pragmatic approach’ and we have considered before that the assertions of what actually ‘works’ is more than a little dubious. So, if “what works is what is true” is the case, we are in serious trouble about what is true, since trueness is thrown back upon ‘works’, which is absent from our observations of the ‘side effects’ of all technology, not just in its ‘operation’, but also in the ‘making’ of it. If spurious aspects are produced by technology, it cannot be said to ‘work.’
No matter how much we might try, we seem unable to find anything but ‘shifting’ foundations for all our assertions of what is ‘true’. What is the ‘problem’ here? The ‘problem’ here is that we are merely dealing with knowledge, which is making ‘assertions’ after the ‘fact’ of the world presenting ‘itself’ to us and such a presentation depends upon the naive acceptance of its mere appearance, as being all there is to it. From the point of view of only having after the fact knowledge of things, we can never ‘assert’ anything about the existence of anything and if we can never know anything about the existence of ‘things’, we can never assert the trueness of anything. So what is true in any real sense can never be asserted based upon a posteriori evidence. 
What is clear is that we must cease to base our ‘lives’ on the assertions of knowledge about the essence of existence, something knowledge can never ‘reach into’. Thus, we must move beyond knowing or wanting to know and move into our real disposition of simply seeing. When we simply see, the world reveals itself to us, because our seeing is consciousness and at last we see, in the process of revelation, that the world is made up of the same stuff and that is consciousness, or the Divine light, and this being the case, we see that the world is not different from us. It is our own radiance. Therefore we seek knowledge, because we do not see (the proverbial blindness) and those who will not see are operating with a cosmology that can only be regarded as a ‘paper-mâché universe’, empty and very shifty underfoot. Thus, seeing is our real relationship to all this apparent ‘stuff’ and this seeing is the action of [[Real Intelligence]] that lifts us way ‘beyond’ the ‘pathos’ of the knowers and at last we are lifted beyond this ‘terrible’ vision, with all its mortality, into the eternal realm of Only God, even while we are still ‘alive’.
