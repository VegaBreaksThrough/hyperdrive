Armageddon is the last great battleground, a fitting metaphor for the warring circumstances at large and extensive within the world at this time. There are wars and rumours of wars, the earth is in upheaval and the end appears nigh. On the last great battlefield, to fall asleep would be to die overwhelmed by the minions of some hideous dark underworld. The Vial Asleep In Armageddon and its counterpart sticker on the sanctuary House '[[4108 August Way 'It Looks Like Rain'|It Looks Like Rain]]' invites us to rest deliciously undisturbed while the battle rages all around and all night long. Further, while we sleep in blissful peace we learn much about 'higher things' than the lowly battle of exploding egos.
