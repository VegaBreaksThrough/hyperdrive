In using The Tarot Wizard with some Tarot deck, what is shown is our present relationship to this matter of the Truth of us and therefore pointing the Way to the Revelation of our True Heart's Desire. Unlike most approaches to a Tarot deck, this is not in the form of a question which is always arising out of doubt, but in the form of the Confession of Happiness at Last and Forever.
