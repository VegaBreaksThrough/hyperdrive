Oh Woe is Us!

There is more room for pain in this world than pleasure. The dark and formidable array of discomforts that confronts us is an endless mutation of suffering. Even what may appear pleasurable is, if we observe freely, permeated with some level of aggravation. In this world, as many know by direct experience, discomfort knows no bounds and we can suffer severely due to its unbridled intensities. Oh woe is us!

Of course we cannot morbidly be left meditating on the negatives of life: We must embrace the transcendence beyond merely these levels into a greater relationship that discovers that love is the greatest aspect ot this world, where we are lifted to the Magnificent Obsession of endless forgiveness of all that appears to offend us.

This is where we rest eternally; in the realm of Only God as love. This Energy is a great and welcome comfort on the ocean of woes that abideth here.

(This being a blue vial it addresses that which stems from the upper coil versus the pink vial which is more the lower coil.)
