What needs to be the response to these Scribbles From Beyond The Gap? 

Clearly the response is scored by what is understood or felt about the author, which is of course left open-ended, as it should be at this point. 
From this understanding, do these Scribbles have more than entertainment value to the reader? A written piece only has entertainment value if the reader is not moved to allow any change to their living circumstance, which is to say, their force of tendencies is greater than the persuasiveness of these writings. The author is therefore saying these writings are an energy that is a force for change, but it is competing with the readers resistance, which is to date winning against the writings. Such an actual situation is not in the least inspiring, showing that the response is not the source of inspiration. If no-one is actually able to move to change how they live, then the author is wasting his time. In the end it is no concern of the author’s, who is able to respond truly and who is not. From the author’s point of view, if this matters to anyone, the failure to grasp the essentials of the writings is and shall be disastrous for the unresponsive reader. It is disastrous because without a true response the reader cannot embrace real life, if this matters to anyone. 

Now, it is known that there have been several positive and cute responses to the writings, but these responses are largely out of the blue, which is to say, that the responder in their very next move transgresses the appeal to righteous living. The author, however, has no expectation that anyone can or shall truly respond. It is clear, because of resistance to engaging real life, that very few, to none, arrive in this place with a force of intention to participate in real life. The true response to these writings is to take them to heart and allow their force to undo the entire conventional stance of not taking anything to heart, that is not allowing these writings to truly touch them, by allowing change to occur at the moment of reading. When the reader is touched and not just processing the writings with their recalcitrant mind, the moments of their living are spontaneously and continuously informed by the writings, rather than the [[muttering dark]]. Such a one is clearly being invited constantly to the disposition of enlightenment, which is being lifted out of their “gremlin nature”. Many may think that there is nothing wrong with being a ‘gremlin’. There is no argument, ‘gremlins’ may do as they please. There will be a reckoning however and we therefore do as we please at the peril of this reckoning. So, until the author sees that the writings serve a real function, beyond simply being of entertainment value, it may be, for the time being, that the author writes no more, or at best only spasmodically, like in Morse code: three long, three short, three long... or until “[[No Worries! The Sun is Bound to Blow Up First|The Sun Blows Up First]].”
