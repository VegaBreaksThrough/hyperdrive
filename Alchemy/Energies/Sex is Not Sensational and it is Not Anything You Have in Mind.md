It is not a difficult matter to ascertain that most people of the world conceive of sexual activity to be principally focused on our fleshiness. What such a focus immediately implies is that sexual congress is merely a matter of nervous titillation and mostly nervous exhaustion through fascination with orgasmic release. However, if we consider our physical forms, they are simply about feeding and procreating and in and of themselves do not suggest they are the centre of ‘sexual intimacy’. It is clear from the fact that most couples find it difficult to impossible to remain excited about each others’ sexual possibility based upon sensory experience, the rub of ‘love’, that their sexual activity will eventually reduce to zero. This is what we might call de-[[Polarisation]] or utter disinterest in one another and starting to look elsewhere. Using the woman as a love mannequin is eventually and quickly defeating. Hence, there has been a proliferation of adult sex shops and selfhelp books attempting to rekindle our flagging ‘sex lives’ so-called. 
These little helpers even suggest bigger and better orgasms are the secret of a healthy life, whereas they merely contribute to our early demise and a greatly enhanced self-fascination. Truly those who engage in orgasmic satisfaction do not know each other in a feeling intimate manner since even as they begin their embrace, they are lost in the search for their own orgasmic release. Such ‘lovers’ are encapsulated within their temporal adventure and do not permit themselves to feel the underlying pain of such involvement.
Any involvement dependent on beginnings, middles and ends is suffering, if only because the so-called ‘pleasure’ ends and we are abandoned to the emptiness of our existential neurosis, faced with what a moment ago was attractive suddenly becoming revolting. This is so because, at the level of our experiential stance, we are at the mercy of the natural order of things, and this is merely based upon attraction and revulsion i.e. what we are merely attracted to we will sooner or later be revolted by. Living at the experiential level with each other, we are at best attempting to offer consolation to each other in the form of mutual masturbation. It is also possible and usual to get aroused sexually all by ourselves and then seek to interest some likely partner, yet even if they respond to our desiring, we are simply attempting to release tension that has accumulated in the lower body. If we feel better doing this, it is because we are temporarily exhausted and we are not feeling our usual frustrations and tensions. Such involvements come out of the mind and therefore are selfgenerated visions of what is hopeful of pleasurable release. These things we have in mind are forms of entertainment designed to appease the [[Hungry Ghost]]. This hunger seeks greater and greater appeasement, moving on into the debased levels of the erotic and pornographic and beyond, and this empty land is about the murder of the body. So, sex is not sensational, it is not even ‘sensational’ from the usual point of view; it is merely a painful, heartless self and other exploitation.
The true form of sexual play is about energy and as energy it is from an already full disposition, not one of hunger. The fullness is a sign of our relation to reality. Abiding as reality we bring our enjoyment to each other, not seeking some bodily experience for ourselves. Since this play is not going anywhere, each moment is full of the enjoyment that is reality, is happiness already, and the play as energy is not a mere artefact serving the desire for orgasmic release. Feeling ourselves as energy, we are conducting the life-force, and from this disposition we are already happy and not merely trying to get happy. Unlike the usual conventional play, real sexual intimacy is enlivening, not debilitating. We are enlivened by our mutual delight in the play itself and not looking for something hopeful within the play. The play of sexual intimacy as energy is beyond any level of self or other exploitation; it is instead a moral involvement of simply being moved by and as our loving beyond time, and beyond, therefore, beginnings, middles or ends. So our loving is an eternal embrace even in the midst of all this ‘duality’. We are transcendent in our loving, whether it be as sexual intimates or in the wider involvement of relational obligations. In our loving, all the many aspects of our engagement with others is simply creating forms of enjoyment. Therefore let us be lifted from the cruel play of the self-seeking, self-fulfilling [[Dreamers Within the Dream]] and be artfully surrendered into the Wondrous Blissful Play of the Great God of the Truth of us, even as we are moved to reach for each other to engage this free energy in loving embrace.
