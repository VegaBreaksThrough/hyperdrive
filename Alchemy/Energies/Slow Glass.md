This _vial_ re-organises the temporal aspects of the life form to a more creative tolerance. This _energy_ is a moderator for those who panic about lack of time by shattering their conventional relationship to time, allowing them to discover more time to meet their obligations. When used with another *vial*, [[Slow Glass]] slows events down to enable the person to see the implications of the relevant vial.
