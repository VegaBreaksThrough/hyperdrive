You look at the ground and you think you are incomplete, submerged in ignorance you do not see yourself. Lift up your gaze and abandon the sensation that locks you in separation from the body. When the veil of your sorrow is removed, you will see the Light that inspires all that surrounds you; you emerge from the chrysalis that has cultivated the lightness of your understanding. Soul and [[Nous|mind]] reestablish the meaning of your existence, you are happy in the face of any circumstance. I have given you the happiness of flying with the wind, of dancing without regard for the contractions of wings that stretch to infinity. You have seen the Light from which everything emerges, leave behind the confinement of your cultivation and be grateful for the joy of this rebirth. 

The Angel Of Happiness is the clear Light that crosses the hells to bring bliss to the thoughts, emotions and feelings of those who embark on the awakening of their essence. It brings the knowledge of the essential completeness of our Being, not separated in body, soul or spirit; a whole integrated in the unity of Consciousness. Separation and consequently the search for what has been lost is the root of the sorrow we suffer in the depths of darkness. Happiness comes with the Knowledge of the Truth of our essence, of God.
