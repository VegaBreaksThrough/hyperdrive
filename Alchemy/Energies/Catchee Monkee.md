It is true that the 'usual' person has no way to know, what is the Truth and what is a mere beguiling persuasion, and so we would not **k**now the God man or the Real process even if we proverbially stumbled over them. Everyone awaits the return of Christ; but most have no capacity whatsoever to recognise him. We saw this enacted at the time of _Jesus_ _of_ _Nazareth;_ very, very few had any intuition of who this person was and as we know, most reviled _Him._ Nothing has changed, if it has, only for the worse. _Catchee_ _Monkee_ opens the eyes of those who are fortunate to have stumbled across the *True* process, yet in the stumbling do not show any rejoicing. In 'open eyes' this rejoicing is obvious.
