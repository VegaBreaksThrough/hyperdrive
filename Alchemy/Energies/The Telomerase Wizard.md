In Medical Science one of the functions of Telomerase is to place caps on the cellular DNA to prevent bits of the DNA from being broken off. It was suggested that this crumbling of the DNA was largely responsible for the aging of the cell. Other functions were mooted, but this capping of the cellular DNA appeared to be the principle one. However, whatever usefulness Telomerase has for the integrity of cellular bodily life is exhaustively employed by the Telomerase Wizard perhaps leading to immortality of cellular life.
