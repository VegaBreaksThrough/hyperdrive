Christ, or ‘Christos’, is a Greek word meaning the ‘anointed one’ and is another way of saying God-realisation. Of course the Alchymist is the same one as Christ, that is God-realised, animated by the one, Only God. If he or she were not, they would not be an Alchymist. Everyone must aspire and attain the Christ consciousness to awaken as Only God. Jesus of Nazareth is a historical character whom many believe or recognise as having attained the Christ consciousness, thus he is known as the Christ. Christ consciousness is not tied to a historical event, but exists now as all realised beings, animated always and forever by the same heart that lives us all. The Second Coming and other such predictions point to other realised beings that have awoken to Christ consciousness arriving in this realm to aid the spiritual enlightenment of humanity.
