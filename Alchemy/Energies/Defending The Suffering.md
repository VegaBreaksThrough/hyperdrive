The effectiveness of each of these products is related to how 'available' we are to the essence of these _Gracious_ gifts. In most cases, the apparent dis-eased condition represents a resistance to _healing_, even a defense of the condition _itself_. Therefore _receptiveness_ is paramount in allowing a _Healing_ to take place. Apart from being _receptive,_ we are only _defending_ _our_ _suffering._ In _defending_ _our_ _suffering_ we are both refusing _Love_ and refusing to be _Love._ This _drink_ therefore penetrates this refusal or fearfulness, thus allowing greater _receptivity._
