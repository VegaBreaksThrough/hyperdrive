The “Holographic Universe” theory is a concept from physics, particularly string theory and quantum gravity, which suggests that the entire universe can be described as a **hologram**. Proposed by physicists like **Gerard ’t Hooft** and developed by **Leonard Susskind**, it states:

1. **Reality as a projection**: All the information about a 3D space can be encoded on a 2D surface, much like a hologram encodes a 3D image on a 2D film.

2. This aligns with discoveries in **black hole physics**, where information about a black hole’s interior is stored on its event horizon.

3. **Implications**:

• Reality might not be as “solid” as it appears; it could be a projection from a lower-dimensional framework.

• Challenges conventional notions of space, time, and material existence.

  

Philosophically, this theory resonates with metaphysical ideas that reality is an illusion or a mental projection.
