It is difficult to impossible to ascertain whether prayers are actually answered. Given that there are billions of beings in this place in all manner of dire circumstances that would give rise, surely, to all manner of demands upon the Divine, the continuation of those dire conditions suggests that there is something wrong with the praying or, whatever should be listening is on vacation, or perhaps, out to lunch.
So what is a prayer? Well conventionally a prayer is asking the Divine for some change in circumstance that is most often emotionally charged with importance for those who pray. Often this importance is the avoidance of some impending disaster and usually it is the ‘portents of doom’ which have turned the individual toward thoughts of seeking Divine help, where ordinarily, consideration of the Divine is not entertained. Such a circumstance could be termed ‘neurotic prayer’ and such prayer would account for most of what praying amounts to. Forgive the telling of this next section, but it brings much to light concerning the attributes of ‘prayer’. A number of years ago a shockingly haunting headline was emblazoned across the Sunday newspapers, “God help me! Please help me!” , which were the cries of a ten year old girl1 whose voice had been recorded by her killers. Does this throw up a host of questions about prayer and God? No doubt the parents of this child were haunted forever, as surely were many. So, welcome to the nightmare! It is indeed revealing about the nature of this world that such crimes are not a rarity. We are appalled by them and we wonder and we wonder why the ‘prayer’ for help of a small child would go unanswered. If there is any faith in God, does this test it? Yet faith is not that which can ever be brought into question wherever it truly exists, if anywhere. It is such questioning that reveals the neurotic condition of most who pray and all such events as those mentioned above and especially those close to such events, only often stoke up an anger towards God that was always present. It is impossible to know the totality of any event in the scheme of things and therefore, if we are in the habit of making judgements, we will almost certainly f ind ourselves awry.
What we see beyond our sentiments is that God is not responsible for what occurs in this place, but in some sense it is all of humanity which is responsible, since it is our offence of the law of this place that has paved the way for all manner of terrible events. The law is the [[law of sacrifice]] which is the demand to yield up everything back to whence it came and that is the infinity of Only God. 
We are not such a sacrifice, we do not live as the [[law of sacrifice]] so it follows that we are a vastly congested structure beset by endless possibilities for Crisis! What Crisis! This ‘crisis’ is an ‘emotionally’ reactive stance towards the world; we have ‘problems’ with something or someone, somewhere, yet our ‘problems’ with the world are not truly problems with the world, they are always problems with God, but always. So we see that if the problematic ubiquitous ‘person’ moves to ‘pray’ for something, they are asking help from that with which they have a problem. Do we think such a plea would be heard? Maybe some devil looks after his own? The ‘bad guys’ do seem to flourish and have a perverse capability for endurance. Hey! Hey! 
So what prayer amounts to is gradually being revealed and this is that the ‘prayers’ of those who offend the law are most likely not answered. Thus a certain level of innocence with respect to the law is required perhaps? And this innocence is whole body sacrifice into the total pattern of every moment of our living. Such innocence is hard to f ind, “O Ye of little faith”. Thus and so it goes, we beseech those who are considered to be innocent to intercede on our behalf. Does this work? Many say it does, so perhaps it does. However, real prayer is whole body sacrifice itself, where the one who loves God is, in each moment, bringing everything into the form that is love, and such a one may only seldomly accede to intervene in the particular events of individuals.
So the prayer that is love transforms the entire circumstance of this world, at last, albeit slowly perhaps, and despite the hugely favourable odds for the ‘faithless’, love is stronger and will inevitably triumph in the demonstration of the Glory of God, as an unequivocal and undeniable display of Divine power. So start trembling.
