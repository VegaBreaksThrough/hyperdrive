The spider takes hold in the unconscious, which reflects its nature - to create a way of trapping its prey and drawing it in through a hidden doorway that covers the hole of its trap. In other words, the unexpected hidden trap that catches and then overwhelms its prey - its all- embracing fear paralyses it victim. This disposition can be witnessed in the human form where people draw others, (in their determination to dominate others and protect self), into their own hidden, unconscious, aberrated states. Thus trying to draw all things into the disposition of fear. People who get drawn into such spaces are generally naive, unaccountable, and unconscious, and thus available to be overwhelmed by such poison. In the witnessing, it was seen that the [[Doorway To Hell]] literally had the Trapdoor Spider over it, sucking people in through their sympathetic unconscious movement, into the hellish emotions of the untransformed - where fear is clung to as the essence of existence and love is forgotten. The Spider became more active as we began to move through these Doorways within the world. Again, the reflection of more people either sympathetically moving against the Light, or the liberation of people beginning to move out of Hell.
