> [!INFO] *Allardyce T. Meriweather in Little Big Man*

---

*Mr. Merriweather*, a character in the film *Little Big Man*, from scene to scene would appear losing yet another part of his body so that by the end of the film, he had lost an eye, an arm and a leg. It is this loss through interaction with the life process that is an inspiration to create a _water_ to serve those who have lost bits and pieces of themselves through the advent of their life. Many of us caught up in the ritual sacrifice offered to Dentists and Doctors often lose teeth by the mouthful and ancient, apparently useless bits of us, like the appendix. These aforementioned professions will remove these troublesome bits of us, simply as a matter of course or simply because it’s fashionable to do so, as in the case of hysterectomies, caesarean birth and so on. This _drink, Mr. Merriweather_ addresses all the above mentioned losses and the losses also due to 'accidents' where we have lost fingers, legs, eye, ear or perhaps even half a face.
Apart from these gross physical losses, there is the loss of the so-called self-esteem, the sweet bird of youth that is lost, the hope that is lost and perhaps relatives by the bag full that we are not quite sure about as to whether it’s a loss or a relief. Along with all this kind of baggage, there is a host of artefacts that have mysteriously vanished - the mystical force that removes one sock from every pair that is inserted into a washing machine. Clearly this world has a great number of aspects that we can lose and if we take a trip down to any metropolitan railway station to the ‘lost and found’ office, most would be amazed at the range of objects that have been misplaced on trains, which probably includes rows of people sitting in the lost and found whom no one met at the station.
The point about all this losing is the having in the first instance. It is because we have, in our egoic grabbing sense, that we set ourselves up to be that one who must lose. What all of this shows us, and the [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] invites us to, is that there is nothing, not even a vague notion that can be clung to or even an outstanding finger nail that might cause us to pause and reflect on its wonder, all gleaming red, but literally nothing can truly be hung on to, all must be yielded up to where from whence it came, which is out of the Divine to be returned via every breath to the Divine. When we live life as a moment-to-moment sacrifice, our karmas of losing begin to dissolve and perhaps remain only as a difficulty to recall - “What’s his name?”
