This fire is the innate happiness that rests in the heart. It is the child's first smile. It represents the safe and happy way. It is the path of life, just as a spring flows, without resistance, that jumps bright and cheerful to join the ravine, which then forms a river, and finally reaches the source and origin of life, the sea. It symbolizes the individual and peoples' path without contractions, with the walk that offers less resistance, more security and guarantees happiness.
