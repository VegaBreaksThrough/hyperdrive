The Alexander Technique is concerned with the wrongful use of the human body. The principal wrongful use is throwing the head backward and compressing the spine in much of our movement. This wrongful spinal compression leads to the breakdown of much of our functional life. The Alexander Technique aims to correct this bad habit by dissolving mental sets that are independent of what is the actual case relative to our movement; for example, moving to pick up an empty suitcase as though it were full and being thrown off balance by such a mental set. Individuals such as Aldous Huxley and John Dewey attribute the last 40 years of their life to the Alexander Technique. Lifted to its highest realisation, this technique can dissolve all the knots of [[Bad Habits]] that lead to our early demise and a life plagued with a lack of energy and general ill health beyond the early years of our youth.
