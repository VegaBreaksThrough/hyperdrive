


The intention of this Energy is to support the traveller in the process of adjusting to the changes associated with moving hundreds or thousands of miles. Without realizing it, travel, even in the same country, can activate karmic conditions that would not be experienced in the everyday environment.

This wand is a good assistant to help you purify, overcome and dissolve disturbances such as the stress caused by flying high and fast in an airplane, the biochemical changes of being in an unfamiliar space, digestive stress, change of schedule, and other travel-related concerns.

An assistant to get you through this transition, to keep you fresh, and to clear the uncertainty and fear of the unknown.

Receive the blessing of this Energy and enjoy the opportunity to travel “lighter”.

Make the most of every moment of your journey.
