The present circumstance gives rise to self-seeking individuals founded in the greed of their nourishment stage failure and this is merely a part of a vast mosaic of failed emotional situations, down to the shockingly degraded.

This is the appropriate use of human energy or the moral responsibility. In this degraded ‘feral culture’, the first acts of the awakening to will and intention are often governed by an overt or subliminal madness; especially so in a culture dedicated to continual doses of ‘media sickness’, which effect the young ‘feral human’ the most as they tremble on the brink of so-called adult life in a state of shock.

Such a culture has nothing significant to draw the young person into, except an immoral role as just another trite self-seeking desperado.
