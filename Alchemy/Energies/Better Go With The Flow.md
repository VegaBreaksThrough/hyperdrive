> [!quote] A LITTLE OBSERVATION ABOUT CHANGE.
> «If anything can change, everything can change, and nothing was binding in the first instance. Otherwise, nothing could change» 
-Jessa O´My Heart 

> [!ABSTRACT] Ingredients
> Cypress, Eucalyptus, Lemon, Sweet Orange

Life is a series of natural and spontaneous changes. Resisting them is painful. Control is nothing more than the illusion that we, in some magical way, can direct the process of growth that is living. It submerges us into a delirium of better paths and limited dreams that fill with judgment every step we take. 

Controlling puts us in the position of ignoring the beauty of each situation and prevents us from recognizing, in the synchronicity, the mastery of that which is superior. 

Better to flow with what arises, harmoniously, moving forward. Remember, the great lesson is to trust. You can always rest in the absolute certainty of being sustained.

> [!tip] How to use this Alqvinergia? 
Resort to Better Go With The Flow to dismantle the resistance you feel towards something that is emerging. Allow the Alqvimia of this blend to transform you and free you, little by little, from all limiting structures. Place a drop on the pulse points or on the palm of your hand to immerse yourself in the aroma with deep breaths. As you inhale, visualize and experience how you are sustained in a space of joy and enjoyment. A few drops can be added in your diffuser to enjoy in your room, during your meditation or yoga practice.
