Science and popular culture has led many, if not most of us away from the expectation of the promised ‘Second Coming of the Redeemer’ into some vision of benevolent space aliens that somehow with their superior technology will ‘save’ us. What will they ‘save’ us from? Why have we been beguiled by the force of the word technology? Clearly in order to be ‘saved’ we have to be in trouble. What do we perceive of the trouble that we are in? Most of us in the more affluent parts of the world tend to deny we are in trouble and are most likely only embarrassed by ‘mad fundamentalists’ brandishing signs in our face that say, “The end of the world is nigh”, “Repent or go to Hell”, “You need Jesus”. Yet most of us would get quite interested if it was announced that; “Dipsy Doodle from the galaxy of Omnicrom” is arriving on some mountain top at some unspecified time. From that point onward many of us would be searching the night sky looking for signs of ‘Dipsy Doodle’. Obviously our search reveals that we have expectations of gifts from on high, even if from Spielberg heaven. The ‘children’ among us are waiting for that bright and shiny thing that will work great wonders for them and these wonders are most often couched in terms of being saved from all the ‘bad things!’ and to have all the ‘good things!’. The ‘adolescents’ among us are looking for the great message that will provide the knowledge that will unlock the mysteries of the universe and give them unlimited power and this power is freedom from the constraints of any level of discipline or the heat of transformation. Nowhere in any of this do we see that there is any notion of something being required of us, we are simply somehow receptors of the gifts, not in any sense the givers of anything. 
The trouble we are in is that we do not know God as the intimate source of us, we perhaps believe that we do or that the arrival of ‘Dipsy Doodle’ is the equivalent. These types of belief are not the equivalent of being alive as the intimate Truth of us. Most of us dream in our heads removed from the actualities of life in this world and are filled with a conglomeration of largely romantic nonsense from the past of our dubious forebears who were in just as much trouble as us. Their remote ancientness, strangely, somehow redeems them from their errors. We must know God as our true heart’s desire and from our true awakening, which is self-authenticating, a great alchymie of transformation begins. We must demand that whatever we are involved with in some religious or spiritual sense carries with it the alchymie of change. To be merely associated with that which at last is only a social demand to be ‘good’ is not to awaken the Truth of us. This is not to say that being ‘bad’ is somehow vindicated; the ‘good’ is an equanimity that is fundamentally required for the ‘lift off’ into something greater; the ‘bad’ is a distortion of our psyche that may cause us to drift ever further from ever seeing the need for real change. Therefore we must see the alchymie of enlightenment arising as our entire functional life, signed by the obvious falling away of the trivial social persona of our born destiny. Each level of awakening is a step taken in perfect trust, whole bodily, of the living Divine, and this is a constant dawning of [[Living on the Basis of Obviousness|obviousness]]. The alchymie of real change cannot ever be confused with the errant claims of all the emissaries of all the ‘Doodlers’. The alchymie of real change is worked in the fiery furnace of the heart of Only God, which can never be mistaken for the heartless counterfeit promises of the tricksters of our ‘childish’ and ‘adolescent’ dreams.
