The way of the boffins is a cruel and crude thrust at the world and we all wear the ‘harness’, along with atoms or any source of power that fits the traces. “We must harness the power of the universe!” is the catch-cry of boffins baying at the ‘homely [[A0457|moon]]’. This way of the boffins, which eventually sees everything in terms of its ‘utility’, is the bare bones “scorched earth” policy; “A tree only has value when it is cut down”, or “Let’s scrub the earth back to bare rock and live in satellites.” The ‘utilitarian’ approach is an appalling ‘blindness’ that fails to see that the world is not just physical but is rather psycho-physical, which is to say ‘it’ responds to our ‘presence’ here. Clearly, the only ‘ultimate’ response that we can expect under the present ‘guidance of scientific technology’ is to be destroyed by the reactions set in motion by our ‘utilitarian’ efforts. Without intervention, the destruction of ‘everything’ is inevitable, the boffins will insist. Of course, we are all always being ‘assured’ by all the scientists that everything is alright, just before ‘they’ are gobbled up along with all ‘their’ assurances. From this it is clear that ‘sanity’ is not a requirement in order to be a boffin scientist. 
The intervention needed is a vast healing force that stops the ‘viral boffins’ in their tracks. Such a healing is possible and perhaps even likely and immanent, all the ‘signs’ are present. Because the world is a psycho-physical event, the presence of the Divine in the midst of the ‘boffin’ disease can work great and lasting ‘changes’. Awakening to feel, to see truly the psycho-physical ‘nature’ of the world and in our surrender, being able to participate as the healing presence of the Divine is the only way we should be moved to invite. There is a Divine technology that is all-powerful and ‘wonderfully exact’ as it removes the effects of the ‘mad scientists’ and maybe even the boffins themselves through radical alteration of their stance. Since the world is a psycho-physical event, the true form of our ‘presence’ here is to manifest the changes required through right relationship, which is not the ‘presence’ of desperate ‘egos’ filled with endless ‘concerns’, but rather as the one who is yielded into the total pattern of relations. The flowering of such a yielding is ‘boundless’ and would bring forth the ‘technology’ of simple manifestation of ‘endless wonders’, rather than seeking to “squeeze blood out of a stone.”
