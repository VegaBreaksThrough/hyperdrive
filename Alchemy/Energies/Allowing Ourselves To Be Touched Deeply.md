As conventional individuals we neither feel what is the case, nor observe what is the case, our shadow falls on all things. It is a difficult matter when we are locked into our ‘Three Minute Googy’ to appreciate the [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]]. In being locked into self-meditation (self-possession), the individual is unavailable, immuned from the [[Feeling Dimension]] or psychic availability. In this disposition we are not just simply unable to feel the [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]], but we are in fact only reading how things affect us, as we are moved only by reaction, and this is not real feeling. The human [[Feeling Dimension]] is only awakened when we are able to transcend our reactions to the life’s circumstances. In the real human [[Feeling Dimension]] we are intrinsically alive as [[Real Observation]].
In [[Real Observation]] it simply dawns upon us what is the
case and in such a dawning we have fallen into the pattern of relations only and what is seen is that previously negative relations or negative reactions are clearly no longer present. When we are in such a negative pattern we only have recourse to Trust until the dawning beyond our self- enclosure.
Real feeling is trust, so cease defining and confining all that is by our own little moment, open therefore to what is greater and beyond any moment in this place. We only see what is True from the point of view of the Mystery, where there is no longer the blindness of self and other. So let us not continue in our superficial manner, untouchable, invulnerable and impenetrable and move beyond our fear of being influenced into an awakening where we allow ourselves to be touched deeply by all that is arising.
