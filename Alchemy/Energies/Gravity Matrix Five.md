**GRAVITY IN RELATION TO THE PSYCHE**   
The Psyche produces the usual destiny. One is identified with the usual destiny, the mortal being.  In the witnessing as G5 enters the form there appears to be the dissolution of creating the usual  destiny, and thus allowing one to fall into the identification with the Mystery itself. The  incarnating Divine personality is allowed to enter overwhelming the frontal personality and thus  allowing the beginning of something greater.
