Is all about receptivity to EVERYTHING

The fire that has been given to you cannot be squandered in the pursuit of mortal pleasure, what you squander will die with your aspirations.  You possess the ability to discipline the curiosity of the soul that the body somatizes in the search and desire for the release of its creative force. Realize that everything, just as it is, possesses the inherent means to bring you what your soul truly desires. 

The Angel Of Perseverance is an aid to the disciplined and constant work of the adept who builds the temple of wisdom in his life. It is the sulfur that transforms the vessel that receives the fire of our Divine Presence, with which we transcend the search for personal fulfillment and deliver the fruit of our work to the Will of God, the Obligation to Love. Perseverance is the temper required for the Art of sculpting the vehicle into Oneness.
