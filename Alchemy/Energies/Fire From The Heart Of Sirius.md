1. ### (CANIS MAJOR, THE GREAT DOG) FIRE FROM THE HEART OF SIRIUS
    

It is the brightest star after the Sun, it is known as the Sun of the spiritual world, guide of the Neter, origin of the seed of life, had great importance in ancient Egypt since its arrival announced the arrival of the floods of the Nile, at this moment it was given the name of Isis, it was connected with Thoth, and Horus, the burning star that guards the flame of life and the secrets of God. The Fire from the Heart of Sirius clears wirchos, spiders, souls and anything in the dark that prevents the conductivity of life-force and the material manifestation of abundance. In the astral chart, it rules the sign of Cancer and the second house.
