Does God play dice with humanity? In many of the things that we do there is a fatal element, since the prime possibility of existence is non-existence, as is evidenced most obviously to us by road kill. This fatal possibility hovers in the background most particularly with airplane f lights and vehicle driving. Both of these activities are like holding a spinning 6-chamber revolver to our heads with one bullet in one chamber, as we know the game. Such a situation keeps us perpetually frightened. Further, where there is a ‘me’; there is always the threatening other; so every meeting with the other (particularly foreign others, which is the terrifying monster of the xenophobe) is felt by us to be a ‘Russian Roulette’ situation. Does what goes around come around?
