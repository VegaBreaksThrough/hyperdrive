Open Sesame is the famous password into a hidden domain from the story Ali Baba and the Forty Thieves. The idea of requiring a password into a secret file or domain is also reflected in computer technology and this may be desirable to keep the forty thieves and their sticky fingers off our private mentionables. However, the usual conventional stance of the human individual cannot be unlocked, so to speak, in order that the individual might begin to be a participant in life and not merely functioning at some level of absenteeism. Therefore, in our usual mode we maintain a vast ‘hiddenness’ of unavailability that we would be unable to yield even if it occurred to us that this would be an excellent idea. So we stand largely muted with a sign flashing above our heads: ‘Access denied’. The [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] is the password that can lead the individual into [[Real Observation]] of their own ‘hiddenness’, into [[Real Observation]] of their own non-participation. The participatory relationship is one where we are yielded beyond any level of ‘hiddenness’, into the open-faced, open-hearted intimacy with all beings, as Only God.
