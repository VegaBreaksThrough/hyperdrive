It is said that by the time we are 50 years old we have the face that we deserve. This Energy explores and begins to reveal some of the ways we arrive at our caricature, which eventually becomes our epitaph or how others know and characterise us in life and beyond. Our stances lead us there. Our facial histrionics lead us there. Many other roads lead us to our face in the fossil. May we be lifted to the realm of the inscrutable. Useful to dissolve face lines, bringing youthfulness to the face.

Being that it is the Pink Vial, this is revealing the lighter side, whereas the Black Vial penetrates more of the 'dark' side of the caricature.
