Since there is only happiness all things are possible. Happiness is the form of reality. Happiness is the freedom from the implication of all that is arising. Since there is only happiness, we are always and only presiding over this domain as the consciousness that is reality. So, our very consciousness initiates the manifestation that is the apparent ‘many-ness’, that is this realm with which we are associated. From this ‘position’ we command the structures of this place; hence all things are possible since there is only happiness.
