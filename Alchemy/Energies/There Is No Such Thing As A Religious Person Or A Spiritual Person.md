There is no such thing as a religious person or a spiritual person. This implies there is no one who loves and likewise there is no one who is Enlightened and no one who is Realised and nor has there ever been. It is simply Light, it is simply Enlightenment, it is simply Realisation and none of these aspects or attributes are owned by anyone. This is simply so because the artlessness of being a 'person' is a separation from what is Love and what is Enlightenment and what is Realisation. To be a 'person' is to be what identifies with a limitation and this limitation is the cosmic experience. We must loose our taste for the cosmic experience in order to fall into that which is Love, which is Enlightenment, which is Realisation, which is the Absolute person of God.
