If we consider movie plots, and the so-called real life characters they often reflect, we see that mostly the characters act as though there was no God or if there was, what does it have to do with them? This is poignantly seen in that the only possibility for retribution for their actions is the common law of the nation and if they can outsmart the cops, everything will be fine, ‘home and free’. The movie plots almost singularly express points of view that are devoid of any intuitive level, everyone is single-mindedly pursuing the ‘gold and diamonds’ of their salvation! Now perhaps the movies reflect life, but because they are pervasive and intensely persuasive, life also reflects them. Many of us become frustrated would-be movie stars or begin to take on the style of movie characters, many of which are anti-heroes and very many of us are learning a very simplistic view of living and this is a non-intuitive relationship to life where often the highest concern is, “Don’t get caught!”. Do what you will but, “Don’t get caught!” So if we “Don’t get caught”, are we ‘home and free’? Is there no retribution beyond this dictate? Let this be said: “There is nothing more important than what we do when we are ‘alone’.” In the situation where we are ‘alone’ we can see what we amount to, which may be all manner of ‘arisings’ we normally attempt to hide from others. If we can then give up these ‘alone revelations’ we can deepen our intuitive response to real life. So, by oblique reference, there is retribution beyond the dictate, “Don’t get caught”, since we are always left with ourselves, surely a most haunting retribution.
The movies mostly deal with ‘mortal sins’ and beyond the major ‘evil doers,’ the rest of the mediocre characters are ‘venial sinners’. Yet, what are the differences? ‘Venial sins’ are cute, these are minor transgressions that don’t ‘cost’ us much; we can pay by the ‘pound’! The ‘mortal sins’ are ‘heavy’ and very ‘costly’. Yet, while this is the view of certain religionists, there are no such things as minor transgressions. In both situations we are without God and there is no middle ground. We either are alive as grace or we live in the ‘zero’ of ‘La La Land’. The ‘mortal sinners’ may not wake up to their situation for billions of years, because of their severe occlusion of the intuitive life. The so-called ‘minor sinner’ may wake up sooner, with some help. 
Now the question on everyone’s lips is, will there be big retribution, a final showdown? Judgement Day. Obviously everyone is taking a punt on this one and most are betting, “No”, especially after the anti-climax of the end of the second Millennium. Obviously, everyone will have to stay tuned for such a final episode. Yet, is Judgement Day episodic? Could there be something beyond it? Does anyone have any worries? Will everything be ‘alright’ on the day?
