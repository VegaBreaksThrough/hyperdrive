The prevailing view of ‘science’ is that everything observed can be reduced to being the effect of gross physical causes and therefore everything can be explained in those terms. In this view even our love for each other is a result of some hormonal instigation and our consciousness is merely an epiphenomenon associated with the organic ‘complexity’ of the human brain. So our consciousness is like the steam arising from a hot potato. When the hot potato is no more then our consciousness is no more and we are no more and we disappear into some imagined ‘black’ of non-existence. This makes nonsense of all religions and spiritual pursuits and demented idiots out of Jesus, Krishna, Buddha and any others who profess a Divinity beyond that of steaming potatoes.
In fact, from the scientific point of view, religion and spirituality are an impossible absurdity and are at best merely the idle dreams of terrified fools seeking comfort in illusions. In fact, it is true that there are many who profess to religious and spiritual involvement who are involved purely on the basis of their fearfulness and these many millions could be said to lend credence to the scientific point of view. In Truth, however, we are not the mono- dimensional beings that science would make of us i.e. merely an elaboration of potatoes giving off steam called consciousness. We are, in Truth, multidimensional beings and our ultimate existence is mysterious and unfathomable. Our consciousness is beyond the conventional functional mind and is the very reality that is God and hence our very consciousness is not contained within our forms, but our forms arise within our very consciousness, and so our forms and this world are the ‘steam’ arising from the modification of our very consciousness. Thus the apparent solid world of the scientist is merely a temporary effusion with essentially unfathomable purpose. The world exists; it does not Exist; Only God Exists, since what is temporary is not real; only what is eternal is real.
