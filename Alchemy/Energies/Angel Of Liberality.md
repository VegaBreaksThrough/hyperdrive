"There is no other reason to exist, you must surrender all that has been given to you, and all that you do with this body must be a sacrifice that you perform generously for the evolution of [[Consciousness]]. Understand, that which gives you form, comes together only as an expression of the purest and most sacred Love that exists. 

The [[Angels|Angel]] of Generosity establishes a space full of possibilities to embody, through the Verb, the Sacred teaching of Oneness. The mysterious Unity and Harmony between the forces that give form to creation, the expression of Love in the synthesis of the human form and its Divine Presence. 

Man receives the Perseverance of the [[A0437|solar]] rays and is cultivated in Justice and Happiness, what emerges from this communion, is the freedom of the [[Soul]] in its most Just and Generous expression, a brightness that surpasses the forces of the cosmos, a luminous beacon that frees the ignorant from [[The Unconscious|darkness]]." - Sirius
