There is hardly anyone alive who has sufficient intelligence to be able to grasp the essence of what is communicated through and as [[The Chone Letters]] as George Bernard Shaw once said, "The trouble with genius is that there are only fools to appreciate it." In this case God is the Intelligence is the Genius and our failure to grasp the ecstatic communication of [[The Chone Letters]] is also our failure to enjoy intimate association with the Divine. This seems like the proverbial 'horns of dilemma', for we need The Chone Letters in order to be informed of the truth and yet if we are not alive as the truth of us we would seem to be totally up against the walL However, the beauty of ecstatic communication is that it is a Healing force beyond the mere comprehension of the functional mind. Yet what is still required is the understanding that ecstatic communication is not merely about information but about purifying and transforming the reader and therefore the reader must be in the very least a participant available and therefore receptive to the gift of The Chone Letters. To treat The Chone Letters as just another bit of literature is to totally fail to grasp the significance and so we see many who say they have read The Chone Letters remaining non compos mentis[^1] relative to the contents of this ecstatic communication. How can this be? Apparently most individuals merely skim the pages and call this reading, but rather than this approach we must enjoy these writings as we might savour a love letter.

Now here we are, can anyone grasp this exposition on how to approach [[The Chone Letters]]?

---

[^1]: _Non compos mentis_ is a Latin legal phrase that means "not of sound mind" and refers to someone who is mentally incompetent or insane. This term is often used in a legal context to indicate that a person is not able to legally speak for themselves or is not responsible for their actions. The condition can result from mental illness, injury, advanced age, or intoxication.
