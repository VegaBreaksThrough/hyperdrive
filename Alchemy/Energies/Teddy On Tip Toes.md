In these tough competitive times those who stand flat footed in the ring are easily set up for the "knock-out'.

We are set up for the ready-set-go posture through the instilled contemplation of the future. Being able to find a creative relationship to the demands of everyday life is the prayer.

We are convinced absolutely by the belief that we are this one and that there are other ones and this carries with it severe implications of mortality and suffering unto death.

Our relationship to Truth, relieves us of being that one who must live and must die and blows the welcome

winds of release through the overwhelmed and distressed one who lies in bed at night listening to his Teddy Bear.
