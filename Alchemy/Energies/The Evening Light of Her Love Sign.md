Bearing her blessing sign of renewal, fluttering about My bow in churning gold.

She’s beckoning me with her ache

To change my course away from the [[A0464|pole star]] of release And toward the evening light of her love sign

She’s stirring. She knows I’ve seen her guiding light.

She knows in her longing I must respond.

I’ll turn her evening way.

Her heart-beat draws me on. She’s feeling my touch.

I’m out there, plying my travelling waves, All Heart spilling

Into her golden light,

Wavering over the wash on my decks.

She’s waking now. She feels my presence drawing near.

Still she moves slowly

The mists about her are dissolving only in their time. She coalesces as I gaze, eyes fixed unwavering her way.

She’s falling into me,

The dark spaces blow away; dust in a storm.

My light pierces her pulse. She feels me now. The faint ache widens to a maddening longing.

She’s seen me.

She’s rushing from everywhere Scooping me up in her swelling.

She falls at my feet blessing me with her tears.

She’s a shower of gold blessing me with her touch.

The dark webs fall away from her eyes.

The poison dries and dies.

Her hair falls and delights my toes.

A wondrous current, flashes again and again Way out across the tumbling waves.

This is the dawn, ushered in, by our unending embrace.

We hang in space, savouring a while, All this vast enjoyment.

Lovers everywhere release their grief, Free to confess the Heart

As Only God, stands out,

In the light of their embrace and We are seen at last, only in the bright,

Of their blissful surrender.
