There is a tension that exists between some idealised internalised persona and the external facts that simply do not coincide. The ‘internal’ vision may be some glamorised self-image, such as a svelte sexual form ‘internally’, whilst grossly fat ‘externally’. Clearly a tension exists here, which may appear to be reason for unhappiness, whereas it is unhappiness that brings about the gross bodily distortion. The [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] is infused with the evocation of the harmony beyond “dilemma in the mind and seeking in the heart.” (As stated by [[Adi Da|Adi Da Samraj]])
