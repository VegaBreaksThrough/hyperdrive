Fear perpetuates and delivers suffering until it explodes egoically in events such as the vast wars of the Twentieth Century.
Intelligence is an awakened blessing. Intelligence is not merely all the functional skills that are displayed by individuals everywhere from the highest to the lowest. This mantric presentation reveals the ‘junkie’ to him or herself, inspiring the individual to practice living beyond fear until that practice is fruitful in perfect realisation of the Truth of us; where all has been released into God and at last there is nothing to defend, nothing to prove. The peace beyond all peace. “Happiness is back in style.”(2.) So be it!
