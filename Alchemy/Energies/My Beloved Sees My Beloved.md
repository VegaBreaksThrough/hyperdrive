This is an ideal.

There is an idealisation.

A point of light spreads a defining touch 
Within the sentient gold of my Beloved’s eye

Drifting ever so in the infinitesimal reflections 
Loose among the planes of shifting geometries. 
This is the seeing, an idealisation of chaos.

There is the seen, an ideal within chaos.

Perhaps a gathering of small places the essence of ideals?

Or is this a shattering within the Primal Radiance 
Striking these tiny geometries resting beyond reason?

Ah! just so.

Exquisitely, sharply so. Oh! ever so, ever so.

As my Beloved sees my Beloved.
