_Now He sits in temples, veiled and secret 
A mystery to you in the pale Truths 
You cast sleepily before your glance
Your most unloving sight, sunk in the pool 
Of your own lost unfaithful reflection.
From the first dissolving touch of His Open Eyes 
His hand held out for yours as a myriad times past
And blessed He died for your most sacred sake. 
But you, shrouded in fancy, clung to illusion 
In all the artlessness of your shallow dying.
His Heart opened and bade you enter in Truth 
Yet you turned to dreams in your night
And forsook Him with but a glance behind.
Your own bleating beating heart 
Fell to your trembling hand
And convulsed in the mystery of your shallow dying.
A dribbling word spilled
Crushed from your frightened eyes
And fell as a stone bird, broken in the far reaches
You thrust toward the lover before you 
Already forgiveness.
Now He sits in temples alone and forsaken 
Awaiting the Graceful surrender of
All the artlessness of your shallow dying.
