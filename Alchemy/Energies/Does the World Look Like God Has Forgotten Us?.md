By no stretch of the imagination could any of us seriously consider that this world is run by a benevolent deity, much more likely it is held in the sway of a bunch of ‘geeky weirdings’ much like ourselves. No doubt many of us like to cultivate cosy notions about all this madness that is propagated in every direction and endlessly in order to feel more comfortable with the horror that is the ordinary lot of even our middle class ensconcement. Before we consider anything else about this place, before the dodo wowsers chime in with “life is just dandy,” we must remind the sundry, if not the all, that in this place there is death. Can we be cute about this matter of death with such catch phrases as, “I’m not afraid of death” or “Everybody dies, so what?” or “What’s so great about this place anyway?” What is wrong with these types of responses is that no one knows what they are talking about. Being relieved of this ‘vision’, at last, is worse than any experience we could have at the level of life. For the unenlightened and therefore atheist executee in the last moments, as the life-force is withdrawn from the body at the top of the head, the legacy of the whole human race in their ancient death throes (a vast black cloud of feeling ourselves ‘snuffed out’ screaming) rolls in upon us. This folks is simply ‘how it is’, we are probably still screaming as our remains are passed through the crematorium or dumped in the ground. Beyond this moment it is then simply a matter of the dreams we hold in our heart as to where we drift to until some place, perhaps this one, is returned to our consciousness by projecting another gross form out of the heart. 
So, does the world look like God has forgotten us? What it looks like is that not only has God forgotten about this place, but has also completely forgotten about Himself here. This is a worry of huge proportions, for it seems we are all alone with this torment and a mad amnesiac God. So does God ever appear here as Himself? Obviously not. Is God somewhere else looking down on mankind taking care of us? Obviously not. Does God care about us at all? Obviously not. God is love, right? So is there any love in this world? For the most part, obviously not. God appears here and has appeared only through the Great realisers as effective agents for purification and change. It is true that any self-forgetting individual could in any moment be presented as love, but this is rarer than hen’s teeth as they say. Further, rather than value the Great realisers, humanity has most often given them a very hard encounter, as we know, even to the point of extinguishing the only ‘hope’ for us all.  
The Great realisers are those beings who appear here out of the God light in order to serve the mad God Who has forgotten Himself and become a bunch of ‘geeky weirdings’, an unparalleled madness and can we figure out why it is all like this? Obviously not; it is not a given. So who you gonna call?
