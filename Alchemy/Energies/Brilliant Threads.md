BIG BLACK HOLE SOAK

*Jessa O´My Heart* 

Our clothing garments and linens become loaded, like us, with thoughts, stress, and discordant energies. Although we do not always realize it, this negativity shrouds and debilitates us. 

This synergy has the intention to clean and liberate that energetic load— to best transform our clothing, linens, and home into pristine and brilliant sanctuaries; capable of sustaining us in every moment. 

How to use this Alqvinergia? 

Place four to five drops of Brilliant Threads on a small wet towel or on wool dryer balls and toss in the dryer during the last 10 minutes of the drying cycle. Alternatively, you can dilute ten drops in 100 ml (3.4 fl oz) of water and spray your clothes once dry or simply place a few drops in your diffuser to enjoy.
