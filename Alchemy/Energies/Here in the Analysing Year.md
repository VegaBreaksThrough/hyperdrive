_Here in the analysing year, dust gathers your eyes And you lust toward a perishing lie.
And the day that rises there Out these dancing motes Is all your eyes can see.
And here you beg the pale worm of rot To fix the maggot eye, steadfast in fact Upon this tump of ages
Spinning as clockwork, in a galaxy of otherness Not true.
O Yes!
The year is a charnel house
Wherein the momentary counting We strangle the pulse of the living And in the numbing dark
Greet the howling formalin dead
Who in glazed agony, sing songs in praise of self.
Lofty indeed
Above the worming pallor
Of the harvest of innocence dying.
Here is one who lightly tossed The keen edge of hard saying Into a spumy numbed clutch Of soft cloying magicians
Cheering and incanting
In their illusion of hiddenness
Favoured by the dim wit of history and long stories Cupped, O Yes! Held
In the nimble fingers of their shadow fears.
These nobles of dreaming
Chew on the fat fingers of lean friends Lying hidden, in a frore dark
Gobbling the sprouting eyes of gasping red limbed lovers Who in their pale innocence, are withered fruit.

_I have seen, in this clutch, the curdled afternoon Crawling into the glass filled corners
Of a friend’s small needs
Afternoons of cursing and lateness of hours Left wandering to the shades
Of stunted hopeful days in flight from despair Hated moments of times, now beyond a reach Fevered in regret, when eyes met
In the fierce knowledge of their weighty understanding.
This ragged clutch, of bones before us now Is the white lumber of a friend’s promise.
Time drove this hapless child away With blighting whips of moist red lies Wielded in the stone eyes
The fusion of [[Epitaph And Face Are One|epitaph and face]] The crouched chained loneliness
Locked into the mortality of its sorrow.
In great choirs you have sung the hymn of your hate And now you lament in the lateness of the hour
When the morrow should never have tempted The new unfettered dreams
Already the yawning rubbish in the cluttered soul Of the much-to-many turning away.
Yes you, burn in the desert
That awaits to pall the eager heart. Here is the long back, of a young brave
Whose painted face
Wears the tight smiles of his dead mother’s Weep, for he dances death, in a tangled whimper
Of his shrivelled challenge.
I was with him
When the strong mouthed lip of his fierce eye Kissed the feet of his enemy’s laughter
And toppled as a scythe
The dappled merchant’s fat plums Vacant under the gleaming eye Of the new child’s brave hope.
O! Here is the shade Who kissed your soul
When you plucked the trembling buds Of your nearly dreams
And led you to the brink of the Water Bridged by your virtuous longing.
O! Gather up these hope free flowers And strew the Way
With the easy scent of this passing over. Hold the dearest of these understandings Close in the choosing
Do not regret
The last of these frail long black shadow smiles.


_O! I have harvested your ears In a hush of whispers
And stood easy in the clinging fear Of the grinning knowingness
Held high and away
In the baleful certainty of your tomb.
O! I have tumbled, in regions of thin reason Where I stormed the dead of the two plus two With Truth of their inness

To weep and abide in the dancer’s ending.
There is no sleep
The eye that pains has lost the lid of its lie And cast its sight beyond the night
For it has seen, the ragged peace
In the last tremble of a lover’s heart
A lover who ate the sadness in your eyes

His fingers torn

clung to the tall shadows of his night.
