Perfection Does Not Exist at the End of Some Search



The whole _[[Cosmic Display]]_ is a _disease_ on _consciousness_, an unnecessary addition, an unnecessary adventure in space and time, some of which is _gross_, some _subtle_, all of which is irrelevant to our _real life_. We must therefore pass beyond all of this irrelevancy into the Truth of us. _Real life_ is not the perfection of _gross_ bodily experience, wherein we attempt to immortalise the bodily form or perfect _ascension_ into _angelic forms_, because in our _confession_ of _Only God_ everything is already achieved in God and no matter what our life experience may appear to be, it is nothing other than perfect, even though like [[Mr. Merriweather]] we are losing bits of ourselves on a regular basis. Life may not be pleasant, but it is perfect. It is not pleasant perhaps because we are unconfessed in our meditation on the pursuit of worldly matters and subtle hopefulness. Our _enlightenment_ depends upon the _recognition_ that the world and the _subtle dimensions_ are not _real_ and are merely expressions of our desiring. Our _confession_ is that we are not independent beings, like waves on the ocean are not independent ‘somethings’ but are mere modifications of the ocean itself. Just so, all of this vision, including ourselves, is simply a miniscule wave upon the eternal wave of _consciousness_, which is the Truth of us. All of this _gross_ and _subtle_ adventuring could be turned off in an instant between one step and the next, just like a movie coming to an end in a theatre. Therefore to pursue that which has beginnings, middles and ends is an enormously fruitless activity, since forever we might find ourselves searching in ‘wrong places’ when the Truth is not other than standing in place beyond all ordinary motivations high or low.

There are many who would have us believe that the Truth is always some other _place_, some other thing and we must pass through some level of effort to move from where we are to where we must be in order to _know_ the Truth, but the Truth is always already the case and it is neither within or without, since the world has no inner part.

Now it is true that meditation upon the false position of being a born person, in fear of being a born person, complicates the structure of this earthly vision here, because such a meditation brings about responses in this _place_ that become more and more uncomfortable, since this meditation on being the born person is not the true relationship to this vision. In being rightly related to this _place_, perhaps this temporary presentation could be more enjoyable and this _right relationship_ is to _see_ our involvement here as completely and utterly non-serious and that there is no great gravity here except the gravity that we produce in our mortal seriousness. The true form therefore is to be literally alive as the immortal or eternal form, while being present as an apparent mortal being. Then perhaps we could merely laugh ourselves into oblivion, because as the immortal form, we are as one who is already dead and the play of this one is the disposition of love, _happiness_ and the utter _blissfulness_ of the _consciousness_ that is and always was, now and forever more, _Only God_.
