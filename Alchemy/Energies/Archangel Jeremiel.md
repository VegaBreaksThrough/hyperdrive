**

Reality is perfect as it appears to us.  Everything that happens is part of a Divine Plan.  Not understanding this plunges us into suffering, frustration and negativity, which is the ” typical” state of someone living in this world.  Archangel Jeremiel gives us the possibility to understand that Submission To Grace Is The Only Way and that to do otherwise is to swim against the current and resist life’s lessons.

In perfect submission there are no more expectations, we are filled with hope, enthusiasm and confidence because we are sustained by God.

Keywords: hope, Sweet Surrender, trust.

**

---

Jeremiel’s name means “mercy of God”. Jeremiel is known as the angel of hopeful visions and dreams and overcoming difficulties. He communicates hopeful messages to people who are discouraged or troubled. Jeremiel’s message is “The challenges you’ve faced have made you stronger and have taught you lessons. Instead of becoming bitter open your heart with compassion towards people in similar situations.” (Words associated with this Archangel are mercy, compassion, hope, and understanding.)
