This Energy calls for help from the plant kingdom, even those plants lost to extinction or those plants yet to appear from other planetary systems. This Energy penetrates deep within the core, allowing us the freedom of contemplation of that which we would otherwise avoid. It is important to hold a focus of intention for specific contemplations. Try placing this Energy on the Stargate with the intention that it imbues your bathwater or imbue a glass of water and have a drink.
