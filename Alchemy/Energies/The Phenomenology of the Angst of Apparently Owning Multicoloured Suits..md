How do we see the body? Is it just a plaything? We can take it skiing, shopping and all the possibilitings. Is it something just to cart our ideas around in, parade them here, parade them there? Is it a worry because it will not behave, because it gets too fat or too thin, or sick, or tired, or too old? Is it genetically challenged? Is it the wrong colour, the wrong shape, not fashionable? Is it too sexy or not sexy enough? Was our career determined by what the body is or perhaps what it isn’t? Are we a ‘scary’ or one of the ‘drop dead gorgeous’ or just a potato with raisins for eyes? And what is our perception of the little ‘coloured suit’? Do we gaze in mirrors or avoid them? Are we delighted with our ‘suit’, or are we for the personality holding sway, like “If you know a goddess, introduce me to her ugly girlfriend.”?1
So what do you feel? What do you think? Have you examined this body thoroughly? Is it a worry that it has this beating thing in the chest, because we know things that tick and tock can stop doing that and then what? Or do we try to forget about it and try to live our life apart from what the body is doing, or is it unavoidable, because it is always leading us around by the nose, demanding this, demanding..., because it has a weak bladder? Or poor digestion? Or is it sluggish? Or overly jittery? Is it always taking us to the ‘body mechanics’ demanding overhauls? Can it touch its toes or jump over the dog?
In the end what is this body? Is it more or less than what we think it is? What do we think it is? Is it just a bit of ‘squishy stuff’ hanging around this ‘hard stuff’, like clothes on a butler stand? What is it? Perhaps there is a clue given in the uniqueness of each one? And since no body can ever stand in exactly the same place as any other body, the circumstance in which the body finds itself is also unique. This uniqueness of body and circumstance give rise to the consideration, at the risk of multiplying entities, of karma. It appears that the body and ‘circumstance’ is the karma we have ‘paid’ for, that is, we have ‘paid’ our money and made our ‘choice’ and this is ‘it’, and it doesn’t get any better, whatever ‘it’ is, it is downhill from here. 
However, is the body only about karma or destiny, the force of our psyches? The ‘worldly’ destiny is always and only about karma. So if the body could be about something else, it would have to be invited to live beyond the ‘worldly’ destiny. The body is not about anything other than destiny and there is an inertial quality determined to keep it right where it is. Can it be shifted and what to? 
Well in the usual life, no matter what we think, the body is in ‘control’, because we ordinarily do not have the intelligence or ‘free energy’ to reach escape velocity without using the false methods of suicide, madness or subtle escapades in the astral. These methods are wrong, because they always fail to achieve escape velocity or liberation at the speed of light. It is required that we be healed of our inertia, through allowing the life and body-mind to ‘rest’ in the force of understanding that is reality and what this amounts to, is falling out of the ordinary destiny, through the confession of happiness and into the disposition of grace. 
We have wasted the opportunity that this vehicle, the body is, if we fail to transcend our worldly destiny and fall into grace. Further, the body is not just this little ‘coloured suit’, it is all of this presentation, ‘body and circumstance’ and is also multidimensional. Therefore, the body is much more than it ‘appears’ to be and it is very much more than we ordinarily make of it, merely a vehicle of exploitation of functional possibilities. So learn to shift gears and open to grace as the only way and allow the purification, transformation of this ‘bodily form’ and beyond into transfiguration and ultimately translation. Discover [[A0019|submission to grace is the only way]].
