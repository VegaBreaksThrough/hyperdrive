To say we are on a path that leads to God is to say that we know what God requires of us. Yet the lack of efficacy of these paths does not arouse our suspicion that maybe God does not require ‘this’ or ‘that’ of us. Why don’t we require any level of efficacy from our ‘path’? If we consider all of the religious and spiritual institutions around the place, we see that most of them have been around a very long time and therefore we all grow up surrounded by these icons, which is to say that there are many aspects about these institutions we believe without any question. Yet, if we look at the places where these institutions are, we immediately become aware of contradictions, because the people who live around the institutions do not display any level of having been touched by God and this is particularly evident throughout the last few hundred years. So can it be said that all these institutions are not in touch with what God truly requires of us and are therefore merely superstitious nonsense? Is it too far sweeping to say all? In the terms of effectively demonstrating God and awakening others to God through that demonstration, all these institutions fail and are therefore only political theocracies that are demanding reverence falsely and this goes for all the false piety of both the institutions of the East and West. In all these institutions we are supposed to believe in all the claims that the propaganda bestows on us. Absolutely none of the world’s religious and spiritual institutions have the capacity to invite us to commune with God freely without the propaganda of the false witness of history.
Whatever the founders or inspiration of the world’s religious and spiritual institutions amounted to is lost in time, for the most part. What is certain is that no one was left by them who had their realisation and who was therefore able to demonstrate whole bodily the verity of the Truth of Only God. Even where, in recent times, realisers have appeared, what has remained after them is but a pale shadow of their inspiration and yet, even this recent inspiration has not been about love, but rather only compassion. Love is action. Compassion is inaction. 
Therefore all of these institutions are dead, because it was apparently not possible to awaken anyone to pass the baton on. Now, if it is true that even the supposed living inspiration of the founders was insufficient, what do we suspect of all that is left behind after they have passed out of sight? Only a political wasteland of empty hopes and empty promises. The institutions now belong to shadowland and can only lead us there.
Those who have become disenchanted with what is on offer as religion or spirituality create their own, largely adolescent, path of pulling themselves ‘up’ by their own bootstraps and teachings like those of Krishnamurti have great appeal to the adolescent, where talk of God shocks their sensibilities, because the demands of God halts their flight from discipline. The only disciplines they will entertain are those that are acceptable to them and these are felt to be “not too much”, “manageable”.
Again all the moves of the disenchanted are a false view of what God requires of us and this path of superstitions is but an attempt to bargain with God in order to sidestep any real demand. When the real demand does perhaps fall upon them, they often seek refuge in flight or madness. We cannot create cosy little paths in relation to God; there are no bargains, God wants everything that everyone amounts to in their self-image and this demand is hot, not comfortable, but in Truth not unhappy. The path is our attempt to hold back from God. Hold on hard enough and we will take flight or go mad or both.
What God requires of us is our confession of happiness, which is now and not in some supposed future. Our confession of happiness is our whole-hearted recognition of the undeniability of the Truth. When the Truth touches us, what we must feel is this matter of undeniability. When this great matter is touched with undeniability there is no where to retreat to, no place, no madness, we must simply cop it sweet and allow ourselves to be undone from all our illusions of the path, even where the path is not seen as such, in the mediocre stand against God. So, give up, allow this happy confession to consume all that we amount to in all our superstitions and realise that we must come clean at last and the sooner the better, since there is no later. So, what God requires of us does not amount to a path, but is a present recognition of the obviousness of Only God. This recognition is a present enjoyment of our confession of happiness and whatever this gives rise to in the form of the actions of enjoyment, these actions are never in the form of seeking, what we call the path, but always and only present enjoyment of the life lived in the intimacy of the always already presence of the living Divine.
