> «May those who have found sanctuary come to rest deliciously undisturbed, while the battle rages all around and all night long» 
> [[Asleep In Armaggedon]]

> [!ABSTRACT] Ingredients
> Juniper Berry, Roman Chamomile, Rosalina, Australian Sandalwood

This synergy has the intention to support us and protect us in the process of surrendering to deep peace. There we take refuge to submerge ourselves in a repairing and deep rest, far from daily disturbances and stress. 

> [!tip] How to use this Alqvinergia? 
> Place three to four drops of Restful Sleep in a diffuser in your room shortly before going to sleep. You can also dilute a few drops of the synergy in water and spray it on your pillow before bed. Inhale and surrender.
