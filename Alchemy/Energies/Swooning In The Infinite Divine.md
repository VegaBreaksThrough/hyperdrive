The usual life is a maelstrom within which we can scarcely take a breath that does not suck us deeper into the mire and the muck. From the beginning to the end of our lives most of us struggle even to believe there is God and even if we manage some neurotic belief in God we do not enjoy any intimacy with the Divine since mere belief does not transfigure into such enjoyment. Enjoyment of the Divine requires real practice in abiding in and as the mystery of existence beyond the humourless emotional disassociation of the activity of mindedness, to the point where at last, Only God stands out. Left to our own devices in these times we would never know the enjoyment of the Divine unless it is demonstrated right in front of us. Swooning in the infinite Divine of the hermetic touch, graciously lifts us, and gifts us with the ‘touch’ of the living Divine, so that we might at last be able to love God more than self.
