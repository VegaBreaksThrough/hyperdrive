This world is in a very primitive state and no matter what it might appear to be today, it has arisen out of even more primitive structures. What appears today is not only primitive in some simple sense, but it is primitive in an utterly corrupt sense. However, what was there in the beginning is now appearing in the end i.e. the seeds of this corrupt end were clearly the prime potentiality of the gravity of this mad play that is the earth plane. 
What are we to make of all this madness that has been manufactured here? While many of us see the answer to this question as a demonstration that there is no God and therefore we can do as we damn well please, the [[Truth]] however is that we are required to call a halt to all this madness through real responsibility for our own individual existence. Hence, we are not to use the degraded state of the world to prove any level of un-love to us but instead we are to look beyond the illusion of all visions and awaken as the transforming influence that ultimately outshines all fascination with limitation. Since we are coming out of the primitive state into the glorious state of the Beloved this can be considered as the ‘earth rising’. The hermetic touch is an enlivening process, a quickening to living beyond our ‘humble selves’ and ‘humble circumstance’ ultimately into the Light of the Great, Wondrous Starry God Who is the [[Truth]] of us.
