No words can describe the Radiance of my Essence. If you see me with the eyes of the Heart, you will see the Oneness of my activity. There is only Light, there is only Life, there is only [[Logos|Word]] and in the oneness of matter, the movement of the gods creates the filigree that defines all my knowledge. All the Geometry and the Art of my Lord, coagulates in the forms that move and change until manifesting the crystalline eye through which you can see the natural light. I am in all and fill all with the Consciousness of the creator. 

The Angel Of Light is the breadth of a life filled with Happiness, you must be reverent to expand the boundaries of your [[Nous|mind]], until in infinity, the space of your phenomenology is one with Heart. Light provides the awareness, the reason and the understanding to gain the Knowledge Of God. You can only know what is at your height and you can only reach such an expansion if you allow yourself to receive the holiness of the message that is expressed in the common form of a leaf, of a rock. Knowledge enlightens the [[Nous|mind]] and generously gifts you with the Realization of the Divine Presence.
