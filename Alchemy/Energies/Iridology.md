**Iridology** (also known as **iridodiagnosis**[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iridology#cite_note-Cline-1) or **iridiagnosis**[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iridology#cite_note-2)) is an [alternative medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_medicine "Alternative medicine") technique whose proponents claim that patterns, colors, and other characteristics of the [iris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_(anatomy) "Iris (anatomy)") can be examined to determine information about a patient's [systemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systemic_disease "Systemic disease") health. Practitioners match their observations to _iris charts,_ which divide the iris into zones that correspond to specific parts of the human body. Iridologists see the eyes as "windows" into the body's state of health.

Iridologists claim they can use the charts to distinguish between healthy systems and organs in the body and those that are overactive, inflamed, or distressed. Iridologists claim this information demonstrates a patient's susceptibility towards certain illnesses, reflects past medical problems, or predicts later health problems.

As opposed to [evidence-based medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidence-based_medicine "Evidence-based medicine"), iridology is not supported by quality research studies[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iridology#cite_note-Ernst-3) and is considered [pseudoscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoscience "Pseudoscience").[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iridology#cite_note-4) The features of the iris are one of the most stable features on the human body throughout life.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iridology#cite_note-5)[_[disputed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Disputed_statement "Wikipedia:Disputed statement") – [discuss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk:Iridology#Talk_page_section "Talk:Iridology")_] The stability of iris structures is the foundation of the [biometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometric "Biometric") technology which uses [iris recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_recognition "Iris recognition") for identification purposes.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iridology#cite_note-Chellappaedited-6)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iridology#cite_note-Pankantiedited-7)
