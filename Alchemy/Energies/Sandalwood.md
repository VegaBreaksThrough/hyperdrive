**SANDALWOOD (Santalum spicatum)**

_Consciousness • Surrender • Detachment_

  

**Properties**

Lymphatic and venous decongestant. Cardiotonic. Nervous calming agent. Immune and sexual system stimulant.

  

**Indications**

Moisturizes damaged skin, especially oily, acne-prone, dry skin, and for rejuvenating treatments. Useful for varicose veins, hemorrhoids, neuralgia, sciatica, and lower back pain. Helps with nervousness.

  

**Precautions**

None indicated.

  

**Emotional Properties**

Helps calm the mind, follow the Heart, and prepare the Spirit for communion with the Divine. Aids in releasing attachments and aligning priorities with the Heart’s will. Facilitates guidance by the inner voice and raises levels of consciousness. Ideal for meditation practices.

  

**Origin:** India

**Part of the plant used:** Wood
