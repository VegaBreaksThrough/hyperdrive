This Energy 'Mr. Darcy’ represents the conversion of mind standing over and against the Heart. It allows true feeling intimacy, the Present Moment, the active principle, drawing us out of the solid structure of mind, awakening to the 'feeling' dimension and animating the Divine presence whole-bodily. That is, it allows the male disposition to yield. The male stands above and beyond the world 'looking at it' with the critical analytical disposition. So this conversion then allows the male to soften so that the mind falls from that stoic disposition to Heart awakening, or [[Feeling Dimension]], or feminine current, allowing the body to be incorporated so that both the mind and the body can be given to Only God. 

_In the Alchymie of creation, a Divine Vision was given: It was an exploding of the red mass of mind. This can be seen as a grid of terror, particularly of the male being afraid of yielding to the body and incorporating the body as part of the prior disposition. It represents the Divine Current entering whole-bodily. The red mass appeared as an acid like substance that had been dripping into the body (because of the exclusion of the body and concentration on the mind centre only). In the vision this was Alchymised and combined with the Divine Current, dispersing the life-force through the entire form. It allowed the [[Feeling Dimension]] to awaken as a whole-body gnosis. So it could be seen as the Divine current transforming and transmuting the acidic release into Divine Nectar, which allows a felt softening and nourishment to the bodily form. This conversion also brings the incorporation of nourishment and trust to bodily life. There is a deep relaxation and flow with this Energy. This transmuting current allows one to feel the release of the angst and withdrawal from life. Instead of standing in 'mind', over and against the world (the female, the body), we begin to incorporate and combine with it. '[[Not 2 Is Peace]]' is the feeling that this energy brings, the dissolution of separation. A very significant Alchymeic Energy!_
