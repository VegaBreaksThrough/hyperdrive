The Lilliputians of Gulliver’s Travels go to war over which end of the egg to open, the round or pointed end; Crisis! What Crisis! Somewhere in ‘lonely land’, a fifteen- year-old boy kills himself because his driver’s license has been suspended and his social life is in ruins, Crisis! What Crisis! No doubt many of us can recall moments in our lives when some trivial matter somehow gained the status of a life and death matter, which may become a laughing matter sometime after the Crisis! What Crisis!
In the lives of children, the transformation of molehills into mountains can occur many times over the course of a single day, as the small ego fails to deal with the frustrations of the living process. All these crises leave their scars since, for the most part, there is no conclusiveness possible in all these ‘critical matters’. The usual conventional life is a plethora of occasions lurching from one crisis to the next; film entertainment is about endless emergency situations. Crisis! What Crisis!
The [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] can illumine us to see ourselves moving as these absurd characters and in this seeing we are released beyond the gesture of this absurdity.
