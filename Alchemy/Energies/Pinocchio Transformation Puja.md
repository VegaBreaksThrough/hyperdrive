Examine all the influences on most people at the relational life level, the push/pull sympathies; influences from friends (peer pressure etc.), circumstances (keeping up with the Joneses etc.), structures (society or family expectations etc.). These push/ pull influences can become boundaries imposed by others.

The connections we have in the world are like strings that bind. Many binding qualities actually appear as pretty strings, desirable things, appealing. it is obviously important to outshine all levels of being 'bound', revealing the true nature of the things we submit to in fear.

By energetically cutting the controlling strings, we are able to stand free, particularly of 'external' control.
