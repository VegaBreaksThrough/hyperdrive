Whatever the level of concern, to be concerned is always such a worry, since we are always trapped within the circumstance of our concern. To be so trapped is as if we are dangling our line in troubled waters awaiting a karmic bite. The karmic jaws of our interest and worries will consume us in the end. To be concerned about anything is to be designing our own death. “But!”, is the retort, “there is much to be concerned about, especially these days, everything is such a worry!” Not even if our whole family and half the neighbours suddenly disappeared in a puff of smoke is that reason enough to be concerned. Such an event would most likely pose certain life level difficulties that could be a worry, but truly, not even this level of loss should ever become a matter of concern. Why? Because it is not our concern. Whose is it? Not ours.
To be concerned with or concerned about presupposes a cosmology wherein we are this poor nano something, someone who is faced with a ‘cosmic machine of death’. It is true, the cosmos consumes untold apparent beings by the nano-second but it is not only a machine of death, it is also teleological. In fact, the cosmos is obviously teleological. We may not be able to discern ultimate purposes of the Divine, it is sufficient to see such purposes exist and when we really see that the universe is unfolding as a veritable happy concinnity, there is a relaxation of the bodily being. So, it follows, we may hope that personal disasters are not in our ‘stars’, yet if such a design is the case, it is not our concern and to be so concerned as an interest or a worry only deepens the so-called cause for worry. There is [[A0326|entropy]]? The universe is ‘falling apart’; what a worry! We are falling apart, all our friends are falling apart, everyone is mad, should any of this be cause for concern? There are no accidents, every moment is a matter of design and it is simply up to us whether this design is in the form of falling foul of the [[law of sacrifice]] in which case the present madness continues or something more pleasant may arise if we strive in our loving to do always and only what pleases God. What we see in all of this matter of concern is that we are meditating upon what is ‘arising’ and not upon the Divine. We should have never allowed our behaviour to be modified by what is ‘arising’, which is what is happening in our concern and interest. Instead, our behaviour must always be simply flowing out of our unbroken meditation upon happiness, which meditation is happiness itself, wherein there is obviously never a reason for concern or interest, or worry, such aspects cannot exist within the happy outshining of Only God. Which is itself, utterly unreasonable. There is no reason for concern and no reason for happiness, the whole cosmos is simply unreasonable. Is that happy?
