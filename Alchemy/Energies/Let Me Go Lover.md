It is common in the lyrics of popular music to speak of possession by someone who was a one time 'lover'. The songs speak of a folklore of long-time distress between so called 'lovers', phrases such as, "...release me...", "...take these chains...", "...she shot her man..." and the old 50's song, "Let me go lover" speak volumes of the troubles and strife that men and women get themselves into. It is clear that failed relationships are often hard to be rid of, both in terms of the moods that remain in the forms of sorrow, regret, guilt, nostalgia and so on, but also by the actual haunting presence of the erstwhile 'lover'. *Let Me Go Lover* releases us in a flash of blinding illumination, so that all of this lingering soap opera fades into utter forgetfulness. "What's my name?" "Who are you?"
