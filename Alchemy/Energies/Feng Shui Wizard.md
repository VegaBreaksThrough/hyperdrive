Based on an ancient Chinese system, believed to use the laws of both Heaven (astronomy) and earth (geography) to optimize the spatial arrangement and orientation of worldly patterns and structures in relation to the flow of energy and life-force. This is all aimed at achieving harmony within the environment and positive chi/qi. This Energy is intended to function as the perfect presentation of this tradition – the way of wind and water.

---

_Feng_ _Shui_ is the way of wind and water. _The_ _Wizard Wand_ _Feng_ _Shui_ is the perfect presentation of this tradition which is essentially about the optimal arrangement of the worldly structures to enhance the flow of _life force._
