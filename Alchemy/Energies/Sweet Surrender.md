In order to surrender 'whole-bodily' one needs to 'trust in the Divine, to know that we are held and loved as part of this whole Unity. Sweet Surrender helps us to transcend the ego, the struggle, and allow the “mystery of our heart awakening to unfold in Grace.

> [!ABSTRACT] Ingredients
> Cinnamon, Wintergreen, Lavender, Sweet Orange, Australian Sandalwood

The intention of this Alqvinergia is to remind us that everything appears in our life as part of a perfect plan. It unveils reality, giving us space to learn to operate with "intelligence", and inviting us to enjoy this learning process that is life. Guiding us from situation to situation, from lesson to lesson, until we arrive at the truth: Happiness. Beings free of contractions. The Surrender to Grace is a loving call to transform every experience by only trusting our Heart. 

> [!tip] How to use this Alqvinergia? 
> First thing in the morning, place a drop of Sweet Surrender on your pressure points or in the palm of your hand to immerse yourself in the aroma with deep breaths. A few drops can be added in your diffuser to enjoy in your room, during your meditation or yoga practice. Let the gifts of each day surprise you!
