This _water_ invites us to submit to growth by ordeal, demonstrating honour and trustworthiness. It encourages us to transcend old patterns of relating and to develop appropriate relationships with men and women. The moral relationship serves the awakening to the *[[Truth]]* of us, the confession of *Only God*, or our dependence on the _Grace_ of the _Infinite_ _Divine._

This _water_ also helps in the release of childhood patterns or emotions which are 'locked' within the adult and are only released finally in _Enlightenment;_ These early patterns of childhood adaptation can cease to be the forms of our relationship through the discipline of happiness even though they are still present by tendency.

In essence, this _water_ helps bring about the _Healing_ and _Transformation_ of those aspects of ourselves which we should no longer resort to in our relationships with others, enabling us to bring about higher adaptations.
