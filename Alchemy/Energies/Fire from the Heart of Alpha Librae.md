1. ### (LIBRA) FIRE FROM THE HEART OF ALPHA LIBRAE
    

This Fire from the Heart is the balance between night and day, when the Sun passes in front of the Constellation of Libra and approaches Alpha Librae it  ponders its energy and gives way to the Autumn equinox, balances emotions, allows the self to manifest itself in society with balance, blocks contractions and dilutes them, shows us the consciousness of the social being, overcoming the confinement of the self. It's time to summarize. It is the soft fire we feel with the sun on the horizon. (The Smile of Horus)  
  

KEYWORD: Consciousness of the Social Being.
