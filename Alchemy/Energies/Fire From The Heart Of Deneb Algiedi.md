1. ### (CAPRICORN, ANKH) FIRE FROM THE HEART OF DENEB ALGIEDI
    

Submission and service to God, protection and nourishment of the spirit, surrender, perseverance, sacrifice as an act of love. When wealth is generously overflowing, and it is driven towards philanthropy, there is a direct connection with the Fire from the Heart of Deneb Algiedi. This Fire is an anchor for the spirit, gives security and spiritual support.  
  

KEYWORD: Sacrifice as an act of Love.
