It appears from information garnered from genetic science that the genes for insect legs and human legs are virtually the same. In this, we see that we are fully made of many things and probably enjoy a so-called distant kinship with some fat, hairy spider.

  

What this suggests is that all manner of creatures, and perhaps even plants, are part of our primitive psyche. Since most individuals demonstrate a quality far less than the fabled “human being,” their degradation into these primitive psychic relationships gives rise to associations somehow manifested in flesh form—i.e., we see some people in their psychic readings appearing more like insects than human beings. This is particularly true of cultures that are highly ritualized and thereby almost totally divorced from open-faced intimate association.

  

The drink _Metamorphosis_ is therefore designed to allow evolutionary development to be lifted closer, at least, to the human possibility. If we transcend the primitive insect mind, we transcend the insect form that we somehow bodily manifest.

  

The key to this description, of course, is that the body is the past and reflects the pattern of the psyche. If we share our genetic structure with insects, we also share the psyche of insects.
