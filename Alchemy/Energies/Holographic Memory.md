“Holographic [[Memory]]” is an extension of the holographic brain concept, suggesting that [[Memory]] is not stored in specific locations but is instead encoded in patterns of interactions across the brain, like a hologram.

1. **Key Features**:

• Any small piece of a hologram contains the entire image, albeit with less clarity. Similarly, [[Memory]] in the brain may involve distributed representations, where fragments can reconstruct the whole.

• This idea explains phenomena like [[Memory]] resilience (e.g., partial [[Memory]] retention after brain damage).

2. **Applications**:

• It’s been explored in **neural networks** and **artificial intelligence**, influencing how distributed computing systems mimic [[Memory]] storage and processing.
