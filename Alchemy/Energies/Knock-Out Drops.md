The wonderful Knock-Out Drops arose as a case of serendipity. A number of Infinity associates were engaged in an experiment of watching at the m**i**cro- level when a glass of water was transformed into [[Mighty Atom]]. When this process occurred, within a few seconds they all fell fast asleep, indicating that we had something akin to a power**fu**l sedative to calm the restless in their nocturnal chambers. So take a long draft of Knock-Out Drops and be close to your bed when you do or you may find yourself almost anywhere when you recover your senses in the morning.
