Conventional life is but a mere ritual enactment, a mechanical process. In so living, either singular aspects of the being appear to take on a mechanical form or the entire being is ‘robotised’. The essence of such electronic transformation is the total absence of feeling across the entire being. We see across nation after nation: clockwork husband marries clockwork bride, presenting mechanical personae to one another until they rust. Dissolving these structures of the body-mind-self can restore the possibility of real intimate association.
