La [[Glande Pinéale]] est la glande endocrine qui le régit. Elle produit de la mélatonine, qui influence notre rythme circadien ET notre sexualité en effet, une augmentation de sa calcification résulte en une baisse de production de mélatonine, ce qui désinhibe les organes sexuels.

La mélatonine gère notre rythme circadien et est intimement liée au rythme du [[A0437|soleil]] (journées et saison) Elle gère notre sommeil ainsi que notre cycle annuel (l’hibernation pour les ours, notre ralentissement, …)

Structure et composition de la calcification:

Corpora arenacea ([singular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammatical_number): corpus arenaceum,[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-1) also called brain sand or acervuli[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-3) or psammoma bodies[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-:0-4) or pineal concretions[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-:0-4)) are [calcified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcium) structures in the [pineal gland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pineal_gland) and other areas of the [brain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brain) such as the [choroid plexus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choroid_plexus). Older organisms have numerous corpora arenacea, whose function, if any, is unknown. Concentrations of "brain sand" increase with [age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ageing), so the pineal gland becomes increasingly visible on [X-rays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-rays) over time, usually by the third or fourth decade. They are sometimes used as anatomical landmarks in [radiological examinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiology).[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-5)

Chemical analysis shows that they are composed of [calcium phosphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcium_phosphate) (later characterized as [hydroxyapatite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroxyapatite)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-6)), [calcium carbonate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcium_carbonate), [magnesium phosphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnesium_phosphate), and [ammonium phosphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonium_phosphate).[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-7) Recently, [calcite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcite) deposits have been described as well.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpora_arenacea#cite_note-8)
