With beguiling sweetness the sirens of Greek Mythology would lure sailors, irresistibly, who came near their influence, to a certain death upon treacherous rocks. The snake at the foot of a tree lures an unsuspecting bird to lunch, a fatal invitation. We will call this fatalistic charm Blue Voodoo. It is the capacity to appear innocent and true to the naive and foolish, and to not be simply interested in gaining some small advantage through this capacity, but to be bent on the destruction of the beguiled. This destruction is fascinating to the wickedness of the charmer. They glory in the agony and utter degradation of their victim. 

The drink, _Blue_ _Voodoo_ dives deep into the human soul and consumes this vast sickness.
We like to think that we have nothing to do with such charming, but in fact there are many individuals within our society who invoke this capacity in order to control and ultimately possess the souls of others. This interplay is an extension of ‘How to Win Friends and Influence People’ (Dale Carnegie), which is essentially about control, in order to exploit any number of individuals for the benefit of the one at the centre of the power play. This type of influence is totally immoral and any level of immorality can easily escalate to the extreme cases wherein the one influenced is actually destroyed for the entertainment of the fiendish. We can only be protected from such degradation of the human soul and all such hellish influence through awakened consciousness. [[Alice]], be careful.
