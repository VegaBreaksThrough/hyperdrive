The Form of the Good is the mysterious highest Form and the source of all the other Forms. It is a [Platonic ideal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonic_ideal "Platonic ideal").
There is an ancient anecdotal tradition that Plato gave a public lecture entitled "On the Good" which so confused the audience that most walked out. At the end of the lecture Plato said to those hearers who remained: 'The Good is the One".
In the course of his famous [analogy of the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analogy_of_the_Sun "Analogy of the Sun"), Plato says that the Good is beyond being (ἐπέκεινα τῆς οὐσίας) in power and dignity.
In Plotinus' model of reality, the One is the cause of the rest of reality, which takes the form of two subsequent "[hypostases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypostasis_(philosophy_and_religion) "Hypostasis (philosophy and religion)")" or substances: [Nous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nous "Nous") and Soul ([psyché](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soul "Soul")).

If you are here it is because you no longer have to walk, the halo of my Light floods your Being, you pass through the gateway to Heaven, following the Truth that has brought you here. I am the Light that frees you from the weight that envies what you Love, the confinement of the [[Fear]] of what your Soul longs for. I embrace you in completeness and your whole image dissolves in my Light. You have established in you the permanence of Good and the [[Powers]] in you are unified in Light and Life. You have returned to the intimate embrace of my communion. 

The Angel Of Good is the final door that expels all the torments of our Soul, it is the loving embrace of divinity that welcomes you home from which you have never left. A home where the flame of your soul is synchronized with the fire of the universal spirit, it is a sacred invitation to abandon the evil seed of envy and greed in the search for eternity, to enter you cannot desire, you must be Reverent to the Truth, you must observe and listen to the Good in all things, you must be silent and receive, the Life and the Light that are the Father and the Son of our Realization. It is time for absolute surrender, all that is "other" flies away and disappears in the Beautiful halo which embraces the total vision of all things. 

_-Sirius Aor_
