The usual man or woman is not ordinary; they are extraordinary, they are magicians and the usual mind is their magician’s hat. The world they ‘generate’ and inhabit is Roger Rabbit Land. In other words, they pull everything out of the air like a magician pulls a rabbit out of a hat - we do not know where it comes from. This can be characterised by saying that they are living in a subjective-objective falsity. The ordinary appears when the subjective-objective dissolves, and the obviousness of ‘relationship’ appears and all is revealed as a [[Feeling Dimension]], in and as reality, no longer appearing out of the ‘blue’. 

(N.B. Subjective is the supposed ‘inner’ part people are talking about, that is inaccessible to all except the given individual. Objective is the supposed ‘outer’ part that is apparently accessible to all individuals. With the advent of ‘science’, the objective only had credibility because apparently everyone could agree about the so-called objective part. This reduced the so-called subjective interior part to an inferior position, ultimately leading to the erroneous position of supposing that our ‘own’ consciousness was a mere epiphenomenon of objective events, i.e. chemical reactions, thus reducing our lives to a total absurdity.)
