**Angry** **Distortions** **Of** **The** **[[Serpent Power|Serpent Power]]** ([[Looking Glass War]] & [[Thru A Glass Darkly]]) 

The _Looking_ _glass_ is the symbol of the self fascinated individual or indeed the common dross of mankind. The _drink,_ _Looking_ _Glass_ _War_ _Healer_ heals the anger possessed character of the self-fascinated. It dissolves the great serpent of anger wretching out from below the navel and restores the fiercely agitated soul to peaceful quiescence.
