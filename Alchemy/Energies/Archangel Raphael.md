**

Is the Archangel of healing and good assistance to the sick.  His Consciousness can help us recognize the spiritual root of the problems that cause us illness, even if they have not manifested on the physical plane.

According to Adi Da Samraj, the ego is the only true disease that exists.  Therefore, the Energy of this Archangel invites us to heal the aspects that prevent the manifestation of the complete and perfect health of the Self.

Keywords: healing, vitality, knowledge.

Archangel Raphael is the supreme healer in the angelic realm and his chief role is to support, heal, and guide in matters involving health. Raphael means “God heals” or “He who heals”. In Catholicism, he is Saint Raphael, the patron of healing and physicals. (Words associated with this Archangel are healing, communication, knowledge and vision.)
