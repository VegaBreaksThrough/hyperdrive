There is a widespread belief that positive thinking is actually a power that brings us only the good things of life. The corollary to this position is that, “thinking makes it so”. There is little doubt that the many folk who write all those ‘very positive’ books that abound in bookstores, “You Too Can Make A Billion Dollars”, along with several testimonials of a few lucky students who reaped the rewards from having read the book, certainly think that, “thinking positive makes it so.” 

However, apart from anything else, the pie is running low on pieces or at least is not infinite, therefore there must be losers in order for there to be winners. So, despite all the assertions to the positive, does “thinking make anything so”? Well the negative position of dwelling on negative courses of actions would seem to be a precursor to much criminal behaviour. The negative thinking in this case is becoming so self-intoxicated that our inhibitions to certain courses of action are apparently overcome. Yet, even in the negative position, can thinking make it so? It is true that this world is a realm of manifest desire, which is to say, what we dwell upon tends to come about. Yet what are we actually dwelling upon? Isn’t our whole stance a summation of our desiring, which generally includes a relatively safe mediocre existence? So, rather than some cause and effect operating in the power of positive thinking, what we actually have is that both positive and negative thinking are simply arising out of our whole position and are merely re-enforced by aspects within our social sphere.

The belief in the ‘power of positive thinking’ fails to understand that thinking of any kind is never a free activity, but is bound to meditation upon the self or simply arising out of our self-intoxification and is therefore only an expression of our karma. Therefore the belief in the power of positive thinking is merely a superstition, i.e. something we think we need to do in order to gain life’s bounties, like the cargo cult in New Guinea. Thoughts themselves are only subtle objects arising which we are falsely claiming as our own. Thoughts do not belong to anyone anymore than a flock of black birds wheeling across the sky, belong to any of us.

What is missing from the superstitious position is the failure to grasp what is actually occurring. Therefore it is realisation that is missing from all affirmation stances. Even our laughter, a supposed affirmation, in the ordinary situation is actually a painful negation, because there is no appreciation of what is actually the case. Therefore it is merely ‘gremlin’ behaviour, like the ‘reaction formation’ of Freudian psychology, where we say and do positive things in relation to, say our children, when actually we are trying to hide negative emotions, where we actually have thoughts of harming them. Without realisation we are always struggling with our demons. We have a social agreement, “you keep all your stuff under control and I’ll keep all my stuff under control”, which is a difficult ask, like New Year’s resolutions. 

The only way beyond all this dilemma is to realise that the supposed affirmer, “yes, yes, I can, I can, I will, I will” does not exist, there is absolutely no such one. In the realisation that the world has no self-nature we have immediately fallen into our true disposition of self-sacrifice. We have admitted at this point that we do not live by our own devices. Most people however seek solutions within the gross dimension and a few others erroneously trust things in the subtle dimension in seeking help from apparently benign subtle entities. These apparent entities are obviously not realised beings, as can be seen by the fact they are still wandering around in the subtle dimension and as such they are fixed forms trapped in a circumstance, hiding out from God in the same way apparent gross entities are. Further, the subtle situation does not allow us to generate sufficient intelligence for the awakening to realisation. Therefore neither the gross nor subtle dimensions are hopeful of release from our dilemma of existence, but are instead superstitious nonsense and if we fail to take care we could be lost in either dimensions forever, even if we get to be a ‘golden god in a golden world’. So, it is hopeless to affirm any level of experience subtle or gross.

Thus we must turn to the Truth of us, which is not to be found in the gross or subtle dimensions, both are deluding, but instead is a dimensionless awakening that occurs when we grow tired of the endless possibilities for experience and therefore discover that the world is a corpse and only appears to be something else through our desiring. In this discovery, we at last surrender all of our life of experience into the source of that experience, which is to be found on the right side of the heart as the false generation of attention, which is the false identification with our own radiance. At last we are able to confess that we are that within which all of this experiential dimension is arising and that is the realisation of Only God. In this we are simply and forever alive as the life-positive presence, that is love, that is the living Divine and here there can never be any downside. Therefore love is the only true affirmation.
