# Tao Energy House: The Alchymeic Manual

## 1. What Is The 'Tao'?

Tao is a Chinese word meaning 'way', 'path' or 'route'. Within the context of traditional Chinese philosophy and religion, Tao is a metaphysical concept originating with Laozi that gave rise to Taoism.

Tao signifies the primordial essence or fundamental nature of the universe. The foundational text, the _Tao Te Ching_, explains that Tao is not a 'name' for a 'thing' but the underlying natural order of the universe. It is "eternally nameless" and distinguished from the countless 'named things' which are its manifestations.

Tao is the flow of the universe, the essence or pattern behind the natural world that keeps the universe balanced and ordered. It relates to _qi_ or _chi_—the essential energy of action and existence. It is a non-dual concept; the greater whole from which all individual elements derive. The object of spiritual practice is to 'become one with the Tao' or to achieve 'effortless action.'
