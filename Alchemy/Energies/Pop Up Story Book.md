**“Can’t See the Wood for the Trees”** or **“Oh! What a Lovely Little Freddy Bear”**

The mind is the author of the ‘pop-up world’ that we simply awake to find each day as the display of our ‘play’. The mind is not a ‘something’, however, but it is an activity of identification and differentiation, all but totally, with the pop-up world as it merely seems to be. This activity encloses us as a pop-up cosmic googy. The pop-up world is not passive to this activity of self-enclosure but rather exacts a measure of [[Just desserts]] for acting unable or unwilling to penetrate the illusion of mere appearances. Our ignorance is all but complete.
At ‘birth’ the pop-up storybook is projected out of the heart. This world is written in the form of reaction to the process of conventional embodiment, which is seen as enclosure within a body and the body enclosed within a greater universe, the implication of which is insignificance and death. The reaction is the story; the story is the reaction. All stories are about time - about beginnings, middles and ends. It’s about time to open our storybook and find popup Jack and pop-up Jill about to enter the dark pop-up woods and discover all manner of pop-up horribles. It’s about time for our minding to begin. Time is the landscape set out as a potential for attraction and revulsion. What we are merely attracted to destroys us, in time, in the end. 
Living from the point of view of mind is already in reaction. The usual person is this reaction already fitted to the emotional stances of labile dramas or buttoned-down stoicism. In any case, behind the dewy eye, the grimace, the stiff upper lip and so on, is the stuff of fear, and on this stuff rests all the dark and vast bewildering, terrifying and shocking array of the proverbial ‘Pandora’s Box’. Fear, sorrow and anger are the precursors to the content and style of the pop-up story, and there is an infinity of embroideries on this theme of fear, sorrow and anger and their terrible offspring; shame, disgust, greed, envy, jealousy, guilt, and on and on, ever deeper into the dark woods, ever more is the light of day occluded as the piercing eyes and sounds of unknown beasts sharpen our cliff-hanging paranoia to a knife edge of exaggerated reaction, the road runner becomes the road rager. This is the degraded view of mind. The ‘casual’ moods of the usual person are communicated within this pop-up story as a daemon of murderous intent, a ‘Freddy’ for all to feel, a ‘Freddy Bear’ for all to hold. 
There is no humanness at the indulged level of mind wherein there is no humour or freedom from the implication of this worldly presentation to the ‘born being’. There is no freedom where there is no humour. To be the mind is to remain forever in an ever - degrading pop-up story of the sub-human eating the sub-human. As long as we feel we have the right to indulge our moods we cannot grow beyond the tormenting limits of the sub-human. In this world the human has yet to appear as a loving presence drawing the all into the form that is reality, that is the Truth of us. The essence of the human is the humour that is reality.
Our true form here does not spring out of a pop-up storybook but it is rather beyond all themes as mindless embodiment. Such a disposition is akin to burning the storybook page by page in the fire of consciousness. To be human is to be restored to the humour of mindless embodiment, forever beyond the mere indulgence of reactions. The story in Truth has become unnecessary through outshining its principle of the adventure in survival, lurching from one emergency to the next, or the childish mentality of ‘go for it’, seen in the ‘Discovery channel’ mode. For the usual person, even if a heroic slayer of beasts, at the end of the story the slayer is always slain, and in death the story book is closed and is returned to the heart, awaiting the strength to get up and do it again. As the one awakened to mindless embodiment, we are no longer generating the fixed qualities of stories, and the pop-up story book is at last outshone in the Wondrous Blissful Humour of the Great God beyond all the cosmic planes of discovery spinning in time and we are dissolved even beyond our immortal form, lost eternally in love-bliss.1 While still presented in association with the cosmic displays, we are alive as the present immortal, totally overwhelming the destiny of our ‘born being’, until it passes out of sight.
