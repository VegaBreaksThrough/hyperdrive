There is no difference between ‘feelings’ and ‘emotions’; they are equivalent. On the other hand there is much difference between ‘emotioning’ and feeling. ‘Emotioning’ or ‘feelingsing’ is simply how things affect you, i.e. they are simply a reading of bodily states. Feeling is being alive as the total pattern of relations, which is to say, totally surrendered into the mystery of existence. Therefore feeling transcends the bodily disposition and is the relationship to this vision, as this vision, of the present immortal.
