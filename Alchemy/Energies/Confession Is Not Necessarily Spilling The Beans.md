Confession is not trying to come out with something; either some sentiment thing or some guilt thing. Both of these positions are filled with egoic concern. Confession is simply happiness itself and this is not in the sense that we feel that we are happy, it is not self-conscious, but simply our heart’s response to abiding in the Mystery. It is therefore a matter of being; not becoming. In our confession of happiness we are intrinsically recognizing the falsity of the conventional point of view wherein we are posed as actors and doers. So be happy and live in the fullness of this confession.
