The [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] invites us to submit to growth by ordeal, demonstrating honour and trustworthiness. It encourages us to transcend old patterns of relating and to develop appropriate relationships with men and women. The moral relationship serves the awakening to the [[Truth]] of us, the confession of Only God, or our dependence on the grace of the infinite Divine.
The release of childhood patterns or emotions that are locked within the adult are released finally in enlightenment. These early patterns of childhood adaptation can cease to be the forms of our relationship through the discipline of happiness, even though they are still present by tendency. In essence, this note is a reminder of those aspects of ourselves which we should no longer resort to in our relationships with others, enabling us to bring about higher adaptations.
