The elucidation of our true heart’s desire begins with the given that there is God and that this is not properly a matter of argument and never will be. Only the absurd pundits of popular science and their obtuse following imagine that life, for example, begins with some clever little molecule swimming in an alphabet soup. Without the given, there is God, only such absurdities can follow. So, all the absurdities aside, what is our true heart’s desire? It is not only true that there is God, but also that there is Only God. It follows that our true heart’s desire is to realise Only God as whole body absorption, to be lost in the swoon of love forever. 
So, since this is true, how matters actually are, why is it that we have strayed so far from our true heart’s desire and why is it so difficult, even when we are enlightened by the Truth, to yield up our straying? Why is it that there is such equivocation about that which is only obvious? What is this wrestling in the dark with our troubled heart? Could it be that we are so weak and diseased that we are the proverbial leaf blown on the wind? In our ‘weakness’ our structure lures possessions of every conceivable nature that control every aspect about us, we are indeed ‘[[Plagued By Daemons]]!’ What a sorry state! Many of us even entertain madness as a possible solution! What is it that we are wrestling with? When we discover this at last we see that from the madness of our uncertainties we believe, “Yes, there is God; and, yes, there is me.” Ehhhhhh! Wrong answer! There is no you!! When we live as though we exist we have to separate ourselves out from the rest of all that is, as some supposed monad. This is simply not the case! When we yield up the notion of self, the independent monad, we are yielding up the supposed interior of the world, our ‘inner place’ we retreat to in our self-madness; thinking, thinking, thinking ourselves into a ‘tangled tale’! When we see the falsity of our self-position, we may suddenly find that we only appear here, in this place, as the total pattern of all that is. Thus, we only see the way that it is when we are able to see ourselves simultaneously along with everything else and this is beyond any possibility of judgement and judgement is the essence of self-nature. When this is true of us, we say that ‘we are saved’ and this is the confession that we do not exist as independent beings and this is also the confession of happiness. 
When we live as the confession of happiness we allow this confession to purify us of our ‘weakness’ and disease. This then is the happiness of the way to liberation, when we have allowed all the symbols of our sacrifice to be consumed in the fire of Only God. Do not fail to take heed! You have everything to lose and nothing to gain.
