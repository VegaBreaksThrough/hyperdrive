Comment on Haiku
	- It is best that we realise the eternity of the present moment: Haiku is its revelation.
	- The demand of Haiku is the vision of the present moment as an absolute eternity.

---

Group. Three oranges 
Clinging late upon the tree 
A gift, still glowing

---

I am consumed whole 
Spring is breathing me to ash
Grandpa Orchids bloom

---

River of worries
The piquant blushing peach is 
Tauntingly sublime

---

The Hill grows steeper 
Last year the flat road Ah! So
Easy on the legs

---

The Game explains rules 
To the children’s flow of tears
Scraped knees listening

---

The smoking toaster 
Confirms anticipating 
Golden toast is dashed

---

I’m moving house
Is this small photograph too
Heavy to carry?

---

My sweet darkened room 
Fiery sun beats down
I am out of town

---

This present moment 
Will not be found at all 
Nor come when called!

---

The only profound 
This elusive present moment
Is not lost or found

---

Sip Sip Sip and 
Sweetly see this delicate
And enticing tea

---

Clouds; gold and blue grey halted 
Echoed fluting birds: the expanse vaulted

---

A flock of black birds 
Alight upon the wires 
Looks like Sheet Music

---

Calm Waters, Two Swans 
Drifting, One flies off,
The other drifts with the One...
