11:11 (Gateway)

This _drink_ begins with a message given by a car radio clock, in that each time we entered the vehicle perhaps over a number of consecutive occasions, the time was always 11:11 . Having being knocked on the head some four times, it is time to respond and this response coincided with a particularly cramped weekend with respect to the flow of _energy_, which was in the Southern Hemisphere a particularly cramped Winter Solstice. A number of years ago, this _11:11_ message also supposedly coincided with some gateway opening, therefore all of this somehow put together pointed to this time and this _energy_ was to be an opening into a greater receptive possibility, where we could therefore be touched more deeply, through our awakening intuition, by the _Divine_ presence. It is therefore a lifting through and up into a higher and more intense response to the living process and this _drink 11:11_ _gateway water_ commemorates and instigates this awakening occasion.
