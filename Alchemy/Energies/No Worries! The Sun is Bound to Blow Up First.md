Is this spiritual life tough or what? The spiritual stuff makes the ‘sperm’ race look like a walk in the park. Maybe we should wait until the sun blows up? Many would agree that waiting or forgetting about it would be the best policy. 
This guy in India was walking home one day when this rock spoke to him announcing that it was actually ‘Lord Shiva’ and that he should be taken home and worshipped. This guy says, “No way! You’ll only want me to do all sorts of rituals and if I forget you’ll punish me!” Many of us would fail to have such wisdom, because a talking rock would arouse our curiosity and immediately we would be deep in ‘trouble.’
It would seem therefore that spiritual life could be a matter of choice, that it is not really our business, it is someone else’s, maybe it’s God’s business? “God has his plans maybe, but what has that got to do with me?” It is true, we do not have to worry about God’s plans, but we are implicated in them through this law of consequences that operates in this place. We co-operate or we get steamrollered. So, does a choice exist?
The thing is that ordinary life is not conscious and is therefore not happy. Without consciousness there is no enjoyment, only consequences. Well, yes it sounds a tough call and no one remembers opting for this kind of deal and who gets to win at this spiritual life? So far, not many. This is why God has given up waiting until the sun blows up and is coming to force the issue. Apparently the spiritual game was a little on the tough side and so God is coming to mankind. Wait a minute isn’t this a re-run on the ‘talking Lord Shiva rock’? The difference is, this ‘rock’ walks us home.
Because God is coming to mankind there is a forced evolution taking place which is ‘lifting’ those who respond, from a development of ‘very primitive existence’ to an availability that would not otherwise take place for millions of years. Clearly, this opportunity is being offered to all, but will all respond in the end, or only a few? The rest? A fate we would rather not think about or maybe the sun will blow up first? “I tried, but the sun blew up first!”
