There is an actual product called 'Glastron', which is a machine that can be strapped over our eyes and ears giving us an intense sensory experience while watching a DVD movie. It takes an evolutionary adaptation to not get 'programmed' or lost in the cyber world when engaging this machine. Inspired by this equipment, the Glastron Vial is inviting an adaptation and capacity to be greater than machinery and other forms of virtual reality. In our use of computers and the like we need to be the conscious presence animating creative control.
