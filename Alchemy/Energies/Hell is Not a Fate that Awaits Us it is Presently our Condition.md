In the condition that is real life, everything must serve. That which serves everything is love, and that which serves draws everyone into their real possibility, which is to be free, but utterly, of the mortal implication of this conventional place. Apart from love as service, all else seeks to exploit the self and other as self-seeking.
The world does not support real life, but instead gathers up our life-force in the waste of trivial pursuit. Only consciousness transcends the trivial, or that which wastes life. Therefore, it follows that we should not be anywhere that consciousness does not dictate or be doing anything that consciousness does not dictate and this is the conservative nature of love.
The conventional stance is that of the whole life becoming ‘habitualised’ and the ‘habitualised’ life is simply consoling the self. To be ‘habitualised’ is simply to be unconscious, merely responding reflexively to the conditions of existence. Seeking consolations suggests that these things that are consoling are the things we deserve or the things we add to our life that apparently make us feel good. Yet these things do not actually make us feel good or make us happy, but merely lead us into intoxicated states, wherein we only think everything is wonderful, even while, perhaps, some daemon is eating at our heart. Truly the only thing we ever deserve in the failure to practice real life, is ‘hell’. ‘Hell’ is where everything is pressing up against us, ultimately to the point that even our body parts are a confused intermingled mass, as pieces of everybody else are fused with us, a veritable bloody heap of writhing insanity: The deep red that is ‘hell’.
Therefore, we must serve all of this conventional vision that we call our life, which is already in a hellish condition, which only the force of consciousness can purify and transform. We serve truly only as the living presence of the Divine, wherein all that this vision amounts to is served simply and most truly by this presence.
