We cannot be reading the writings looking for some message. There is no message as such. The relationship to the writings is not one of gleaning. A message implies something that can be taken from the writings and perhaps initiate some type of future action; the gleaning process. The writings, however, are more like ‘doorways’ that must be passed through. In the world of the information superhighway we are habituated to reflexively regard all writings, for the most part, as merely ‘informational’, which may bear some importance or not, depending upon what are the engagements of our life. The form of the ‘poem’ invites a ‘wider response’, which lies beyond its so-called informational content; this is why it is said that “the poets are next to God.” However, [[Ecstatic Communication]] is not written by a ‘someone’, unlike most poetry, and yet ecstatic writings are closer to poetry than all the instructional ‘cook books’. 
So, the writings are an invitation to pass through a particular ‘doorway’ in order to be ‘relieved’ of some ‘aspect’ of our conventional living. This implies that conventional living has a structure associated with it, going right down to the ‘DNA’ and therefore the writings, properly related to, are a transforming power if we become willing to release our ordinary stance of gleaning, which is non-participatory, into the participatory relationship of opening to ‘entrance’ into all the doorways of the writings. The action of [[Real Intelligence]] is just such a participatory involvement and this is an immediate recognition that shifts our ‘entire structure’, we are in no sense the same after such a process. 
Therefore if we remain clinging to the ‘arrogance’ of ‘[[Gremlins and Angels|gremlin]] perspectives’ we will ‘apprehend’ nothing but our own little ‘gremlin face’ staring back at us and we will totally miss the import of the writings. There is an evolutionary shift in our structure that can take place by taking the writings to heart. Therefore, we cannot just read them once; we must become ‘imbued’ with them, until we have passed beyond the limitations that are addressed by some particular ‘writing’. This is not to say, learn the writings by heart, but rather be ‘freed’ of all our ‘present limitation’. This present addendum is also another doorway required to be passed through. The writings are literal at ‘base’ and are not merely ‘allegorical’ or ‘metaphorical’ and what is therefore felt and seen about them may only have been initiated by the verbal aspect and does not in any sense end there in some verbal quagmire. By passing through the writings, we really come to see how anything or everything actually is, including ourselves and this is in no sense merely knowing something, like a patient might know their diagnosis. Such a ‘knowing’ can never be radically transforming.
The ‘demand’ for the present ‘writing’ demonstrates the point of not ‘writing’ any further at present, except within the Metareflection, because the whole matter dealt with here is already addressed within the Scribbles: [[Dipsy Doodle is Coming to Town]], [[The Information Superhighway is a Battery Hen Scheme]] and elsewhere. Clearly these writings were not in the least ‘passed through’, but at best ‘only gleaned’. Therefore there is no point in ‘writing’ more until the present sequence of writings have been appropriately addressed and ‘passed through’, otherwise they are at best merely ‘entertaining’ and have not come to truly ‘matter’. So we must return to the writings perhaps many times, even pass through possible ‘boredom’ with them, until we pierce the enclosing ‘bubble of the gremlin mind’ into true seeing.
