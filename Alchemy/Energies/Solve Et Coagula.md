Solve et Coagula is an ancient practice, transmitted by word of mouth, which summarizes the teachings of Traditional Alchemy. 

It is an exercise brought from the East and has been used to allow the transformation and liberation of chaos in practitioners, which is obtained thanks to the presence of the Divine Current. 

The Solve Et Coagula wand was created with the intention of emulating this practice and supporting you to release within yourself, in a continuous and harmonious way, the chaos generated by karmic conditioning. It acts as a personalized machine completely adjusted to the process of its owner. 

In Solve et Coagula, described by Traditional Alchemy, three fundamental elements intervene: the Alkahest, the Sulfur and the Mercury. 

The Alkahest, known as the Universal Solvent, is the Divine force that orders and releases chaos in matter. 

Sulfur represents the awakening of Consciousness or discernment. 

 Mercury symbolizes the internal transformative potential of each individual.

When chaos is exposed to the Alkahest, an internal process begins that activates Sulfur and releases Mercury, thus awakening the Divine potential.

The union of these three elements forms the Rebis, a combination of Sulfur and Mercury that represents the culmination of the alchemical process. The Rebis opens the door to a new state of Consciousness, beyond the original chaos.
