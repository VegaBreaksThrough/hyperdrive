If we do not live in a state of grace we are in need of repentance and contrition. Living in a state of grace is to be continually opening ourselves to God possession. We do know if we are in disgrace don’t we? In disgrace we are self-possessed, which of course is the usual disposition of the common man. It is the only kind! The usual meanings attributed to repentance are always referring to ‘misdeeds’. Our misdeeds however, flow from our self-possession, which further invites all of the other possession which f lows endlessly from the ‘bottomless pit’. Living in self possession is living in a moral vacuum which invites all that is not moral to possess the ‘life, body and mind’ or in the old language, ‘Devilish things do possess thy soul’. 
The moral disposition is love as service, wherein we are truly a healing presence that draws all toward a state of grace. Therefore, to repent is to recognise our disgrace as a clear and certain observation of that one’s absurd actions and life filled with the terrible cruelties of the reactive disturbance that is the ever present lot of the self-possessed. The selfpossessed are quite literally murderers of the soul and clearly they are also destroyed by all other self-possessed presences. When our disgrace is seen to the point of feeling the pain of our own actions and the actions of others we simply find that we can no longer find the energy for the usual immoral life. 
Ordinarily we do not awaken to the moral life, because all the fiends of self-possession support one another through sentiments that suggest that those actually in disgrace are really loving and are the ‘good guys on the block’. Such a position is actually deluded and is fostered and maintained through an innate ignorance which creates a dreamy cosmos of false notions. Clearly, repentance is not simply a ‘change of mind’ like taking on ‘New Year’s resolutions’, which are the ‘New Year’s delusions’, since the usual person is not in a position to vow anything and what may look like successful vowing, is at best, an ‘oedipal demon’ clinging fiercely to, “I’ll show them!”

So repentance is the actual penetration of the illusions of self-possession, wherein the terrible pain of this possession is felt conclusively, which is to say that no more life-force can be diverted into self-possession. When the state of grace is established it arises spontaneously that we simply live our circumstances as acts of contrition. Clearly these acts of contrition are in no way self-conscious, since in grace we see we are not in any sense the doer of any action. Therefore for those who live in grace the acts of contrition can never accrue toward the ever growing kudos of some ‘good-guy ego’, but in a greater stead, live beyond self-consciousness forever, as a healing presence of the living Divine until we pass out of sight of this world, which is to say, it is no longer arising within our consciousness and we are literally lost in the swoon of love as the eternal presence of Only God.
