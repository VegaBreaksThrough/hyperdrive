1. ### (SCORPIO, THE SNAKE) FIRE OF THE HEART OF ANTARES (The Heart of the Scorpion)
    

Called a real star, it was one of the four directional stars of the sky, and more than 3000 years ago marked the Autumn equinox. Antares is the humility of the warrior, it is a red star that remembers the fire of the dawn of Mars, every two years Mars approaches Antares, in that encounter Mars is impregnated with the fire of Antares, renewing its forces, the Fire from the Heart of Antares gives strength of will to change, strengthens the character but with great humility, the force that is felt with that fire inspires respect, born of correctness, kindness, integrity, firmness and rigorousness but with much tenderness. It is the impulse to be reborn after the transformation. It symbolizes armies to liberate nations.  
  

KEYWORD: Character with Tenderness.
