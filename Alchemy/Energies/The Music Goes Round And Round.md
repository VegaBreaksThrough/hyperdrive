Music, the art of the Muses, has many aspects associated with its manifestation. Greatest among these is inspiration, and greatest among inspiration is Divine inspiration. Thus, those aspiring to be musicians of an artful kind could do nothing greater than to reach for the highest expression of the art, worthy of panegyrical acknowledgment, where each note is the heartfelt confession of only God.

  

The _Music Goes Round and Round_ helps us slay our clumsy levels of incompetence, like Perseus slays the Medusa, and therein we are raised, ears stretched, listening to the music of the spheres.
