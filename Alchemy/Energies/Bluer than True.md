‘Bluer than True’ relates to those functional areas beyond ‘True Blue’ and therefore refers to the higher mental intuitive, mystical opening and of course, this is the beginning of the awakening of the functions of the frontal brain, which ultimately allows the enjoyment of the higher Divine states. The higher mental functioning refers to those cognitions that arise out of the intuitive recognition of the Truth of us and refers to all levels of [[Ecstatic Communication]], which may include the written poetic forms of this communication. This higher mental functioning awakens beyond the self-conscious dimension of the lower mental and therefore begins to function as that which penetrates all forms of illusion, revealing and dissolving those lower communications that intrinsically bind us to limitation, the central theme of which is the fear of death. The perfect reception of the hermetic touch therefore continues the development initiated in the lower mental function and so we are looking at a reclamation of the higher functions above, which include all the fabled capacities: clairvoyance, clairaudience, clairsentience and so on, which themselves ultimately dissolve into omnipresence and omniscience. The omnipotence of this is simply the utter power of love, which is the all power of the ultimate Divine, Only God.
