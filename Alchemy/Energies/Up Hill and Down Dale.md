Consider the total surface of the earth and its many and varied climates and surface conditions: such as hot desert sands where the living soil has long ago blown away, the wild demonstration of erupting volcanoes, the splitting of the earth as earthquakes, the frozen dead waste lands and the vast collection of human parasites and their scaly dwellings and so on. Across this varied earth surface we are perhaps looking at a mirror reflection of the ‘micro world’ of the internal and external skin surface of the human body, especially how it might appear after years of self-indulgent living; for example the weeping ulcer, internal or external, bears a remarkable similarity to the volcano in the ‘external’ world. Therefore, as the human form deteriorates more rapidly in this world, so does the ‘external’ surface of the earth and the deep hidden disturbances within its interior. As we allow our own restoration on a large enough scale, so too can the earth pass through a healing process.
