“Just meditate on God and everything will be okay.” “Let go and let God.” These are just two of the admonitions that suggest we can escape our predicaments by simply following these edicts, but like Houdini no packages arrive with any step by step instructions, we are simply not told how to actually carry out these Jujitsu manipulations of the mind, or why these troublesome messages allow us to escape our bondage. “The universe loves and supports me at all times.” Have we seen anything like this on the news lately? What these admonitions and epithets show us is just how absolutely mad are those that buy such things and how opportunistic the manufacturers of this type of sludge are by preying upon the millions of new age simpletons.
These new agey admonitions and epithets suffer from the failure of all levels of our verbal communications, because as with all the usual speech, the speaker or writer does not suspect that these statements have no meaning. The statements only look a little like genuine communication, but along with the great mass of written material, they are empty. “Just meditate on God....” Perhaps many folk think they know what meditation is and what God is, but the truth is, they do not. Meditation is a thinking process, an act of pondering upon matters arising around some conceptual focus. Usually this activity is supposed to still the mind. We use the mind to still the mind!? This ‘stilling’ of the mind is supposed eventually to help us escape from where we are to someplace else, the else being better. Now clearly, if we are an uptight individual, sitting down quietly for a time may be beneficial, but the general notion that this act of meditation is somehow ‘spiritual’ is pure nonsense. Who is the one supposedly performing the Jujitsu of mind, called meditation, who is that one? Isn’t this the one who is in bondage to the mind, who is suggested into some form of mind space, where freedom or escape from bondage is supposed to be found? What we fail to see here is that the root of the mind is no great wonder, no release into some blissful heaven of our salvation, even in the subtle ascents. The root of mind is fear and no matter how far we might climb up our Tower of Babel, we will always remain anchored to the root of fear. Therefore, meditation as a method of mind manipulation in order to attain spiritual advancement or escape from the ultimate levels of our mundane existence is a con-job and at best we talk ourselves into some numb ‘emotional state’.
The ‘God’ part of the ‘admonition’ is easy to dispense with, since if we do not know God in the first instance there is no method of mind manipulation that can suddenly reveal what God is. So, the use of the word ‘God’ is empty and meaningless for those who sit in their separativeness trying to find what they are essentially avoiding. No one has any right to talk of God who does not already enjoy an intimacy with God, beyond any delusions of such an intimacy. So God is not anything we have in mind. So sitting down to ‘meditate on God’ is simply an arrogant and ignorant mind numbing activity. 
If we can use the word ‘meditation’ for some form of real activity, what would it be? Meditation would be something that someone who had passed through a ‘conversion process’ might engage. This ‘conversion process’ is a shift from the principle of self-survival, the adolescent stance, to the principle of self-sacrifice, which is the confession of happiness and this happiness is always already free, not seeking to be free. So real meditation would be simply f inding a moment to sit quietly in the present enjoyment of our confession of happiness and allowing such communion to consume our entire existence. Real meditation is therefore divine communion with the always already presence of Only God
