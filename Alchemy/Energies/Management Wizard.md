We all have a massive array of things to 'hold' on a daily basis—some more than others... How we manage these things largely determines the amount of free energy we have in our lives left over for the 'important things'—like contemplating the process of Divine Awakening. Are we a good manager or a poor manager of our time and energy...? If only we could gain a bigger picture look at everything and let go of the coping and stress. The Management Wizard invites Real Intelligence and Divine Grace into all that we are dealing with on a daily basis and invites us to yield our separative stance, allow such Grace to be felt more truly over time. May it be so!
