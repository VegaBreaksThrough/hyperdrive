There is self, it is simply not the self we are thinking of, as in cogito ergo sum, “I think, therefore I am”. This is an internalised absurdity, because ‘thinking’ is a posteriori and cannot prove the existence of anything. ‘Thinking’ is a posteriori, because it is merely a subtle arising and is therefore an object, just like anything ‘else’. So ‘thinking’ is not something we ‘do’, it is merely something we ‘observe’. In [[Actuality]] the arising of ‘thought objects’ is not something that is particularly desirable, like warts are not desirable, ‘thinking’ is arising out of the ‘stress’ of the being. This ‘stress’ is simply the ordinary consciousness ‘living’ the presumed and false cosmology of mortality. The ordinary self is less like a ‘who’ and is in fact more like a ‘wart’ and ‘wartish activity’. “Are you a good ‘wart’ or a bad ‘wart’?” So the ‘earth’ has a bad ‘case’ of ‘warts’ and the earth would rather not have ‘warts’.
So if there is a self it is not anything we have in mind,
i.e. not something we are ‘thinking of’. Therefore we cannot read in these writings that there is no self-nature and say, “Hey, I’m not a self”, because that is the ordinary self talking and with the thought, “I’m not a self” sets about giving itself licence to do as ‘it’ pleases and ‘it’ pleases to ‘do’ what is taking place across this world. The absence of self-nature is not a ‘thought’, but a realisation, which is a conversion process from being self-centered, which is about self-survival within the cosmology of mortality, to self- [[sacrifice]] within the cosmology of Only God. This conversion therefore is a transformation within consciousness ‘itself’ and not merely how we might ‘think’ about something.
So when we are no longer presented as an ordinary self we have been divested of all fear arising out of our supposed ‘mortality’ and essentially this is all the fear there is, except the body has a ‘fear response’ needed for its ‘survival’ as a creature. If the fear of self-consciousness is absent, then this is the realised disposition of mindless embodiment. The disposition of mindless embodiment is the self of the entire pattern of relations, which has no supposed ‘internal part’ and since there is no ‘internal part’, we cannot know ourselves in this world, but only as we appear within this total pattern of relations, i.e. we know ourselves only as feedback from the world as a whole and not what we have ‘put together’ as our self-image, our self-identity, which is only how we engage ‘survival’. If we operate only as an ordinary self, we are a ‘psychopathic wart’, ‘psychopathic’ because we are ‘virtually’ the sole arbitrator of what is right and what is wrong and we sit in judgment upon these ‘matters’.
So, mindless embodiment is the only self there is and this
is what we might call the ‘soul’, but clearly this self is only ‘temporary’ and not immortal. So the soul is ‘everything’ we must be confessed of in our happiness, not something we desire to cling to and present this ‘wart’ at the Pearly Gates. There is an immortal form beyond what is presented within the [[Cosmic Display]] as gross, subtle and causal and this is the real ventriloquist who has finally confessed that he never was the dummy after all.
