We have witnessed over many thousands of years of human history many manifestations of the ‘dark side’ of human nature in the form of vast and shocking crimes against humanity. The ‘dark side’ that gives rise to this unbridled cruelty is an intense contraction below the navel, manifesting as fear, sorrow and anger.
Fear felt at this level reduces the individual to paralysis (if only temporarily) or mindless screaming. Sorrow felt at this level is a sinking to some immovable depth of darkness as an unremitting grip on the emotional being. Anger felt at this level can move the individual to do anything to anyone - there are no bounds.
The human species has at various times attempted to keep a plug on the ‘dark side’ by employing a number of cultural imperatives such as the Ten Commandments. When these imperatives are no longer felt to be so (such as at the present time), many individuals begin to experience the ‘dark’ motivations below the navel, often appeasing the tiny conscience with some flimsy religio-philosophical justification, also arising out of the madness of the ‘dark’. At last, most are teetering on the brink of the ‘dark side’ of fear, sorrow and anger. The [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] may, in grace, relieve the de-stabilising intrusion of the ‘dark side’ into the realm of our waking consciousness.
