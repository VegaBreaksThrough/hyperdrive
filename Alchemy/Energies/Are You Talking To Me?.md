Most of us in our so-called speech are merely voicing what pops into our heads. Ordinarily we don’t care from where the speech popped, it is simply sufficient that we “just got an idea”. We fail to realize that these ideas are controlled by our desiring, which is the equivalent of a little devil whispering in our ear. If we simply roll along with what simply occurs to us we are easily targeted for any and all possibilities for negative influence and because we do not doubt that many of our ideas are worthy things and should therefore suffer communication, these influences spread like a virus infection. So, because most, or all, of our communication simply occurs to us in our untransformed dilemma of existence and the verity is not considered, our conversations are disease carriers.

In truth what merely ‘pops into our head’ is seen to be nearly always, if not always, simply a karmic course that is suggested to us in order to influence us in a certain way and others in our sway. We tend to go with our ideas. If we continue to dwell on some of these ideas, they can swell to a booming level, until sometimes we enact something shocking and they find us muttering, “God told me to do it”. Because we are karmically bound, everything that simply occurs to us is binding us to some false action and perhaps others to false action, so through this kind of mechanism we are constantly binding each other to the ordinary destiny. It is true that in our ‘freedom’, alive as the Truth of us, all matters simply occur to us, but in our ‘freedom’ we are obviously ‘unbound’, at least in our own self-authenticating recognition, and our presence in all of its various forms can never be binding to others, in fact such a presence releases all others from all levels of bondage. Such real communication is never political-speak.
What we need to see, is that merely voicing what merely occurs to us is not real communication. Real communication comes from our heartfelt responses to ‘other beings’ and is always centred upon what these ‘other beings’ are really communicating and not just some political spin that is floating around in ‘bubbles’. Therefore, real communication may perhaps appear like a ‘meta language’ as it moves to respond to what underlies most conversations and this is fear, sorrow and anger. Real conversation, therefore is always a response to what is weighing upon the heart, which is the disease that underlies the ordinary stance. Conventional speakers are therefore never actually speaking to anyone or listening to anyone nor have anything to say that anyone needs to hear, unless it is because they demand such disease karmically. We must obviously cease ‘infecting’ each other with the diseases of our communication. We must cease to merely voice our ‘off the top of the head’ notions, by sacrificing such impulses to the heart. Mere notions can never arise from the Truth and are always the poisons of doubt. If we practice truly the sacrifice of our ‘mind objects’ to the heart, at some point perhaps a true response to the world may arise and in this, we can cease to bind ourselves and others to all our heartless garbled nonsense. Until such a wonder occurs keep all our opinions to ourselves and let them slide away back into the ‘[[muttering dark]]’ where they belong. If such a practice is ‘intensely endured’, at last even the ‘[[muttering dark]]’ vanishes, outshone in the light of understanding. So, are you talking to me? If you are, then divest yourself of the casualness of a political-speak punk, where the arrogance is perhaps only outweighed by the ignorance. Instead allow the heart to speak its always and only heart confessions. It is these heart confessions that are the healing force of the living divinity of Only God.
