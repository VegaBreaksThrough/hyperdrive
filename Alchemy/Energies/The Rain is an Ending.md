[[A0015|The Divine enters the world through water]]. As it falls from above, this very display is felt as a blessing. The blessing finds its way into every nook and cranny and imparts a refreshing liveliness to all living things. The rain lends a mysterious touch to the landscape and enfolds us as one across the domain of its blessing. We are touched and changed by the falling rain; our beginnings, middles and ends merge into a delightful transcendence of the solid world before and after the rain. 
In the rain, we are held for a moment beyond much of the obligation of our worldly involvements. ‘Lovers’ kiss and melt away in the rain spilling over them and many may look above for signs of the Divine as the rain falls. God Bless all who know such wonder.
