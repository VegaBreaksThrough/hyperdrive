Provides the discernment between truth and illusion.  He helps in the process of transformation or death, both physical and spiritual.  A support to let go of all that we identify with and be reborn. 

Keywords: truth, understanding, transformation, rebirth.

---

Azrael is the “Archangel of Death”. He is also the “Angel of retribution”. Azrael is a powerful Angel who teaches you about truth and illusion, life and death, to bring strength of [[Soul]] and help to transcend your fears. Azrael is also an extremely loving and gentle being, who offers assistance to [[Soul|souls]] through the death process, making their transition back into the spiritual world from the physical. (Words associated with this Archangel are truth, understanding, insight and strength). Because _Azrael’s_ job is to help transition souls through the death process, he is also called upon to help us let go and release all that we are clinging to – all the illusions and material fascinations we have in this earthly realm. Learning to align to _Azrael_ whilst we are alive brings us to a whole new level of understanding about life itself. It is only in truly surrendering whilst alive that we come to a greater intimacy with the Divine. We must learn to truly die whilst alive – meaning, we must learn to let go and trust, rather than hold on in fear. Archangel _Azrael_ is considered a ‘grief counsellor’, assisting and supporting people who have lost their loved ones, helping them with the grief, sorrow and pain that they are suffering. _Azrael_ is here to help individuals transform human life to spiritual life. _Azrael_ assists everyone to their highest good and always honours the free will of a person.
