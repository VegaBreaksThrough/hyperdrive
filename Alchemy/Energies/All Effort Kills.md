There can be no changes worked without karmic retribution at the level of life, except through conscious exercise. This [[Real Observation]] dissolves all our aberrations over time. All effort on our behalf only adds to our destiny, which is always death. This death is always hastened by the effort that kills. 
Beyond destiny is real life, which eternally transcends all worlds of association where all that we may come to love must die. In our transcendence of such worlds we see we are not dependent on that which must die, but our dependence is utterly and forever simply upon Only God, which is the Truth of us and there is no effort in the remembrance of the Truth of us.
