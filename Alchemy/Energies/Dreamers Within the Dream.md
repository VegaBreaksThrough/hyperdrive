The psyche is the engine that drives the _destiny_ There is no way to change that _destiny_ except through the _Grace_ of _God_ i.e. being moved to live from a different disposition than that of _egoic_ _survival_. As long as _survival_ is the principle of existence, only _ordinary_ _destiny_ designed in the fear of death is the case now and forever. The structure of the psyche makes the _ordinary_ _destiny_ necessary. Where it seems to be that someone has succeeded at some method related to change, it is only the karma of that individual, not the propensity of that method. The uncontrite egoic activity is an activity within the dream, but within the dream, which is quite clearly devoid of capacity for Real Change. In Truth there are no actors and doers but these apparent actors and doers are merely the dreamers within the dream and as such cannot know any 'freedom' or any 'release'.
