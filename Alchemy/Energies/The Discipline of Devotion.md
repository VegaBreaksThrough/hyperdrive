What is ours?

Only our devotion to Truth.

Is that enough?

God is sufficient as our devotion.

Every breath is the discipline of our devotion, to Truth, to God.

Can we catch an elusive glimpse to where this devotion flies?

What might it be that scoops up our discipline?

Perhaps the Great Starry God?

Can this possibly be so?

Oh! Let this be so!

Every breath as surrender touches the Great Starry God.

Could this be so?

The very notion threatens to destroy us.
