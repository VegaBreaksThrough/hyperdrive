The information superhighway is essentially about ‘nerd power’, the pimply-faced 90 pound weaklings of the ‘50s who received the sand in the face on the beach have finally taken over the world. We all thought the jocks were losers, but in our ‘wildest’ did we imagine that the 90 pound clowns would shrink us all down to the social level of ‘e-speak’. Did anyone notice that at some time in the last fifty years the entire world was nano-mised and is now floating in the circuitry of the nerd mind? Did anyone notice? Were we distracted by the sleight of hand? So now, quite suddenly there is e-mail, e-tail, e-commerce, e-sex, e-travel, e-fun and the sorry sight of an old ‘dinosaur’ trying to say, “Retail” with an interviewer, just as eager to impress us with “e-tail”. Soon we will all enter these little boxes at a certain age, say thirty five, quite willingly in our induced dazed state, where we will be turned into little protein cookies to feed the starving millions, under thirty five and we will simply be doing our ‘civic duty’. Is this a joke? Do you feel so?, or do you think so? When we simply allow the endless degradation of human existence, is there any bottom? Did anyone notice those 90 pound weaklings looked just a little like E-T?

What we see about the e-net is that it is exactly the Apple that was apparently the start of all this downfall. The Apple was about wanting to compete with God, by wanting what God seemed to have and that was perfect knowledge of all matters. So, “knowledge is power” we thought. If we know absolutely all things, we can control ‘everything’ and ‘oust’ God from His spot. Our present position is hardly the Valhalla of our dreams, now we are ‘gods of the garbage dump’. The e-net is luring us away from the possibility of our human existence, by suggesting, quite literally, that a virtual life on the net is superior to that other virtual life in actual space. Now, isn’t that exactly what an ‘alien nerd’ would think? The strange thing is, that so many, like the helpless Eloi, are siding with ‘nerd power’ and plugging themselves into the net. When we allow ourselves to be sucked into e-space, into the circuitry of the e-net, we are, perhaps unwittingly, opting for de-socialisation, or ‘e-socialisation’ in the form of the solitary e-shopper, with their little e-trolley, wandering the lonely e-aisles of the virtual e-market and as we sit alone somewhere pecking buttons on our console, staring at the monitor, awaiting our ‘reward’, our latest re-enforcement schedule, and when this is duplicated everywhere, we see that the ‘alien nerds’ have ‘got us’. We have been ‘got’ and now we are the equivalent of a ‘battery hen’ in a vast ‘hen house’ awaiting to be tabled on the next agenda, de-sexed, de-volved, dehumanised and de-void. 
So knowledge is not power in any ultimate sense and what power we have is like the power of an insider trader or the power of exclusive information which is simply the power to be sneaky. The kind of power being sought here is simply power over the majority, who are not in ‘the know’. This then is power over the ‘knowledge poor’. Yet who are those who seek such power? Aren’t these the ones who are unhappy, inadequate and weak, the “I’ll show them” kids of the oedipal reflex?
Truly, the only power that should exist is power for, not power over. Such power for is never found at the end of seeking. Seeking, at last, only reveals [[HyperDrive/For the curious mind/Emptiness]]. Power for spontaneously arises in the transcendence of the ‘mind of knowledge’, the ‘mind of authorities’, and in this we see that the information superhighway is simply a parody of the true form and real life, which is the Omniscience, the Omnipresence and the Omnipotence of Only God. The search for knowledge is doubt of love and in doubt of love we end up with virtually nothing, except perhaps, the geometric smile on the ‘happy face’ screensaver. Knowledge is the ‘poison of doubt’, we seem perhaps to gain some advantage in the survival stakes, but as we survey the bleakness of our e-world, we may see that we have sold our soul to some devil in an Italian suit called ‘Slick’. Hey! What if someone pulled the plug? Hey! Hey!
