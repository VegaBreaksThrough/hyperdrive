Break away from the image of Christ as a fascination. The Christ is the anointed one who comes to serve all beings; this is not a status, but a happy confession of perfect identification with the Divine. Christ is the Truth of us and is not a fascination, in and of himself. He is simply a manifestation of the Truth of us. He is no more fascinating than a leaf on a tree or a stone in a brook. He does not draw you to himself as himself; He draws you to the Truth of you and the Truth of all that is. Christ is ordinary, not the ordinariness of common things or conventional things, but the ordinariness in simple enjoyment and the creation of simple enjoyment. Therefore divest yourself of all images. God cannot be what you think; He would never be what you think; He is simply the ordinary Truth of you, as a leaf on a tree or a stone in a brook. These things are the very vision of God. 
The leaf on the tree and the stone in the brook are ordinary visions of God, and Christ is also the very ordinary vision of God. He may have functions that are extraordinary, but these functions are for the transformation or awakening of all beings, they are not to be seen as matters of fascination. Clearly, the Christ is the one who lives as all of this, and who appears here suffering this condition for the sake of the Divine work of purification and transformation of the all, and this is a unique function in the form in which it appears. So be ordinary in your happiness, presume strength in the ordinariness of your happiness and be bereft of all the false daemons of mind. Ordinariness is falling out of the egoic presumptions of mind into being alive as the total pattern of relations only, wherein there is no inner part that could be the source of fascination. There is only the confession of Only God and the witnessing of Only God in and as all apparent manifestation.
