_These little poems, resonance of the being Arise as petals of the mind, surrounded By the living Heart of the Beloved.
They swell to flowering from the felt
Unimaginable depth of the Secret Heart. Yet sometimes, as these little lines gather
Up the being, the Heart depth vanishes, and They fly to the height and depth of the world.
I Am by Grace, the fruitful tree of this flowering Gathered to Remembrance of the Heart of Love.
