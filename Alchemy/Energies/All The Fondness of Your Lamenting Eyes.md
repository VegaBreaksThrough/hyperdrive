_I looked for you always
In the fondness of your lamenting eyes
But now you have found time fallen to your hands In trickles of your passing
You have cupped in the night
Of your sleepy eyes your lidded fancy.
What passes now for your joy
In all the before and all the after of those glances?
The suspecting half turns Towards the hazy fitful traces
Of the lover gone.
The slow reaching suspended
In the mists of your unknowing fondness.
O! If your happiness could be said With a mere dribbling word of release
Yet no simple saying can turn your gaze From the dimensions of your own fondness.
There has been the sweet call to you From the singers down simple heights And though they looked for you
In the intricacies of your far away eyes
You were lost in that all too usual reverie Of beginnings and ends
And here you call this passing moment Your joys, your sorrows, your humanness.
O! How the sweet prophet
Has longed for the joy of your seeing But now this very One
Who was True and was Eternity before you Says to your lamenting eyes “I know you not”.
He knows you not.
You in your far away reaching glances Have wearied even His great patience. In sullen fancy you gazed distracted
By your own dream of foolishness
You clung fiercely to your seeming sweet choosing And therein you died.
Now what is there left for the saying?
What can you offer now That One?
Who in every moment of His time was there to end All the sorrow of your lamenting eyes.
_
