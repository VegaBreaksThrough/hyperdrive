The ‘sleeping’ referred to here is living in unconsciousness and suffering and delivering suffering as a matter of course from sunrise to sunrise. Such a supposed way to live is heartless and most often utterly mediocre. Even if we develop the high flying life, no matter how high we might fly, our wings melt at last and we crash to earth below a little sign that says: “Here lies Fred Bloggs. He sure flew high.” Therefore, all manner of living in the dream of unconsciousness is heartless, destructive and cruel upon all who are collusive members of such a dream-time. The hermetic touch invites the restoration of the heart of consciousness where we awake beyond the dream-time into the open eyes of the confession of Only God. This is a passionately heartfelt awakening of the sleeping being. 

The drink *[[All The While You Were Sleeping]]* is about restoring the _Heart of Consciousness_ where we awake beyond the _dreamtime_ into the _Open Byes_ of the 'confession' of _Only God._ This is a passionately _Heartfelt_ awakening of the sleeping being.

**

All the while you were sleeping Is all that is left, of a lost poem Of a lost friend.

Alas!

The poet’s trembling heart has died And Now I

Poor stumbling poet that I am

Struggle to resurrect my dead friend’s Sweet and most sacred confessions

Whispered, touching still

In this lover’s long discarded trysting place.

These echoing passions Chill and bless my Heart

With their simple innocence

I know in his simplest most open Heart He is saying still

As always

That the Heart’s Truth was always given by many Shining in a shattering stillness

Brighter than a myriad luminous heavens 

All the while you were sleeping.

**
