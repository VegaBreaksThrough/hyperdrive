The connections we have in the world are like strings that bind. Many binding qualities actually appear as pretty strings, desirable things – appealing. It is obviously important to outshine all levels of being ‘bound’, revealing the true nature of the things we submit to in fear. By energetically cutting the controlling strings, we are able to stand free, particularly of ‘external’ control. Pinocchio Transformation Puja is given to help with such a process of awakening.
