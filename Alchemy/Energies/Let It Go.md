> [!ABSTRACT] Ingredients
> Cypress, Lemongrass, Peppermint

How much of our lives do we waste ruminating on what we can't change? How many events or situations keep us contracted and condition our response to life? Our skin is marked by shared wounds, which drown us in grief and justify this illusion of suffering that absorbs us. 

The intention of this Alqvinergia is to remind us that only we can harm ourselves. It invites us to let go of that contraction which maintains the illusion of suffering, allowing love to transform everything: the emotions, the storyline, and the characters. An alchemic surgery that gradually removes the scars from our skin, helping us build a better version of ourselves. An intervention that supports us on our path of transformation, as we move from the chemistry of stress to the chemistry of enlightenment. 

> [!tip] How to use this Alqvinergia? 
Before or after a bath, activate your heart by placing a drop of *Let It Go* on your chest or the palm of your hand to immerse yourself in the aroma with deep breaths. As you inhale, feel the current of love and release the tension with each exhalation. A few drops can be added in your diffuser to enjoy in your room, during your meditation or yoga practice. Remember to breathe and let go.*
