Everyday ‘[[Reality]]’, or the dream state, is termed actuality for the purpose of this book. Actuality is the everyday ‘[[Reality]]’ that most people take for granted, but in Truth is not real. Actuality is like a dream state, which we only call ‘real’, rather than recognizing it as a temporary psycho-physical effusion or dream. (See Glossary: [[Reality]])
