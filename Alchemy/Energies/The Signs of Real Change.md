

We all change spontaneously through maturation and aging processes and these are largely beyond any level of control; they are simply set as biological clocks as part of the karmic destiny of an individual. There are other kinds of changes, wherein we apparently engage in some deliberate process, such as bodybuilding, learning a musical instrument, gaining knowledge and so on. These latter series of changes are founded on accumulating something, that is we gain skills, we gain knowledge, we gain body mass and strength, but what remains unchanged essentially is the one who is doing all of this; is the one who would become adept at a musical instrument; is the one who would be strong; is the one who would be erudite and so on. These kinds of changes already mentioned are the most ordinary levels of change that everyone knows. The fundamental aspect that ordinary changes do not engage is this matter of destiny. Ordinarily we are simply animating a set karmic program from birth to death and, no matter how great we become in the scheme of things, we are always reduced to zero in the last few minutes of our destiny in the earth plane. Therefore, from the point of view of transcending karmic destiny, no matter what the usual person does; all ordinary changes are not real because all ordinary activities do not allow any real freedom from this earthly vision. Therefore the ordinary individual, and we see this often, may be an artist of very great skill, but still demonstrates the reactive dimensions of childhood or adolescence. Such demonstrations or dramatisations of reactive dimensions of existence are sub-human.
The human being appears when there is stable responsibility for these reactive dimensions of existence. The usual person is unconscious as to the form of their [[Subtle communication]] and in fact most of us are not particularly aware that there is such a thing as [[Subtle communication]]. However, most of us give some recognition to [[Subtle communication]] in the case of ordinary speech when we talk about such things as ‘good’ and ‘bad’ vibrations, and such statements like, “...you could have cut the air with a knife...”
The human therefore is partly being responsible for all this unconscious communication and partly the level of true enthusiasm we bring to life. This level of change in an individual is what we call *Real* spiritual change or development, wherein the unconscious sub-human levels of communication are no longer made the form of our relationship to each other, but are transcended in *Real Responsibility* for what we amount to as a mass of unconscious tendencies. Therefore the signs of real change in an individual are to be seen as stable responsibility for communicating *Only Happiness* or *Only the Mystery of existence* or only the *Humour of existence* or *Only Understanding* or *Only God* at last and forever.
The energy demonstrated by someone living as *Reality* is *Real Enthusiasm* and not merely the aggravated excitement and hype of the usual person. This *Real Enthusiasm* is *Reality*, is *Love*, is *Only God*. When we see the steady demonstration of abiding as the mystery of existence in an individual, we are seeing the signs of real change. If we see someone phasing, that is sometimes appearing to be responsible, but mostly collapsing into dramatisation of negative emotions, we are witnessing someone who has not truly changed at all, but at best has been drawn unconsciously by their association with some form of Divine process. It must be utterly understood that responsibility for ourselves as an ‘apparent karmic entity’ must be stably and perpetually demonstrated without fail before we can regard ourselves or be regarded by others as having truly or really changed from the usual unconscious destiny to conscious responsibility that awakens our destiny as Only God.
