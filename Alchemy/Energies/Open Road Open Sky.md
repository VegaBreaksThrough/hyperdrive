There is a block between the functions below the brow (the road) and the functions above the brow (the sky). The functions below the brow are associated with the ‘lower coil’ or the ‘extended world’, which includes therefore the human female. The functions above the brow are associated with the ‘upper coil’ or ‘unextended realm’ of the human male, the mental and the higher mental. True reception of the hermetic touch can help establish free conductivity of the life-force between the ‘upper and lower coil’, releasing the pressure that tends to build at the root of the nose due to the failure to conduct the life-force.
