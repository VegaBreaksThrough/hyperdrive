Analysis is a bold and false ploy to unearth what makes everything tick. In the final analysis what is not a part of some structure does not exist. Analysis is an absurdity based upon the presumption that there is a final analysis where the glittering gold of success flashes in our analytic pan, “Eureka!” One of the difficulties with this is there is literally no end to smallness and therefore there is no end to analysis and this is true of every gross physical structure. The analytic investigations into the elemental atom have revealed this lack of finality. Why is it imagined that what the essence of anything is, is to be found somewhere in the smallness of its structure? There is absolutely nothing to suggest such a platform. Is the essence of push bike to be found in any one of its nuts and bolts? Thus, we have all these false positions such as the ‘biopsy’, the ‘active ingredient’, the ‘selfish gene’ of human behaviour, and so on. All these absurdities are paraded before us even perhaps as momentous discoveries, but like the King’s new clothes, they are naked of essence. The ‘biopsy’ is not the disease (even the essence of disease itself is not captured by the medical diagnosis). The ‘active ingredient’ does not perform as the whole plant does and many side effects are introduced through the ‘active ingredient’, and the ‘selfish gene’ is hardly worthy of consideration, since it is a nonsense of anthropomorphism. 
So should we be looking, not in the analysis of things for their essence, but rather to the ‘wholeness’ of the structures of this place? Is the gestalt of anything revealing of its essence? Clearly, when we break something up into its pieces, something has been lost. The gestalt is what has been lost, i.e. how any structure actually performed is lost. A dead body in the morgue cannot reveal anything essential about the living person, unless like Holmes we are the ‘god of inference’, yet even in this case, inference is not analysis. The gestalt notion is clearly greater than analysis, since especially with living things analysis is the equivalent of road kill. 
What even the Gestaltists are missing from their picture, along with the analyst, is the multidimensional essence of all that is. The world is not just physical structures, it is not just subtle structures, it is not just causal structures, but the essence of all that is, transcends the structural presentation. The world is an all at onceness, arising out of the God light and this all at onceness suffers no game of ‘blind man’s bluff’. The Truth of us is Only God. The Truth of all that is, is not to be found in the ‘nuts and bolts’ of this place. The analytical bluffers are not just wrong, they are also misleading. All of their “Eurekas!” are false alarms of ‘fools’ stumbling about in the dark. Therefore, analysis is not ever an appropriate relationship to anything that is, in the all that is, at last the only true form of revelation is to stand as the Truth of us, wherein we see that all that is, is simply our own radiance, the absolute brightness of Only God.
