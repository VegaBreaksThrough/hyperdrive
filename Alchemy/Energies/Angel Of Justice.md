I am the Heart of Understanding, without me you are lost in the search for [[A0309|balance]] that will never reflect the peace required to sit on the throne that has been designated for you. You must understand that in you is found the Strength and Integrity to execute the activity of God, the Destiny to which you agreed when you decided to embark on the eternal path of Oneness. There is only Justice, all that you see as unjust is your ignorance.

The Angel Of Justice is a portal of no return. On your knees you surrender your activity to what is meant to be and what is destined to Be. It is a throne accessed only by one who has the discipline to surrender completely to Providence, to execute what is Necessary and to embody the Destiny of being a creative agent for Art and Learning. "This is the Government of the Universe , which depends upon the nature of the one who is unique and extends through the one [[Nous|mind]]."

Your Divine Presence demands that you recognize this One Truth in which rests the capacity to hold the scepter of harmony and the sword of justice to execute the designs of a soul that comes from Light and Life, from Good, Truth and Generosity.
