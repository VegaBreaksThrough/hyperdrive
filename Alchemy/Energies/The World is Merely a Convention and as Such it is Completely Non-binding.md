All must be wary about merely worldly obligations. There are real obligations and these are the obligations that serve real life or the awakening to the heart of Truth, but all merely worldly obligations do not serve the Truth and are therefore trivial. We find that when we move beyond conventional existence the world literally fights back, attempting to draw us back into our former unconscious existence. The ploy here is to suggest to us or remind us that some trivial obligation is to be taken as mortally serious. Yet even the death of a relative or close friend is not significant from the point of view of Truth. We might as well fret about a mosquito we just squashed. From the point of view of Truth, the death of a single human being is simply not significant and so we must in the very least not allow such events to draw us away from the Truth. Simply speaking, all this worldly human life is trivial; i.e. not a worthy pursuit.
It is not heartless to offend the conventions of ordinary existence. What is heartless is allowing the conventions of life to draw us away from the Truth. If we allow ourselves to be drawn away from the Truth, this could place us in jeopardy of losing our connection to Truth forever.
