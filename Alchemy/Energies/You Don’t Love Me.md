“You don’t love me” is the cry of an individual who is always looking to see if they are loved or not. In living in such a disposition, the world constantly proves the case by throwing up all the signs of apparent ‘un-love’ that appear like rabbits in a shooting gallery. The living circumstance can hardly ever appease such an oedipal response to life and so the given individual sinks ever lower into their anger and despair. These people, and there are many, need to awaken to what it is to love and not be concerned at all whether or not they are loved.
