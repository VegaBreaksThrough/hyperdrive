All that your [[Nous|mind]] perceives is my work, it is the radiance and emanation of the goodness of my lord, of the Light and space that give rise to your existence, you are immersed in the stream of Life that transforms all things that come to Be and you are the bearer of the flame that brings and influences spirit to your creations. You are here because it must be so, because you are the Child of Light. It is your duty to be Reverent to the one who allowed your existence in the blink of a timeless moment. 

The Angel Of Life is the Wisdom that must be walked to realize the Divine design, Providence, Necessity and Destiny are an instrument that articulates to perfection to bring light to the darkest confines of material chaos, life envelops and pushes from the Heart of every being that lives in this universe, it is a relentless force that will always come to be, for it is eternally the fullness of the existence of all things. You can only listen to its call and embody its function to give generously of the Light of your Eternal Soul to all that surrounds you.
