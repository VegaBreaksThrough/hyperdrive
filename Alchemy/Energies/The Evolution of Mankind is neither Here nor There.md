Every day we have an opportunity to make right our relationship to all matters. At the break of dawn we have a vast expanse lying before us: To live this day ‘rightly’ by drawing all beings to their highest possibility. This is, in part, the super evolution of mankind - first the advent of human being, and then onward and upward, to Divine being. Initially this amounts to sacrificing the dramatisation of all our reactions so that Only God stands out. The human being is sacrificed into the Divine being via the breath, yielding up all residual negativity. At last there is “No seeking in the heart, and no dilemma in the mind.” 1
