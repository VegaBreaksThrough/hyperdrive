**“Numeras numerans”** from _The Temple of Man_ by Schwaller de Lubicz is a deeply esoteric concept. Here’s a concise unpacking:

---

### **🔹** 

### **Numeras numerans**

###  **“The counting that counts”**

- **“Numeras”** = “you count”
    
- **“Numerans”** = “counting” as an act or principle
    

  

But _numeras numerans_ isn’t just about arithmetic—it’s about the **conscious act of differentiation** in the human mind mirroring **cosmic ordering**.

---

### **In The Temple of Man**:

  

Schwaller uses this phrase to describe:

- The **innate human faculty** to number, distinguish, and measure
    
- The **divine principle** of number in action through the human
    
- **Man as a conscious intermediary** between spirit and matter
    

  

Man doesn’t just count things (_numerata_), he performs **the act of counting itself**, participating in the **active Logos**, the universal ordering principle.

---

### **✨ Philosophical Implication:**

- The **act of understanding** (through number, proportion, rhythm) is sacred
    
- The **temple (body)** is the **instrument** through which the cosmic Logos is expressed
    
- True knowledge is not passive; it is **syntonic participation** in cosmic harmony
