Arranging 330
Goodbye Electro-Magnetics.

There is a background hum to our houses of 50 cycle electro-magnetics and the communications from the so- called user-friendly computer are worse than our toaster. We are subject to varying degrees of *electromagnetic* disturbance no matter where we go these days. *Goodbye Electro-Magnetics* does not remove the disturbing intrusion, but moves to lift our attention or higher dimension of existence. May it be transformed, into something like a picnic, with buttercups, by a stream, after a rainstorm.
