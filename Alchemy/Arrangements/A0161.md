The Medieties are the crux of the crossing, the point of inversion. The mediety has a dynamic representational quality that takes various forms; arithmetic, harmonic and geometric. "Man considered as a natural finality is not in opposition to the Universe, he is the 'mediety' of the inverse elements that are universal and particular. In him, opposites are resolved and cancelled; he is the all in the All." (Pg. 28; The Temple of Man by R.A. Schwaller de Lubicz.
