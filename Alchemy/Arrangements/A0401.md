Leviathan: rayon/sabre nettoyant

Leviathan is a powerful energetic tool designed to be used on oneself, others and our environment to clear psychic intrusion. In particular, the Leviathan is designed to help us cut free from the subtle levels of control and overwhelm of which we are unaware and by which we seem to become manipulated.

The Leviathan is an old biblical word that has been used to describe a daemonic octopus-like creature, which rises up from the depths of the ocean and is a symbol of a hellish condition. Coincidentally, it has also been used for many years to describe the rise of capitalism, and government and corporate greed.

As we know, the developing countries have had their labour and resources exploited by the west for many years. Every day, those of us in the more affluent parts of the world are faced with the outcomes of this exploitation and greed, and we are seeing, in the media, on the internet and retail stores, the twisted, manipulating force that is trying to mould us into a particular shape. This force is devoid of feeling, care or integrity, wanting only to control and exploit and now, people all over the world are rising up against it. It is to this end that the Leviathan Energy product has arrived – to help free us from the subtle levels of control into which we have all been unconsciously lured.
