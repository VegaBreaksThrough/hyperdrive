Robert

Robert Detzler (1935–1998) was an American author and spiritual teacher known for founding the "Spiritual Response Therapy" (SRT) and developing the "Spiritual Response Therapy" system. His work combined elements of spirituality, psychology, and healing, focusing on clearing negative spiritual energies and patterns from an individual's life. Detzler's approach aimed to assist people in resolving spiritual and emotional issues by accessing and transforming their subconscious beliefs and energies. He authored several books on his methods, which remain influential in the fields of spiritual healing and personal development.
