Arranging 214
Clairsentience is 'knowing beyond the present conventional configuration.' As an Arranging, this is an enlightening current, even the brain may feel lighter, and there is simply a knowing - and you know that you know.

The Energy also holds the qualities of claircognisance. This Energy is for feeling and knowing beyond the present conventional configuration. It could be feeling the ‘vibe' of a person, a place, the future even -the intuition and 'gut' feelings that one senses. it appears in the mind as a gnosis - you 'know that you know'. Divinely inspired revelations - may God's Will be revealed.
