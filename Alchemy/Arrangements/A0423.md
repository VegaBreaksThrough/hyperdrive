It is an energy of deep spiritual cleansing. It shows us the spirit in its essence, it is goodness and service, it shows us the spiritual nature of being, spirituality from the heart at the service of God, makes us recognize God in us. We describe Three Functions of the Fire from the Heart of Neptune: 

  

1. The Fire from the Heart of Neptune eliminates bonds and covenants in past lives and the present existence, as well as karmas linked to vices, and addictions of any kind. Discover and expel very old emotions, linked to demons, rooted in the soul from many reincarnations, product of bonds, oaths or spiritual contracts that enslave and avoid spiritual evolution. 

2. Disarms and destroys magic, negative spiritual influences, death attacks, enchantments, eliminates the contamination of places with demons or wirchos, also prevents the emission of magic from our being to other volumetric beings or places. Create a sphere that acts as a shield to prevent the entry of spiritual and psychic attacks. 

3. The Fire from the Heart of Neptune gives us the blessing of connecting with Spirit.
