There is a particular insanity associated with the possibility of being reduced to a pile of mush such as road kill. The individual suffering this insanity has severely occluded their intuition of the Divine, so that they begin to see themselves as bits and pieces of fleshy functions. Restoring an individual to a full intuition of the Divine, wherein, we see, even if unconsciously that we are not merely this flesh form.
