The list of glands in the **encephalon (brain)** is complete as it pertains to structures traditionally classified as **endocrine glands** within the brain. However, certain regions of the brain have endocrine-like roles or interactions, which might sometimes be included in an expanded list:

**Core Endocrine Glands:**

1. **[[Spit|Pituitary Gland]] ([[Spit|Hypophysis]])** – Master gland with anterior and posterior sections.

2. **[[Spin|Pineal Gland]]** – Secretes melatonin.

3. **Hypothalamus** – Regulates the [[Spit|pituitary]] through releasing/inhibiting hormones.

**Additional Structures with Endocrine Functions or Influence:**

4. **Epithalamus** (including the pineal gland):

• Besides melatonin, it is indirectly linked to hormonal rhythms.

5. **Choroid Plexus**:

• While not a traditional gland, it helps in the secretion of signaling molecules that influence neuroendocrine processes.

6. **Adrenal Medulla (Sympathetic Nervous System Interaction)**:

• Though technically part of the peripheral endocrine system, the brain, through hypothalamic connections, exerts direct control over the adrenal medulla for rapid hormone release (e.g., adrenaline).
