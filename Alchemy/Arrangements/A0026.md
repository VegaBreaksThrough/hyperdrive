With all the religious and perhaps spiritual presence across this world and all the obeisance that is supposedly done in recognition of God and given how long this has been going on, why is there so little evidence of the presence of the Divine in this world? The religious are apparently concerned with bringing God into the life, whereas the spiritual are interested in the God beyond this life. Of course the spiritual considered here is largely pseudo, since it teaches an absence from the living process, whereas what may be termed real spiritual life is a radical shift in the lifeforce relative to the body. The aforementioned are largely exoteric and the latter is esoteric. So, as we can see there is much activity across this world supposedly about God, but showing no sign of such a presence. What is actually happening then? What we are witnessing here is the search for God, not communion with the presence. Yet God can never be absent from this world, it is just that God is not organised in any cognisant way to help us, except through the Living Adept. So, although God is obvious, the religious and spiritual are worshipping dead Adepts and even mothers and friends of dead Adepts. This approach might have been effective, mothers and friends aside, except for the fact that there was not anyone extant, after the death of these Adepts, who could allow the presence of the Adept or the heart of love to continue. This is of course assuming that the central aspect of all these movements was always a true Adept. Hence, what has remained, largely across every level of religious and spiritual pursuit, is simply an empty heartless ritual of appeasement of [[A0288|the unholy]] conventional life of emotional turmoil. We come to churches to try to dump our feelings of guilt and the like.
So, God has very little way into this world. There is no open invitation. With this as the prevailing mood for hundreds of years, no significant purification or transformation has taken place here and with each passing year we have slipped deeper into the darkness; the occlusion of the heart of love. The primary focus for most people, in this world of darkness, is literal survival, or in the more affluent areas, holding on to what we have and perhaps wanting more. In this, we the more affluent, have generated many spurious obligations which do not serve real life, but instead are the signs of doubt of love. Clearly, if we are to be an invitation to the heart of love, we must quickly, but quickly, divest ourselves of all pursuits that do not serve real life and allow the transformation of the remaining so-called essential obligations .
The transformation of all our worldly obligations is the radical shift in the life-force relative to the body that is required for real spiritual life. If we baulk at this, i.e. throwing our lot in with God, our hesitation is simply doubt and this doubt is not opening to the life of grace and such doubt takes along with it only whatever the destiny of the worldly is to be. 
We must cease to believe that real life can be accommodated, along with the spurious worldly obligations. The transition of making way for the flow of Divine energy into our life may appear an insurmountable difficulty and yet we must yield up all such fear and trembling if we are to be counted among the living. We cannot be looking back to what has been or we may turn to a pillar of salt, instead we must look toward the light of [[Truth]] that awakens the life of grace, wherein our complete trust is in the living divinity of Only God. Do not procrastinate! All of this appeal is for the most sacred sake of all who can hear. Listen! The bells are ringing and soon the trumpet will sound.
