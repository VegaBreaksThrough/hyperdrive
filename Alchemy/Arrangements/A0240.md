In the unconscious playground there is often a vast tangle of dangerous objects that can often harm our children or even lead to their death, as in the case of the unfenced pool or the uncovered skimmer box of the same pool. We leave these 'things' around as part of our unconscious neglect and then we bewail the consequences of this neglect when children are maimed or killed. Asbestos, is part of the 'unconscious playground' of the adult world. Initially perhaps we did not know of its harmful effects of those who came into contact with this substance, but later we did and still did nothing about it. Unconscious neglect now becomes 'criminal' neglect. The world now has become filled with such fatal consequences as asbestos across all the fields of our living sphere. The intrusion of asbestos is a fundamental intrusion into the very breath of life, it is part of all the corruption that strikes at the very Heart of our breathing life. This Energy moves to awaken and restore the consequences of this vast neglect in wrongful relationship to things and bring us to the Recognition of the true form of Surrender into the Divine with every breath.
