Feed life, encourage hope to grow. Three functions that describe this energy:  
  

1. It is the consciousness itself, the consciousness of natural processes, the consciousness of being, the consciousness of vital energy, by calling it active awareness of the processes of life on the Planet, it is the Spirit current, which flows to Mother Earth, floods our being of vital energy, acts directly in the revitalization of the heart. Each human being possesses a Fire from the Heart of the Sun.  
      
    
2. Cleans and removes the darkness and evil of souls, of all volumetric beings, cleanses natural and human environments.
