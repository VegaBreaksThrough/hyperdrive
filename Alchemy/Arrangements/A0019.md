# Take Heed Submission to Grace is the Only Way

If [[Truth]] be told
Our lives are dominated
By childishness
Or adolescent posturing

The child needs ...
The child struggles ...
The child fears ...
All in the mood of dependency
Like a little baby bird
Always looking for Mamma

The adolescent reacts
The adolescent rejects
The adolescent sulks
All in the mood of stroppy independence
Like a mad dog chained to a stake
Always looking to bite and go his own way

But God is neither our Mommy or Daddy
The dramas are in our heads
And out of alignment
With what is

God is Only.
God is the Eternal [[Truth]] of us.
Submission to Grace is merely
Heartfelt recognition of the [[Truth]]

Recognise and be free
Cling or avoid and
The endless round continues

---

Apart from [[Truth]], we are always struggling with our childish need for dependence and our adolescent need for independence. The child fears being abandoned, the adolescent fears being controlled or parented. The adolescent feels that the most important need to fulfil is the one of freewheeling it through life, the proverbial rolling stone. The adolescent does not bring any ‘intelligence’ to this precious independence to see if this position has ever amounted to happiness, and see, therefore, that this could never be so. The childish individual feels sorrowful and overwhelmed. The adolescent individual feels angry and trapped by others and the circumstances that exist around others. The adolescent always looks to escape, usually justifying this action by blaming others or situations for how he or she feels, latching upon any or all complaints to justify separation or, if this fails, calls upon self-doubt and ‘confessed’ weakness.

All of this struggle occurs because life is being lived only from the point of view of the mind, which is emotional disassociation or removal from the [[Reality]] of existence. From the mind’s point of view only the given individual matters, there can be no appreciation of serving the needs of others. The preferential existence is the only course for the angry adolescent. The dependent existence is the only course for the ‘sorrowful negative child’.

Beyond all of this terrible fruitless struggle is the point of view of grace, where we surrender our childish and adolescent tendencies and dramatisations into the infinite Divine. This gesture takes a great deal of ‘intelligence’ and therefore ‘discipline’ since it requires us to penetrate the illusion of ordinary existence and be ‘disciplined’ by happiness in the service of others. In grace we utterly accept the perfection of what is presently arising, since we understand that the Divine is creating our life for the sake of our undoing and the undoing of others until, at last, we are able to stand free in the perfect confession of only happiness, Only God. There is no way to grow beyond the terrible circumstance of ordinary existence if we deny grace or retreat from the circumstance of grace in fear and/or anger. If we deny grace we may be doomed to wander realms such as this, forever in fear, sorrow and anger. Submission to grace is the only way to the realisation of the [[Truth]] of us as Only God. God is not some great parent to be sought in fearful neurotic dependence by the childish or avoided in fear of control by the angry adolescent forever apart from all. God is Only. God is the eternal [[Truth]] of us. Submission to grace is therefore not submission to some imagined overbearing, overwhelming parent, it is the heartfelt recognition of the [[Truth]] of us, our true heart’s desire.

---

[[Grace]] suggests that spiritual striving is not real. Spiritual advancement is granted effortlessly and freely by the Divine as grace.
