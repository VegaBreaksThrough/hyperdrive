Arranging 229
This is a picture of utter desolation of the human soul, the 'Ghost in the Weed Garden'. This Energy moves to Awaken the Heart of Remembrance.

The _Ghost In The Weed Garden_ is a picture by a psychiatrically diagnosed 'schizophrenic'. It is a picture of utter desolation of the human soul, it is a paralysing emotion at its most intense, onsetting 'catatonic schizophrenia'. This dreadful emotion is felt to some degree across all human souls. It is a waste land of hopelessness and despair that is a crippling "sickness unto death". This _drink, Ghost_ _In The Weed Garden_ reaches deep down, but deep down into the human soul, _Healing_ this anchor in desolation.
