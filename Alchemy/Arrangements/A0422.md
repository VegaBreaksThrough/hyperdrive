1. ### ♅ Fire from the Heart of Uranus
    

The Fire from the Heart of Uranus is alchemy, it is an energy that cleanses great processes of civilizational change, ignites spiritual transitions, which affect national or world political changes. Let's look at Three Functions of this energy: 

  

1. Heal Karma linked to oppressive processes of tyranny and slavery. It promotes personal and social processes for spiritual, personal and social liberation. 

  

2. The Fire from the Heart of Uranus connects us with the higher mind, with the hermetic arts, with wisdom. It reveals high thoughts for the spiritual evolution of humanity. Innovation, originality and invention are inspirations of this energy. Correct any mental and emotional anomaly that prevents the spiritual connection with this energy. Evolve communication to unimaginable levels. 

  

3. It shows us La Libertad as it is, without political, ideological prejudices, or emotional and mental pollution. It is a Spiritual Puja/Force for the radical change towards Freedom.
