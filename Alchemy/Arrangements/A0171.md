Osirian Seed - Seminal - crude but capable of growth - seed from which something can be given (Osirian Seed gives rise to Christianity). Revealed a formula - in Readiness, actualising process, what is Revealed, embryo of Cosmic man, animal to human or Cosmic or Divine. Preparation to be put together - it must arise - original impulse - to be Returned to Spirit. Returns from whence it came - Horus taught to the Elite of the Palace, Osiris taught to the masses.
