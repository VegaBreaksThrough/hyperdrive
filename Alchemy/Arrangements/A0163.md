Arranging 163
The Great Book of Nature. Vision: A felt moment of literally walking through leaves in a book - pages that were transparent and translucent opening as though the pages had been closed or 'bound'. Initially around the head - opening one side at a time, then the other side and so on - an unfolding was experienced, layers releasing and opening from the contracted stress of mortal identification and mortal knowing. After the head, the energy moved to the throat centre, then the heart-centre, (continuing to be felt as a releasing of 'leaves' of energies) through the release of bondage from each centre of the energy form and continued its way down through the 'lower coil'. The Alchymie of this current of energy is the reflection of the 'Book' that has been bound - there are seven bindings - depicting also the seven seals and hence the chakras of the body-mind. The breaking of the 'bindings' - the release of the gross-vital form, beyond identification with limitation, into knowing again the release of whole body Enlightenment - no longer identifying with limitation, but allowing the initiation of the Eternal form.
