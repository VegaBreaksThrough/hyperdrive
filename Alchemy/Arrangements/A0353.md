Arranging 353
Iron Heart. Where the wound of the heart centre is replaced with the felt connection to the intuitive Heart, which allows us to feel always and forever held. We are not separate and alone. Nourishment and restoration for the true Heart centre.

---

Iron Heart moves to open the Heart as a 'feeling centre', inviting the remote arrogant head to fall into this heart-consciousness. When this feeling centre is open, it is felt to be a vulnerable position and we may quickly close it again to protect ourselves. What are we moving to protect? Some image of self that doesn't exist. There can be no protection where we have become love. It is a risk... We take the risk and dare to love and we are eternally wounded in that loving. [[The Wound of Love.pdf|The wound of love]] churns in the side forever. 

Iron Heart, my Iron Heart. I am the Love you find in thee - come embrace Me there.
