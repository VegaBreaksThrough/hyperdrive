Arranging 204
The sense of vision receives an excessive amount of attention in the 'usual' person, to the detriment of all the other senses. The vision sense is therefore over-loaded and seriously dulled,  and these unpurified levels of the brain can most often disturb the sleep. _You_ _Can_ _See_ _Forever_ is the clear day.
