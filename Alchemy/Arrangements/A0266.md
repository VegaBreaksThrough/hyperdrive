Arranging 266
A sea creature, the Hydra, has been found to be 'immortal' because of its endless cell division - it is endlessly recreating itself. Germ cells are spread across its body, whereas in the human they are only found in the sperm and the ova. What can we understand from the endless regeneration of the Hydra? Here is an invitation to allow attention to move into the Eternal rather than the mortal form.
