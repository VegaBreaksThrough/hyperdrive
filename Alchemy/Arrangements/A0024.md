The End of Death "The Crucifixion is not a quaint story of vicarious salvation: a tuck-me-in bedtime story. The Crucifixion, whatever may be gleaned from it, is the story of a barbaric murder, plotted and carried out by stupid, cruel and vastly ignorant people who effectively murdered their own Intuitive Heart of Love, condemning all involved to the loveless wilderness of their own vast betrayal until the demand for [[A0055|redemption]] is met. Until then it is simply true that the sins of the father will be visited on the son, this blood will not simply wash away. The usual consciousness of mankind, these murderers of the Intuitive Heart, is epitomised by the image of the gravestone over the grave or the face of political characters over the corruption or unconsciousness in the ground. The crucifixion creates a shattering of the usual corrupt consciousness with the image of the one who suffers his or her existence, as the cross - which indicates the vertical line of gross and subtle adventuring together with the horizontal line of the Awakening of the Eternal Heart of Real Consciousness. The Crucified One submitted to His existence shines forever in the Heart of mankind as the hope beyond the consciousness of death of the usual man or woman as the True Form of Eternal Life or the One who was never rendered within the usual circumstance of this earthly vision." Jessa O' My Heart; 'Easter: The End of Death' from, "The Will of God is the Obligation to Love".
