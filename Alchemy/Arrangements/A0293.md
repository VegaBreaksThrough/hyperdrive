The age for the awakening of 'Will and Intention' is usually at around fifteen years; the passage to adulthood. With this advent, the young individual is suddenly and for the first time confronted with the demands of their particular 'culture' and whatever that offers. The reactions and responses that emerge through this stage have a wide range: rebellion, frustration, confusion.... This Energy moves to touch the individual, relieving the terrible stress, by opening them to a greater and true way to Real Life, as the morally awakened 'human being'. In the vision of this Arranging, the being was seen coming out of the bent over position, unconscious, to the waking upright position.
