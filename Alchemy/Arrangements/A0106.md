Arranging 106
Vital Natural Cause Reaches the End of its Activity by Excess of its Effect. Brings itself to an end (e.g. production of wine fermentation produces alcohol. Stops at a certain point when alcohol is at certain levels, still more sugar left - creates wine, brings an end to activity.) Effect of action becomes its own resistance.
