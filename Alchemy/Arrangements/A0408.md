Arranging A0408
Sweet Point: Resurrect the whole body over time as the Phoenix Bird, brings about [[A0309|balance]] and a reawakening of the body at a cellular level, into the Divine Light.

This _pendant_ has a wonderful _energy_ designed to resurrect the whole body over time as the Phoenix Bird which, once dead, arises alive and renewed from the ashes. It brings about [[A0309|balance]] and a reawakening of the body at a cellular level, into the _Divine Light._ The creative centres of the body also need to be constantly restored to [[A0309|balance]].

The overall inspiration of this _pendant_ is to allow an individual to live a long and disease-free existence, in the hope this may turn them to the True significance of human existence, which is to transcend self.

Refer to the poem [[Nebulae]] at the rear of this book. The energy of this poem is rejuvenating at a cellular level and is part of the design of Sweet Point.
