Arranging A0402
Weapon Shop: Brings strength and equanimity required in order to serve in the holy war in which Infinity  is engaged.

The image of _The Weapon Shop_ comes from two novels by A.E. Van Vogt, and while this image is only useful to a point, it is about a way of selecting and serving those who show low or non-existent levels of aggressive vengeance. Likewise, this drink, _The Weapon Shop_, is designed to serve those who serve Infinity, drawing them to the strength and equanimity required in order to serve in the holy war in which Infinity is engaged.

This drink lifts these _Servants of Truth_ beyond all existential dilemmas, into the truly inspirational realm of the _Mystery of Only God_. In this holy war, we are most often faced with those who do not rightly acknowledge us and who most often appear as ‘friends’ who then go on to betray us. This drink helps us to go on with this holy war beyond the heart sickness that is most often delivered to us and would perhaps otherwise engulf us except for the _Remembrance of the Divine_, to which _The Weapon Shop_ restores us, holding us fast in the _Mystery_.
