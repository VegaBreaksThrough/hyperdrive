Arranging 308

The vicissitudes of the living process would scarcely challenge the imponderables of the so-called 'Chronic Fatigue Syndrome'. The attempt to pull this Will O' The Whisp out of the blue into the solidity of a downtown bus must surely be worthy of those elusive 'Gold Medals'. The name of this disturbance should be changed to 'Globulus Nebulous'. All of this considered, it is about fatigue to the degree that an individual can scarcely function at the level of life. Since this realm is manifest desire, with this disease we are seeing a withdrawal from life, the reasons for which may be legion.

The withdrawal from life involves very shallow breathing which even at ordinary levels would minimise energy availability. The true form of relationship to the living process is surrender into the 'infinite ' via the breath and this is the essence of the 'conductivity' of the 'life force'. It is exactly this relationship that is missing in the case of the 'Globulus Nebulous Syndrome'.

The retreat into more and more sleep sees hope in the release into death. Such a death is no release but like the day after day becoming life after life is endless repetition allowing no release.

Underneath The Arches is a wake-up call for all those who would rather sleep in. There was a man called Fred, who liked to stay in bed, by and by the y found out why, they found out he was dead. Come forth Fred!
