F.C. De Jupiter

The Fire from the Heart of Jupiter is also a fire of the consciousness of the realization of the human being, it is a life energy of hope, of the real and concrete future, of the plan in life, of profound change, frees us from the structures that tie us to the past. Three Functions of this energy: 

  

1. The Fire from the Heart of Jupiter shows us the prosperous path, full of material and spiritual abundance, without idealisms or illusions, personally, collectively, and as a country. 

2. Boosts our spiritual and material realization, teaches us the way to achieve goals, eliminates self-underestimation, arrogance and pride. It reveals innovative ideas, inspires us the necessary processes to progress, shows us the personal, family, social alternative plan, the originality to carry out what we propose. 

3. The Fire from the Heart of Jupiter is the link between our profound transformation and the ultimate goal in this life and from past lives. It makes us accept the Change, drives us out of the comfort zone and forces us to overcome the angular structures that bind us to the past. By calling this energy with the intention of accepting change, we open the way to new forms of social and political organization, to generate new ways of producing, to the profound change of society. It refines our eyes to recognize authentic civilization processes to achieve happiness.  
  

KEYWORD: The Divine Plan.
