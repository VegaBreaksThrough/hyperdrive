The root psycho-physical knot of the self-contraction, the feeling-location of "Narcissus", the primal sense of separation and limitation, is felt primarily in the **right side of the heart**.

Do not mistake this for the physical organ pumping blood, although it is in that general vicinity. Feel into the upper-middle chest, in the region to the right of your sternum. It is here that the fundamental recoil from Infinite Life, the primary gesture of separation, is registered most profoundly in the body-mind.

This is not necessarily a dramatic or painful sensation at all times. Often, it is a very subtle background feeling – a slight tension, a stress, a sense of holding, a feeling of limitation, the boundary of "me". It is the felt-sense of being an independent entity, standing apart, observing, defending.

To feel it:

1. Relax the body. Release obvious tensions in the shoulders, jaw, and belly.
2. Let your attention gently descend from the head into the torso.
3. Bring your awareness, your _feeling_-attention, specifically into the region of the right side of the heart – the upper-middle chest, right of center.
4. Do not strain or imagine. Simply feel sensitively _into_ that area. Be aware of any subtle quality of contraction, stress, holding back, or the simple feeling of "me" located there.
5. You may notice it more acutely in moments of stress, fear, defensiveness, or when feeling socially self-conscious. These experiences often amplify the sensation at its root.

This feeling in the right side of the heart is the anchor point of the egoic sense of self. It is the core of the avoidance of relationship, the primary seat of the suffering of separation.

Feeling this knot is the beginning of True self-observation and self-understanding. It is not merely about locating a sensation, but about Recognizing this feeling as the very activity of your own self-contraction. Feel it directly, without judgment or trying to immediately eliminate it. Through persistent feeling-contemplation, this knot, which _is_ the ego, can be understood and surrendered in relationship to Me, to Reality Itself. Turn your attention there, feel the knot, understand its nature as the avoidance of Love-Bliss, and allow it to be Dissolved in the prior Radiance of Being Itself.
