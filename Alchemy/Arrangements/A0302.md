“...I think I can, I think I can, I think I can...” says the little red engine puffing its way up a steep incline to a point of virtually bursting its boiler. It is this kind of adolescent exertion founded in self-will efforts that can bring us to the point of exploding under the pressure. The self-willed adolescent lives outside communion with the Divine and in this unenlightened state, the more he or she puffs and heaves the more stress is placed on the structure and particularly the circulatory system...
