Gender Bender

Whatever biological presentation we might be, most of us present ourselves as a disturbance of that sexual disposition and this disturbance amounts to all the horrors of the proverbial Pandora's Box. Presenting ourselves as a problem sexually is demonstrating unhappiness with some or all aspects of our 'given' gender. Most sexual preference apart from the heterosexual disposition is grounded in sex as a gross emotional-physical problem, and rarely because we are identified with the opposite sex higher up in the subtle dimension. Even within the ranks of the so called heterosexual preference, many aspects of our stance in life demonstrate our dis-ease relative to the demand to function as a sexual being. In fear of women, men may retreat to the extreme 'macho' condition and likewise in fear of men, women can retreat into the ultra feminine or the violently aggressive 'butch' position. Hence, most of us are gender-benders in that we do not enjoy 'free creative relationship' to our own bodily form which embodies both the male and the female structures, if we are to develop as 'spiritual beings' we must achieve a total acceptance of our sexually opposite presentation, since in our 'immortal form' there is no longer the duality of the male and female, but only the undifferentiated wholeness that is [[Reality]]. Gender-benders need to straighten up and fly right. So drink this [[Alice]].
