According to R.A. Schwaller de Lubicz, the inner ear, specifically through its spiral structure of the cochlea, performs a _function of translation_ that is both physiological and metaphysical. This process transcends mere auditory mechanics to involve the transformation of vibrational energy into a comprehensible form, serving as a bridge between the material and the spiritual realms.

The spiral shape of the cochlea symbolizes the universal process of transformation, reflecting the principle of dynamic evolution and resonance. Schwaller de Lubicz connects this to ancient Egyptian symbolism, where the spiral is an emblem of life’s unfolding and consciousness’s progression. Through its function, the inner ear translates external vibrations (sounds) into electrical signals that can be interpreted by the brain, paralleling the transmutation of sensory input into higher levels of awareness and understanding.
