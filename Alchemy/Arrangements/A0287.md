Arranging 287
This is a special arrangement of carbon atoms. Carbon 60 rolls into a ball and is named after Buckminster Fuller who created the Geodesic Dome, Bucky Ball C60. The felt current of the geometric structure is enlivening. Vision: Felt the current descending into the gross-vital from above, gradually moving down the body like refabrication of the entire form.
