**(No Worm Holes in [[Alice]]-land**
**might have been an alternative title.)**

When [[Alice]] chases after the white rabbit down the hole into ‘eat me, beat me’ land, this could be considered the beginning of an allegorical tale about irresponsibly retreating from life into the vagaries of some fascinating and bizarre dream state. The dream state or subtle dimensions are no solution to any felt dilemma at the level of life, growing taller or smaller or disappearing except for a foolish grin does not essentially alter any troublesome condition. The habit of transforming emotional-mental states into physical states is an attempt to escape the demand to function 'truly' in some life situation, as though this would solve the 'irksome'. Such hysterical moves not only disrupt the gross vehicle, but opens us for alien and other manipulation through the very dimensions we seek to retreat into, and in this way we become use-able to be harmed ourselves and in turn we harm others. In wider terms our hysterical retreats into the creative centres of the body are an avoidance of incarnating truly in this yellow place thereby avoiding functioning truly across the entire functional display of the gross vehicle. Where we are not responsible in some functional area, no light can enter therein and in this way we make a reeking dark of our form where all the evils of the world are spawned. At last we disappear from this place as a scream in outer space after having suffered a degrading demise or some ‘accidental’ rendering.
The presence of the _drink,_ _After_ *The* _White Rabbit_ unravels all possible 'worm holes', thus preventing 'dimensional invasions' or 'dimensional retreats', rendering them void and the [[The Hermetic Touch is About Remembrance|hermetic touch]] is a frustration of the retreat into some other dream state and thereby invites the individual to incarnate as the whole body, even where, as in most cases, this has not ever been allowed. This incarnating process in this yellow place demands that we deal with matters arising here, which includes all levels of reaction, including reflexive reactions, by turning to love as the whole body, rather than the conventional response of dramatisation or suppression. When this can be lived moment to moment across the entire arising functional display, this creative response, beyond the conventional, is the sign of the human possibility. Be therefore blessed in this discipline of happiness until even this yellow place passes out of sight. Scooby Doo!
