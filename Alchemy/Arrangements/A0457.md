### ASTRONOMY 

The Moon is [Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth)'s only [natural satellite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_satellite). [It orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_of_the_Moon) at [an average distance of](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_distance) 384,400 km, about 30 times Earth's diameter. The Moon always presents [the same side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near_side_of_the_Moon) to Earth, because [gravitational pull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_force) has [locked its rotation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_locking) to the planet. This results in the [lunar day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_day) of 29.5 Earth days matching the [lunar month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_month#Synodic_month). The Moon's gravitational pull – and to a lesser extent the [Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun)'s – are the main drivers of the [tides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide).

### TRADITIONAL ALCHEMY

In traditional alchemy, the Moon is associated with several symbolic meanings and correspondences : 

1. Metal : the moon is linked with [[Silver]], representing purity, clarity and the reflective nature of the lunar surface. 
    

  

2. Element : Water, symbolizing emotions, intuition, and the subconscious;
    

  

3. Philosophical principle : The moon embodies the alchemical concept of the feminine principle, often referred to as the “anima” of the “soul”. This contrasts with the [[A0437|Sun]], representing the masculine principle.
    

  

4. Phases : The Moon’s phases are significant in alchemical processes, symbolizing cycles of transformation, growth, decay and rebirth.

The “great work” is a process of achieving the philosopher’s Stone, which symbolizes the ultimate goal of alchemical practice. Involves the 7 stages : 

- Calcination
    
- Dissolution
    
- Sublimation
    
- Putrefaction

The Philosopher’s Stone  is a legendary substance believed to have the ability to transform base metals into gold and grant immortality. It represents enlightened self, the culmination of spiritual and material transformation. It is a metaphor for the inner journey of personal transformation and the quest for the divine wisdom.

ASTROLOGY

The [Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon) (![](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXdXH-SoFEhZPkH84V8XOW3U1EMB4oUszuHSTFGyBoYe1px7SrjNRAQJEXyCoGNZL5bdEigURSRYTNWK11ZpG4Zh48BgQgt27n7mv6eg6WwUNfN4Q1WvKbQFpXI92kNFcgvcR9WK4cn9kEL1I7NLt7q2NGSd?key=KQLLnGKO9sj8ETdBIZmx3A)) is the ruling planet of [Cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer_(astrology)) and is exalted in [Taurus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taurus_(astrology)). In classical Roman mythology, the Moon was [Luna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luna_(mythology)), at times identified with [Diana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana_(mythology)). The Moon is large enough for its gravity to affect the Earth, stabilizing its orbit and producing the regular ebb and flow of the tides. The [lunar day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_day) syncs up with its orbit around Earth in such a manner that the same side of the Moon always faces the Earth and the other side, known as the "[far side of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_side_of_the_Moon)" faces toward space.

In astrology, the Moon is associated with a person's intuition, emotional make-up, [unconscious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unconscious_mind) habits, rhythms, [[Memory|memories]], moods, femininity and their ability to react and adapt to those around them. It is associated with the mother, maternal instincts or the urge to nurture, the home, the need for security and the past, especially early experiences and childhood. 

In medicine, the Moon is associated with the digestive system, stomach, breasts, the ovaries and menstruation (which occurs on a monthly cycle)[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planets_in_astrology#cite_note-24) and the pancreas. In modern astrology, the Moon is the primary native ruler of the fourth house (House, family), but traditionally it had its joy in the third house.

The Moon or Luna is associated with Monday,  

- In [Chinese astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_astrology), the Moon represents [Yin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feng_Shui#Yin_and_Yang), the passive and receptive feminine life principle. 
    
- In [Indian astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_astrology), the Moon is called [Chandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandra) or [Soma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soma_(deity)) and represents the mind, queenship and mother.
