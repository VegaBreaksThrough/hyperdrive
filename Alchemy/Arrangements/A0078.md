The “North and South Meditation” is a practice attributed to Thoth, the ancient Egyptian deity of wisdom, and is detailed in the Emerald Tablets, particularly in Tablet XIIIm (The Key Of Life And Death. This meditation involves specific visualization techniques aimed at achieving spiritual balance and enlightenment.

Key Steps of the Meditation:
	1.	Centering Consciousness: Focus your awareness on the “life seat,” often interpreted as the heart or solar plexus area.
	2.	Energy Division: Visualize your consciousness splitting into two streams.
	3.	Directional Projection: Direct one stream toward the north and the other toward the south.
	4.	Energy Circulation: Envision these energy streams circulating back to the center, creating a harmonious flow.

This process is believed to balance the body’s energies, aligning them with Earth’s magnetic field, and facilitating deeper spiritual insights.

For a comprehensive understanding, you might explore the Emerald Tablets of Thoth, which delve into various esoteric teachings and meditative practices.
