Arranging A0434

When we observe the [[Fire from the Heart of the Solar System|solar system]], we see that it’s in need of tuning. Earth is at an angle relative to the plane of the ecliptic, the [[A0457|moon]] is not in an orbit within the plane of the ecliptic and the earth’s orbit around the sun is eccentric. No doubt there are many more odd wobbles throughout the [[Fire from the Heart of the Solar System|solar system]], yet the primary wobbles are those of the earth and the [[A0457|moon]], indicating that the earth plane is tuned toward a situation of chaos or shadow world. The entire [[Fire from the Heart of the Solar System|solar system]] is the extended form of the structure of the brain-mind and accounts for a greater part of the human form. Hence if it is wobbly, then we are wobbly; out of sorts, out of sync, out of luck. By straightening out this situation we recognise how out of focus we have been and a new relationship can be discovered by tuning out the static.
