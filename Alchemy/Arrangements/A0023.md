Arranging 23
House-Five-Sky Genesis of Creation 

"An impossible something called House-Five-Sky. How sweet it is, a totally unfathomable configuration. How the Heart sings in its presence. It is a mystery. It is out of the deepest of Mysteries. There is no verbal expression of it that would be complete. It is not ultimately known by us completely. There is no concrete expression of it that could even plumb a level of this Mysterious depth. Any statement can not circumscribe the essence of House-Five-Sky, suffice to say, it is the essence of this realm with its four directions and vaulted sky. It is the 'symbol' of the entire cosmos, a cogent reminder of our own raison d'etre. We can only feel House-Five-Sky in the oneness of our essence, so to speak, a logic of understanding, the volumetric Heart of Synthesis and allow this to be the Transformative vision that touches our Open Heart and engenders a Transformation of Qualitative Evolution and ultimate Transcendence as the path of Infinite Return. (The ultimate destiny of all the cosmic fractions as gestations on their Mysterious unity from whence it came.) way to this The House shows the way to its own dissolution in its beginning. And oh the sea, the sea is left behind in the rack and ruin of early experimentations as we leap forward, ever onward, ever upward and ever beyond; or should we say, no leap, no forward, no onward, no upward, but only beyond, where no movement is possible in the timelessness of the ultimate unity, as appearance gives way to [[Reality]]. The ultimate expression of our comprehension is the wordless presence of House-Five-Sky of which we can say no more. We set House-Five-Sky, my dearest Heart adrift in the streams of changes, where the struggle for understanding is constant and incomplete. May you be blessed my Heart by finding this Great Wonder which is your own understanding and at last simply bathe in its Mystery and not to swaddle it in a mass of conceptions in search of some comprehension. House-Five-Sky is at odds with all you conceive." 

-Jessa O' My Heart
