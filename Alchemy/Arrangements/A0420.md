Trivia: The Cat's Eye Nebula is a convenient and accurate marker for the axis of rotation of the Earth's [ecliptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecliptic "Ecliptic"), around which the [celestial North Pole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestial_pole "Celestial pole") rotates. It is also a good marker for the nearby ["invariable" axis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invariable_plane "Invariable plane") of the [Solar System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System "Solar System"), which is the center of the circles which _every_ planet's north pole, and the north pole of every planet's orbit, make in the sky. Since motion in the sky of the ecliptic pole is very slow compared to the motion of the Earth's north pole, its position as an ecliptic pole station marker is essentially permanent on the timescale of human history, as opposed to the [pole star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pole_star "Pole star"), which changes every few thousand years.
