Worrying about anything is not helpful. The disposition of worrying is a meditation on a negative condition, tending therefore to bring about the negative condition. Trying to get positive is also meditation on the negative and does little to ameliorate the money worry. What is this money that we are worried about? Clearly it's not money or it wouldn't be a worry. It's the absence of money that is the worry. Money itself is a symbol of life exchange, i.e. exchange of 'life energy'. If there is not sufficient to go around it is largely because we are not 'conducting' the lifeforce and so we represent a poverty of 'energy' that is reflected in our 'broken piggy bank'.

Of course there is a huge number of apparent 'slobs' who are wealthy beyond our wildest dreams. How this has come about is a mysterious consequence of karmic tendencies set in motion in past ages. Instead of looking upon these rich folks with obvious envy, it should be considered that wealth is most often a hindrance to real spiritual development, since being wealthy enables us to hide from many 'testing' circumstances. 'Winners are grinners no doubt, but are the 'winners' ultimately happy because of their wealth? It is true we do need sufficient funds in our Western society to maintain ourselves beyond the 'poverty line'. This level of abundance could easily be granted by simply exploring the responsibility for conductivity of the life force wherein the karmas of poverty could be forever dissolved.

The Money Worries vial/Money Tree Wand dissolves our negative disposition wherein we are simply complaining about our dreadful situation and begin to help us 'count our blessings' and in so doing heals the disposition that has created our negative circumstance.
