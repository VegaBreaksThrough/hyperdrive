In _The Temple of Man_, Schwaller de Lubicz presents **Amun** as the symbol of **universal potentiality**—the unmanifest source of all manifestations. “Amun and the Universal Ramification” refers to how **Amun**, whose name means “the Hidden One,” embodies the **latent, undifferentiated essence** of creation.

  

### **Universal Ramification:**

  

This idea describes how **all forms, forces, and beings “ramify” or branch out** from a single, divine origin—Amun. Think of it like a **cosmic tree**: Amun is the invisible root, and all things in the universe are the branches and leaves growing from that one source. In physics or metaphysical terms, it’s analogous to a **singularity** or **source field** from which multiplicity unfolds.

  

In the temple architecture (especially Luxor), this is mirrored through:

- **Proportions** that reflect a human archetype, unfolding from a point of origin.
    
- A **progression of chambers** symbolizing stages of differentiation—from divine to human, hidden to revealed.
    

  

De Lubicz ties this into both Egyptian theology and a kind of **sacred science** where **Amun represents pure potential** before it takes shape in time and space.
