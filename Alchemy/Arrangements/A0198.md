"When we consider the total surface of the earth and its many and varied climates and surface conditions such as hot desert sands where the living soil has long ago blown away, the wild demonstration of erupting volcanoes, the splitting of the earth as earthquakes, the frozen dead waste lands and the vast collection of human parasites and their scaly dwellings and so on. Across this varied earth surface we are perhaps looking at a mirror reflection of the 'micro world' of the internal and external surface of the human 'form', especially how it might appear after years of self-indulgent living. Therefore as the human form deteriorates more rapidly in this World, so does the 'external' surface of the earth and the deep hidden disturbances within its interior. This Energy moves to transform this ravaged internal and external disposition awakening the individual to greater care and feeling."
