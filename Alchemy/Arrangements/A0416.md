Arranging A0416
Nuclear Tracker: Can be sent into your own form to dissolve negative structures in the body, or externally to dissolve negative connections; frees people from being locked into negative conditions and dispositions; destroy that which gives rise to the impulse in them to be negative.

Tracking areas of locked-in negativity; transmuting and dissolving the impulse and thus the source of shadow. When applied to a territory, a golden light is seen to flow out across the land, sucking up the ‘dark’. Through continual purification the land is cleansed of its grosser elements, leaving a brighter world in its wake.

The inspiration for this Vial is to energetically track areas of negativity, or shadow, or that which is a contraction away from the light, and dissolving such negativity at its source. Try using it to track lost or misplaced or forgotten items, [[Memory|memories]], people. This Vial can be sent into your own form with the prayer to outshine negative structures in the body, or focused so-called externally to transcend negative connections (relationships, land when used with Fractal Earth Healer).

The heart-felt prayer with the contemplation of Nuclear Tracker is that we may be free from being locked into negative conditions and dispositions; and the impulse to be negative.

When this energy is applied to a territory, psychically it is seen that a gold form flows out across the land "sucking up the dark' that is present everywhere. The gold is then drawn back up again, leaving the land cleansed of its grosser elements. On repeated applications the gold energy penetrates deeper into the negative social and territorial structures, leaving a brighter world in its wake.
