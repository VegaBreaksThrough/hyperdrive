Arranging 119
The Revelation of Hermopolis. Lower Coil, opening navel, legs. Tunnel of Light below.

**Hermopolis (Thoth)**: At Hermopolis, the celestial message begins to interact with the terrestrial realm. Thoth, as the mediator of this process, oversees the birth of principles represented by symbolic creatures like frogs and snakes. These creatures, referred to as "[[The Ogdoad]]," embody fundamental cosmic principles and the ongoing process of spiritual manifestation.
