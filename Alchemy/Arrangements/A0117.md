Arranging 117
any inertia. The Revelation of Heliopolis. Vision of a place where a shaft of light was given and beings one by one would come and kneel before it and receive an initiational current - a dissolution of the duality of mind, mind as separation would dissolve, opening the mind current - Third eye.

**Heliopolis (Atum)**: The creation process begins with Atum, symbolizing the emergence of the divine from the unmanifest (Nun) into the manifest. Atum represents the initial act of creation, the beginning of differentiation from the cosmic potential, leading to the creation of other divine principles like Shu and Tefnut.
