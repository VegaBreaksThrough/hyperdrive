Arranging 309
The balance and equanimity of all form. Everything – left, right, up, down, in, out.

Millions of interactions are going on within the earth plane. Millions of ways to imbalance. All of these interactions can be imbalanced through wrong living and wrong action. So balance is about restoring balance at the level of life to those aspects toe to crown.
