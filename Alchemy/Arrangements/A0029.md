Arranging 29
Through the 'Mystery of Reversal', the passive negative becomes the positive active. The polarity shifts to allow the Awakening to Consciousness. This new polarity occurs at death, when the negative polarity is annihilated. The cycle and struggle for domination of each polarity keeps the [[Cosmic Display]] going, giving rise to all phenomena. The 'Mystery of Reversal' reveals that Eternal life is prior to the [[Cosmic Display]] and reveals the space beyond and shows the way through to Eternal Life, releasing the drama of ordinary life.
