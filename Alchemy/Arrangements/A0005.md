Arranging 5
Based on the 'Missing Attributes', which is an energy that you may call a 'safeguard'. I didn't imagine that I had discovered all the essential aspects of existence, like [[A0168|Soulfire]] and [[A0011|171]], 172, and I wanted to be able to include what I had not yet discovered. There are any number of missing aspects that we may not have discovered that are fundamental aspects, like [[A0168|Soulfire]], actual intelligence '[[A0011|171]]' and cosmic intelligence '172'. (That which comprehends the limited structures of the cosmos is an attribute, because actual intelligence is 'mad' - shows no comprehension, but gradually learns over time if you turn it that way.) Missing Attributes account for the qualities that we still need to find and make into actual energies but that we still require to work for us now, before we discover them, because we only discover an attribute by inspiration and over time e.g. looking at Sulphur - saw [[A0168|Soulfire]].
