The Hieroglyph or natural symbol does not stand for something, it is that something. It's not like writing and ordinary communication where we create imaginary meanings. The alphabet doesn't have any evocative power, the Hieroglyph does. The alphabet is imaginary so we have to 'learn' it to give it meaning and therefore there is no force in its communication. The Hieroglyph is naturally apparent; consider the symbol of a bird taking off in flight - enormously evocative. Contemplate the Hieroglyph in your mind, e.g. a bird, and everything this symbol evokes to your mind. When you've exhausted the contemplation of all that it brings to mind, this is how you discover all that a Hieroglyph amounts to.
