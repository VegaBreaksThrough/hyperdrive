John Thomas' Cube

The original _John Thomas' Cube_ was a very inventive way not to have to go to school, achieved by placing a black cube suspended in the air of the driveway which prevented exit from the garage, hence no way to be driven to school. This cube was not of the earthly plane, but was a manifestation of the little boy in the story. This *John Thomas' Cube* is also not of the earthly plane, it is the creation, not seen before, of a new human form which gradually evolves from a primitive skeletal structure to the whole fully fleshed individual capable of entering the _Dawn_ _of the_ _New_ _Age_ of mankind.
