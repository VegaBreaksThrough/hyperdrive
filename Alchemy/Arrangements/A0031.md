R.A. Schwaller de Lubicz explores the concept of _Function-Power_ (sometimes translated as _Force-Function_) in relation to the _Neteru_ (the gods or principles in ancient Egyptian thought). His perspective is that the Neteru are not anthropomorphic gods but rather fundamental, living principles that structure reality.

  

**Function-Power and the Neteru**

  

Schwaller de Lubicz describes the _Neter_ as a cosmic principle that embodies both function and power. This means that each _Neter_ is not just a symbol or deity but an active force that governs a particular aspect of existence—whether in nature, human consciousness, or the metaphysical order.

• **Function** refers to the role or purpose that a _Neter_ plays within the cosmic order.

• **Power** is the force or energy through which that function is actualized in the material and immaterial realms.

  

For example, the _Neter_ Ma’at is not simply a goddess of [[truth]] but the actual function of balance and order in the universe, and the power through which harmony is maintained. Similarly, _Ra_ is not just the sun god but the dynamic principle of illumination and cyclic renewal.

  

**The Neter as Archetypes of Reality**

  

Schwaller de Lubicz viewed the _Neteru_ as archetypal forces that manifest through nature, human behavior, and spiritual development. They are not separate beings but expressions of the universal intelligence at work in creation. Each _Neter_ is a _Function-Power_ that organizes reality at different scales—from cosmic cycles to personal transformation.
