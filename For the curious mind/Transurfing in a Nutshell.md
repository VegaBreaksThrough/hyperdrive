Transurfing of Reality presents 12 golden rules to help shape your reality and achieve your desires.1 These rules focus on controlling your thoughts, intentions, and energy to navigate an infinite "Space of Variations" where every possible reality exists.2

# 12 rules of Transurfing:

---

### 1. The Space of Variations

Your thoughts create mental energy that tunes you to a specific "line of life" in the Space of Variations. To change your reality, you must change your thoughts. You can't change a scenario, but you can choose a different one by shifting your mental frequency.3

---

### 2. Pendulums

**Pendulums** are energy-informational structures (like ideologies, organizations, or beliefs) formed by people thinking in the same direction. They feed on your mental energy, whether positive or negative. To escape a pendulum's influence, you must acknowledge its existence, then become indifferent and ignore it.4 You can't fight a pendulum; you can only deprive it of your energy.5

---

### 3. The Wave of Luck

Positive and negative energy create your own reality. **Accepting and transmitting negative energy creates hell**, while **transmitting positive energy creates heaven**.6 To attract luck, nurture small positive changes and successes.

---

### 4. Excessive Potentials

**Excessive potentials** are created when you give too much value or importance to an object or event.7 These potentials are balanced by **equilibrium forces** that act in the opposite direction, often causing problems. To regain balance and freedom, you must **reduce importance**.

---

### 5. Induced Transition

Emotional responses to negative information pull you into a negative "vortex" or line of life.8 To prevent this, **intentionally ignore and deprive negative information of your attention and engagement**. Stay indifferent and "empty" to avoid being drawn into negative scenarios.

---

### 6. Rustle of Morning Stars

Your soul has access to a complete information field and knows what's best for you. This is felt by the mind as a vague sensation of spiritual comfort or discomfort, also known as the **"rustle of morning stars"** or **intuition**. When making a decision, if you have to persuade yourself, your soul is saying "no."

---

### 7. The Current of Variations

Instead of fighting or demanding, you should move with the path of least resistance. **Do everything in the easiest and simplest way possible**. Trust that the most energy-efficient variations will be realized. When things feel difficult or complicated, you're likely going against the current.

---

### 8. Intent

Your desires are realized not just by wanting, but by your **intention**—the firm determination to own and to act.9 Transurfing differentiates between two types of intent:

- **Internal Intention:** The determination to act on your own.
    
- **External Intention:** A focus on how circumstances align themselves to make your goal a reality.10 This is achieved when your soul and mind are in harmony.
    

---

### 9. Slides

A **slide** is a distorted mental picture of reality. To change your reality, you must **create a positive slide** that is pleasant to your soul and mind. Live in this virtual reality, adding new details, and embracing the idea that you are worthy of it.

---

### 10. Visualization

Rather than visualizing the final outcome, **visualize the process of achieving each step toward your goal**. Focus your attention on the current stage and enjoy it, which extends your comfort zone and makes the goal feel more attainable.

---

### 11. The Soul Fraile

The **Soul Fraile** is the unique path of your complete self-realization. To find it, you must stop trying to be like others and instead **convince yourself that your soul is worthy of love**. When your mind is tuned to your soul, you feel inner harmony, which is the source of your unique charisma.

---
