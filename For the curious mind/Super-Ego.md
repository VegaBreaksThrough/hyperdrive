The **superego**, in Freudian psychoanalytic theory, is ==the part of the personality that represents our internalized moral standards and ideals, acting as our conscience and striving for perfection==; it develops from parental and societal influences and functions to control the id's impulses, often leading to feelings of guilt or pride through its two components: the **conscience** (the "should nots") and the **ego ideal** (the "shoulds").
