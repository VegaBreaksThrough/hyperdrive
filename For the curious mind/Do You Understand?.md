TLDR;

- Recognize the underlying suffering or sensation that motivates seeking.
- Observe the root motivation for seeking, which is the suffering itself; this is the beginning of "understanding".
- Through self-observation, see your suffering as your own activity, a present-time self-creation.
- Become aware of the fundamental self-contraction, the root activity of avoidance and separation, which creates the illusion of separateness and the loss of conscious awareness of relationship.
- Consistently observe, understand, and transcend this deliberate, present-time activity of self-contraction ("radical self-understanding").
- Experience a crisis in conscious awareness where the ordinary state is utterly turned about and unconsciousness is undermined.
- Transcending the ego-pattern and all artifice or defense.
- When there is absolutely no defense left, and the self-contraction has utterly ceased, realize the Very State of [[Reality]] Itself, which is Liberation, Fundamental Happiness, and unqualified relatedness (Non-separateness). This is the realization of the True Divine Heart, Consciousness Itself.

This process is described as the Divine Way of Adidam, an ego-Transcending Relationship, not an ego-Centric Technique. It is also stated that the Most Perfect [[Realisation|Realization]] ultimately happens by [[Grace]].
